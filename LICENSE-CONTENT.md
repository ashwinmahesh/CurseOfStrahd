## Generated art and text: what the license can and cannot promise

Most of the art was made with Google Gemini and Veo, and some dialogue lines were drafted with a language model and edited by the
author. Under current United States practice, output that a person did not author is not protected by copyright, so there may be
nothing for this license to license in a given picture; where there is, it is CC BY 4.0. Google's Gemini API terms say Google
does not claim ownership of the output, that it may generate similar content for others, and that you may not use the service to
develop competing models; they say nothing against sharing output under an open license. ElevenLabs' terms leave the owner of a
paid plan the rights in the output and allow commercial use on paid plans; that is why the voices are held back anyway. This
paragraph is the author's understanding, not legal advice.
