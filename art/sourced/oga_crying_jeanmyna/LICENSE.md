weeping_loop.wav is that clip, edited for the game: converted to 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit, the silent last 0.1 s cut, a 0.12 s fade in and
0.35 s fade out, the peak set to 60% of full scale, and 6.5 s of silence added so it repeats as a weeping (5.4 s) and a pause
(about 12 s a round). The Amber Temple's hall of echoes plays it from Patrina Velikovna (art/audio.json "npc_beds").
