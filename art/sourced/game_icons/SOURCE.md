# game-icons.net

Downloaded 2026-10-06 from https://game-icons.net/archives/svg/zip/ffffff/transparent/game-icons.net.svg.zip
(every icon, white on transparent; SHA-1 of the zip d70209efb48634bf4f1289d08a55f1c443788ac8) and unpacked here
untouched. Licence: icons/license.txt (CC BY 3.0, or CC0 where it says so), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.

The game uses the silhouettes art/icons.json names: `make icons` frames them as art/icons/<spells|items>/<key>.png,
and art/credits.json credits each artist used. `.gdignore` keeps Godot from importing the 4,180 SVGs.
