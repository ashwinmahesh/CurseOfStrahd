Concept paint-over of a screenshot from a gothic horror party RPG set in Barovia (Curse of Strahd), showing the game's target look. Keep the reference image's camera angle, framing and layout: every building, wall, prop, path and character stays where it is, at the same size. The characters stay exactly as drawn: 2D hand-drawn cartoon sprites with clean ink outlines and flat cel colours, crisp and readable, standing in the scene and tinted by its light. The screenshot is only a rough layout mock-up: its flat drawn textures, repeating tiles and ink lines are placeholders, so don't keep or trace them. Everything but the characters is re-rendered from scratch as a modern, richly lit 3D diorama in the spirit of Square Enix's HD-2D games. Stylised and hand-crafted rather than photoreal, with real modelled shapes instead of boxes and printed patterns: individual cobblestones and flagstones with depth and worn edges, timber beams, window frames and sills, eaves and roof tiles, carved wood, plaster and stone with relief. Painted materials that catch the light (wet stone glints, polished floors reflect, brass and iron shine), warm point lights against cool shade, soft shadows from every light, light in the air. No ink lines on the world, only on the characters. No text, no interface, no watermarks, no new characters.
