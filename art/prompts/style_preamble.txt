Hand-drawn 1990s Saturday-morning-cartoon style for a gothic horror game set in Barovia. Bold, even black ink outlines; flat cel shading with two or three tones; exaggerated, readable silhouettes; limited saturated palette of deep blood reds, bruised purples, moonlit blues, bone and parchment, sickly greens and candle orange, in the spirit of Castlevania: Symphony of the Night. No photorealism, no soft airbrushed gradients, no modern clothing, no text or watermarks.
