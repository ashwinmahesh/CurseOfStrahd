Hand-painted decals for a modern, richly lit 3D diorama in the spirit of Square Enix's HD-2D games, made for a gothic horror RPG set in Barovia (Curse of Strahd): marks that will be laid over the game's own floors and walls. Stylised and hand-crafted rather than photoreal, painted like the world in the reference picture, with soft painted shading and crisp small detail and no ink outlines. Dark, cold, desaturated tones with only small touches of colour. Each mark sits alone on a plain, flat, pure white background with nothing else drawn around it: no floor, no wall, no shadow on the white, no frame, no text, no watermarks.
