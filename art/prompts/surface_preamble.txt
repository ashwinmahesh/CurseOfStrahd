Hand-painted surface texture for a modern, richly lit 3D diorama in the spirit of Square Enix's HD-2D games, made for a gothic horror RPG set in Barovia (Curse of Strahd). Stylised and hand-crafted rather than photoreal, painted like the world in the reference picture: real modelled-looking materials with soft painted shading, crisp small detail, worn edges, grime and age, and no ink outlines. Dark, cold, desaturated tones: soot, damp, mist grey and bruised violet shade, with only small touches of warm colour. Lit flat and evenly from straight on, with no cast shadows, no highlights from a light source, no vignette and no lighting gradient, so the game's own lights can light it. No text, no watermarks, no frame.
