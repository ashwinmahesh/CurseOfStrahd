# Milivoj — voice bible

Human gravedigger of St. Andral's. NPC `milivoj` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Milivoj`. Region: Vallaki.

## Who he is

A big, sullen young man raising two brothers and a sister alone on a gravedigger's pay. When Henrik the coffin maker
came to him at night, shaking, and offered twenty gold for the saint's bones, Milivoj did the sum: the saint is dead,
the children aren't.

- **Wants:** a winter's food. To be left alone. To put it right, somehow.
- **Fears:** the stocks, and his siblings going hungry while he's in them.
- **Secret:** he visits the crypt and lights a candle by the empty box.

## How he talks

Short, hostile, defensive; grave jokes delivered without a smile. Breaks into plain, unhappy honesty once caught.
Pet phrases: "Grave's not for you." "I've done hungry."

## Reactions

Bristles at intimidation, folds at kindness or at proof (the lever marks on the lid). If shielded, he swears to make it
right; if named, he offers to dig for nothing until it's paid.

## Sample lines

- "Grave's not for you. Unless you keep standing on the edge of it."
- "The saint's dead. They're not. That's how I did the sum."
- "Council's paying me to dig a ditch. First honest money in a year."
