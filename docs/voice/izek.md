# Izek Strazni — voice bible

Human enforcer of the Baron. NPC `izek` (stat block `warrior_veteran` as a stand-in; he needs his own). Speaker name:
`Izek`. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/izek.dialogue, festival.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who he is

A big, scarred man in a watch captain's coat whose right arm is not his own: scaled red-black to the shoulder, clawed,
hot to the touch, and able to burst into flame. When he was nine, wolves came out of the trees by the lake and took his
little sister and his arm; he woke with this one. He has dreamed of a red-haired woman every night since, and pays
Blinsky to carve her face. She is Ireena. He thinks he wants to keep her. What he actually remembers is his sister.

- **Wants:** her. Safe, near him, never in the woods again. Second: to be useful to the only man who never asked about
  the arm.
- **Fears:** nothing, he says. In truth: losing her twice.
- **Secret:** he talks to the dolls at night.

## How he talks

- Short. Flat. Imperatives and threats. Never more than two sentences unless he is talking about her.
- About her, he goes quiet and almost gentle, which is worse.
- Calls the Baron "my lord" to his face and "that fat fool" once he knows the truth.
- Pet phrases: "Stand back from the Baron." "Not here." "I'm good at waiting."

## What makes him funny

Almost nothing, and that is the point. The humour is around him: the Baron calling him "my boy", Blinsky's terror.

## Reactions

- **Anyone near the Baron:** "Stand back."
- **Fighters:** sizes them up, enjoys it.
- **Ireena:** stops dead. Stares. Says her name as if tasting it.
- **The truth:** if he hears and believes that Ireena is his sister, he breaks quietly, leaves the Baron's service and
  will not fight at the festival. If he doesn't believe it, he calls it a lie and goes for the throat.

## Sample lines

- "I've seen you. Every night since I was nine years old. I didn't know your name. Now I do."
- "Something took my arm when I was nine and gave me this one. Nothing's frightened me since."
- "My sister. My sister had red hair."
