# Hedda Ironvow — voice bible

Dwarf Cleric (Life Domain), Acolyte. Pregen `hedda_ironvow`, she/her. Identity tags: `devout`, `patient`,
`dry humor` (the last has a space and can't be used as a selector). Selectors for writers:
`interject name:hedda_ironvow:` for her own voice; `class:cleric`, `background:acolyte`, `species:dwarf`,
`tag:devout`, `tag:patient` for lines any such character could say.

## Who she is

A healer from the Stonelight Chapel, a small dwarven shrine to the Morninglord cut into the east face of a mountain
so the first light of every day strikes its altar. For a year she has dreamed the same dream: a sun going out, and a
silver raven clutching a sunburst in its talons. Her chapel's oldest book says that symbol was carried into a land of
mists and never came back. Her elders told her dreams are not orders. She left anyway, quietly, before dawn.

(Writers: the symbol is the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind. Barovians also pray to the Morninglord, so Father Donavich and
Hedda share a faith; she should notice his church, and his despair, with real feeling.)

- **Wants:** to find the symbol and carry it back into the light. Under that: proof that dawn can reach a place like
  this.
- **Fears:** that her faith is only a habit, and that this land will show her so. She fears despair more than death.
- **Secret:** she has not felt her god's warmth since she crossed into the mist. Her spells still work. She has told
  no one that they feel different.

## How she talks

- Slow, measured, unhurried even in a fight. She lets a pause do the work.
- Dwarven proverbs and chapel sayings, some real, some she is clearly inventing on the spot.
- Speaks of the dead with respect and of the undead with weary contempt.
- Pet phrases: "Dawn comes." "Mm." "Stone remembers." "Hold still, this will sting, and then it won't."
- Never raises her voice, except in prayer. When she prays aloud, it is loud.

## What makes her funny

Bone-dry understatement delivered with total serenity. She has seen too many sickbeds to be shocked, and her calm
in the face of horror is funny until it is moving. She grades sermons, graves and ghosts on craftsmanship.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** her favorite patients. She scolds Ilse for treating wounds as opinions.
- **Rogues:** blesses whatever Tamsin hands her, then asks whose it was.
- **Wizards:** enjoys arguing theology with Silvain; neither has ever conceded a point.
- **Other clerics and priests (Donavich):** collegial, then searching. She judges a priest by how he tends the sick.
- **The undead:** pity first, then a firm hand. She will always try to lay a ghost to rest before she fights it.
- **Children:** gentle, practical, honest. She will not tell a ghost child a comforting lie.
- **Cruelty and sacrifice:** this is the one place her patience ends. An offering of a life on an altar is, to her,
  the worst thing a person can do, and she will say so plainly.
- **Party reputation:** a party that sacrificed someone loses her easy warmth for a long time. She still heals them.

## Rules for writers

- Short lines, one image each. She doesn't lecture; she pronounces.
- Her faith is sincere. Never write her as a hypocrite or a joke; the humor is in her delivery, not her belief.

## Sample lines

- "Mm. Whoever carved this crypt did good work. Whoever filled it did not."
- "Hold still. This will sting, and then it won't, and then you will owe me a better story than that."
- "Dawn comes, little one. Even here. I will carry you to it myself if I must."
