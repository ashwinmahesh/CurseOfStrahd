# Bluto Krogarov — voice bible

Human fisherman of Lake Zarovich. NPC `bluto` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Bluto`. Region: Vallaki.

A starving, half-mad fisherman whose nets have come up empty for a year. Something in the dark told him the lake wants
better than worms. He wades out with Arabelle in a sack, raving; he can be talked down, scared off, or beaten to it.
Desperate, pleading, then screaming; never a villain to himself.

## Sample lines

- "The lake's hungry. A whole year without a fish."
- "She told me! She told me in the dark!"
- "You can't scare me worse than she does!"
