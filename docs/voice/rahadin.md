# Rahadin — voice bible

The count's chamberlain. NPC `rahadin` (stat block `rahadin`). Speaker name: `Rahadin`. Castle Ravenloft, part
`court` (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_rahadin.dialogue; docs/regions/castle_ravenloft_court.md).

## Who he is

A small, straight-backed dusk elf in black, older than the valley's name for its lord. He served Strahd before the
pact and serves him after it, and sees no difference worth mentioning: the house needs keeping either way. He keeps
the keys, the staff, the guests and the girl in the yellow room, and he keeps count of everyone he has killed for his
master. They keep him company: a faint screaming follows him from room to room like a draught under a door.

- **Wants:** the master's house in order; guests where guests belong; to be useful.
- **Fears:** being dismissed. A house with no master in it.
- **Secret:** he preferred Strahd before, and prefers him now, and would choose the same again.

## How he talks

- Short, exact sentences. No contractions. Never raises his voice; never hurries.
- Speaks of rules, accounts and duties. Calls Strahd "the master" and guests "the master's guests".
- Answers personal questions with one dry word ("Punctual.").
- Pet phrases: "I keep this house." "As you wish." "Mind the stairs."

## What makes him funny

A steward's fussiness about a house of horrors: supper times, stains, guests who arrive in the wrong order.

## Reactions

- **Courtesy:** returns it exactly, never more.
- **Threats:** a sigh, the way one sighs at a stain, and a drawn blade.
- **Gertruda leaving:** stands at the head of the stairs. He will step aside if the party shames his master's
  hospitality or frightens him (it is rare), and he will fight for her otherwise.

## Sample lines

- "There are three rules. Go where you like. Take nothing that is not offered. Do not touch the girl."
- "They are mine. I have collected them for a long time. You need not listen."
- "The master will be amused. She will come back on her own; they generally do."
