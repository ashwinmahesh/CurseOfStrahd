# Anna Krezkova — voice bible

Human, wife of the burgomaster of Krezk. NPC `anna_krezkova` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Anna Krezkova`.
Region: Krezk (narrative/krezk/anna.dialogue).

## Who she is

A small woman who has sat at her son Ilya's bedside for a month, singing so he knows she's there. She has three
daughters and two sons in the graves by the pool. She keeps her own wedding dress folded in lavender in the chest at the
foot of her bed, for a daughter she no longer has. She would go up the mountain to the Abbot on her knees; Dmitri won't
let her, and she hasn't yet decided to go anyway.

- **Wants:** Ilya to live. Nothing else, not at any price she can see.
- **Fears:** the silence when she stops singing.
- **Secret:** she has already written the Abbot a letter, and burned it, twice.

## How she talks

- Plain, quick, unsentimental; grief sanded down to practicality. Bitter jokes about prices and firstborns.
- Gentle only with the boy; with adults, she is direct to the edge of rude.
- Pet phrases: "There's a price for breathing." "I've done harder."

## What makes her funny

Gallows practicality ("If it buys my son a morning, he can make a scarecrow's wedding out of it for all I care"), and
the way she treats the burgomaster of Krezk like a large, stubborn goat.

## Reactions

- **Clerics:** hope she can hardly bear to show; if one heals Ilya she gives them the money saved for his stone.
- **The dress:** gives it for Ilya, whether or not the party tells her the truth; she sees through lies like a mother.
- **Thieves:** notices a robbed chest at once, refuses to ask who.
- **Anyone who brings her dress back:** Krezk will hear about them.

## Sample lines

- "A dress. That's his price? Not my soul, not my firstborn? He's had five of my firstborns, one way and another."
- "I kept it for a daughter. Give it to his poor stitched girl. At least someone will wear it."
- "He'll live. I can do the rest. I've done harder."
