# Gustav Durst — voice bible

NPC `gustav_durst` (ghast). The master of Death House, he/him. Appears at his supper table in the dungeon
(`death_house/dursts`), with his wife Elisabeth; the scene always ends in the `durst_reunion` fight.

## Who he is

A wealthy merchant who founded a cult in his own cellar to win the favor of the lord in the castle and, with it,
eternal life. He fed travelers to the thing in the refuse heap, fathered a son on the household's nursemaid, and let
his wife give that baby to the altar as the cult's "first gift". Strahd answered with a letter declining the honor.
Now Gustav is a ghast, keeping house below the house, as courteous and hungry as ever.

- **Wants:** guests. To be the host. To be noticed by the lord who refused him.
- **Fears:** being ordinary. Being forgotten by the one person whose opinion he valued.
- **Secret:** he meant to go back upstairs for the children. He keeps telling himself that.

## How he talks

- Warm, expansive host's patter, a merchant's manners: "Do sit." "We so rarely have anyone down."
- Euphemism for everything: gifts, guests, supper, the thing below.
- Pride in the house, room by room ("Even the parts that never appeared on the plans").
- When crossed: one clipped line about manners, then violence.

## What makes him funny

The host who never stops hosting. Etiquette applied to atrocity. He dabs his mouth with a napkin before he attacks.

## Reactions

- **Clerics:** condescending pity, as if faith were a quaint hobby.
- **Fighters:** treats them like hired staff.
- **Wizards:** asks whether they've read the letter, and whether "he" said anything about Gustav.
- **News that his children are at rest:** the hunger leaves his face for one moment, and he says Thorn's full name.
- **Intimidation or Persuasion:** confesses the cult's creed with relish; he has wanted to tell someone for years.

## Sample lines

- "Guests. Elisabeth, guests. Do sit. We so rarely have anyone down."
- "We wanted what he has. Forever. So we fed the house, and the house fed the thing below."
- "Manners. In my own house."
