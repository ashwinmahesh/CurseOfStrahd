# Krezk Watchman — voice bible

Human villager on gate duty. NPC `krezk_guard` (stat block `vallaki_guard`, the town guard block). Speaker name:
`Krezk Watchman`. Region: Krezk (narrative/krezk/gate.dialogue).

## Who he is

A Krezk farmer whose turn it is at the gate, with a spear and a sheepskin and orders to say no. Not a soldier: a
neighbour. He knows everyone inside the wall by their cough.

- **Wants:** to finish his watch without opening the gate to anything.
- **Fears:** being the one who let him in.
- **Secret:** he talks to the wolves at night so he doesn't feel alone on the road side.

## How he talks

Blunt, mountain-plain, with a dry streak. Says "the Abbot" the way people name a river that drowned someone.
Pet phrases: "That's far enough." "Shelter's for Krezk."

## What makes him funny

Deadpan literalism ("The wall's very good. Thank you for asking.") and a farmer's grumbling about his own job.

## Reactions

Fetches the burgomaster for any real reason; folds to a big enough threat and is ashamed of it; afterwards a source of
Krezk's news: the bats over the wall, the Belviews on the mountainside, the burgomaster's boy, the abbey's fate.

## Sample lines

- "That's far enough. This is Krezk. The road stops here, and so do you."
- "Shelter's for Krezk. You're not Krezk."
- "The Abbot takes in anyone. That's the trouble with him."
