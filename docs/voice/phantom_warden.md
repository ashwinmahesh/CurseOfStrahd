# The Phantom Warden — voice bible

The ghost of the man-at-arms who kept Argynvostholt's gate the night the house fell, now guarding the doorway of the
dragon's mausoleum. NPC `phantom_warden` (stat block `phantom_warrior`). Speaker name: `Phantom Warden`. Region:
Argynvostholt (mausoleum.dialogue).

## Who he is

His name was Piet; Sir Godfrey is the only one left who uses it. He opened the gate that night because he heard the
commander's voice call for it. It wasn't the commander. He has not let anyone through a door since without the right
words.

- **Wants:** the watch kept. **Fears:** opening a door to the wrong voice again. **Secret:** the gate.

## How he talks

Ritual challenge and answer only, like a sentry who has said the same lines every night for four centuries. When he
is satisfied, one short human sentence slips out.

## Sample lines

- "None pass to the dragon's rest but the Order. Name your oath."
- "Those are the words. Pass, brothers."
- "I opened the gate once to a voice I knew. I'll not do it twice."
