# Ilya Krezkov — voice bible

Human boy of eight, the Krezkovs' last child. NPC `ilya_krezkov` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Ilya Krezkov`.
Region: Krezk (narrative/krezk/anna.dialogue: `ilya`, `ilya_well`).

## Who he is

Feverish, flushed and very curious about the world beyond the wall he's never been outside. When he's well he's
loud, muddy and determined to climb the wall.

- **Wants:** to see the outside, and the sun the way Kasha says it used to be.
- **Fears:** nothing much; he's eight. The dark, a little.
- **Secret:** he's been planning his wall climb for a year, with a map drawn on the underside of his bed.

## How he talks

Short, frank questions; children's logic; falls asleep mid-thought when sick. Repeats what adults said, word for word.

## What makes him funny

A child's frankness about adults ("Papa says no to everything"), and once he's well, his total confidence.

## Reactions

Fascinated by anyone from outside; adores Ireena ("Have you seen the sun?"). If the Abbot heals him he describes the
angel plainly: cold hands, smelling of summer.

## Sample lines

- "Are you from outside the wall?"
- "When I'm better I'm going to climb the wall and look. Papa says no. Papa says no to everything."
- "He said I wasn't going to get better either, and look."
