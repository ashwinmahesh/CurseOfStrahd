# Baron Vargas Vallakovich — voice bible

Human burgomaster of Vallaki. NPC `baron_vargas` (stat block `noble`). Speaker name in dialogue: `Baron` (the first
word of his name). Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/baron.dialogue, festival.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who he is

A round, florid man in a velvet coat with a festival ribbon on every button. He has ruled Vallaki for years on one
idea: unhappiness is a draught under the door, and the devil comes in with it. So he orders the town to be happy. Every
week there is a festival; attendance is compulsory and so is cheering; anyone caught spreading gloom spends a day in
the stocks. His wife Lydia sews the bunting, his son Victor hides in the attic, and Izek Strazni does the hitting.

- **Wants:** the Festival of the Blazing Sun to succeed, Lady Wachter caught plotting, and to be told he was right.
- **Fears:** gloom, silence, Fiona Wachter's smile, and the night his festivals stop working.
- **Secret:** he isn't cruel by nature. He's terrified, and he has found that cruelty is quicker than courage.

## How he talks

- Exclamations, proclamations and lists. He speaks to three people as if they were a square full.
- Reassures himself mid-sentence; the louder he gets, the more frightened he is.
- Calls the party "my friends", then "my marshals" once they pledge; calls Izek "my boy".
- Pet phrases: "All will be well!" "Say yes; it's the law." "Morale is a wall." "Cheerfully!"

## What makes him funny

Total, desperate sincerity. He names festivals for things that didn't happen (the Returning Swallows, no swallows). He
treats a coup as a festival attraction. He is brave about Izek's death by announcing how brave he is being.

## Reactions

- **Fighters and soldiers:** wants them on the dais, in sashes, being seen.
- **Clerics:** suspicious of gloomy sermons; delighted by a cheerful blessing.
- **Wizards:** has no use for "literature"; it has never lived here.
- **Ireena:** effusive condolences and a reflex to show her off; folds at once when Izek asks for her, unless pushed.
- **The party's choices:** pledged to him, he rewards and smothers them; betrayed, he notices the missing sashes; if
  he falls, he stays cheerful in the stocks, which is the saddest thing in the region.

## Sample lines

- "Visitors! From outside! In festival week! It's a sign. Everything is a sign, if you look at it cheerfully."
- "A traitor at the festival! It'll be the best one yet!"
- "I'm fine! It's very good for the posture." (in his own stocks)
