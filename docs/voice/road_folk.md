# Folk on the Old Svalich Road (Bogdan, Grigor) — voice bible

Two Barovians the party may meet in the road's random events (narrative/svalich_road/events.dialogue). Stat blocks:
`bogdan` commoner, `grigor` warrior_veteran (his three nephews fight as scouts). Speaker names: `Bogdan`, `Grigor`.
Portrait `commoner`, sprite `villager` until they get their own.

## Bogdan, the farmer with the coffin

A stooped farmer from a steading off the road, pushing his mother's coffin to the village church with his son Petru
pulling, because he will not put her in the ground unblessed. She was bitten; he doesn't know it.

- **Wants:** his mother buried properly. **Fears:** the dark catching them on the road. **Secret:** she told him a
  lonely gentleman came to her window, and he thought she was wandering.
- **Voice:** slow, polite, plain words; country courtesy ("Morning. Or what passes for it."). Breaks when he learns
  the truth, then holds together for the boy's sake.
- **Sample line:** "My mother, in the box. We're taking her to the church in the village. I won't put her in the
  ground unblessed, not with things the way they are."

## Grigor, the wolf hunter

A grizzled hunter leading his three nephews through the Svalich Woods, killing wolves one pelt at a time since the
pack took his brother off the road. He shoots first at strangers after dusk, because most strangers after dusk aren't.

- **Wants:** the wolves dead, his nephews alive. **Fears:** that he'll lose one of the boys the way he lost his
  brother. **Secret:** he has seen the big wolves stand up on two legs, and has told nobody but the boys.
- **Voice:** clipped, suspicious, hunter's economy; turns gruffly generous with anyone who gets past his bow. Knows
  Kolyan's family, and Ireena on sight.
- **Sample line:** "Strangers on this road, this near dark, are mostly his. Give me a reason to think you're not."
