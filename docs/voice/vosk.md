# Master Vosk — voice bible

An arcanaloth posing as a mild human scholar in the Amber Temple's library. NPC `vosk` (stat block `arcanaloth`).
Speaker name: `Vosk`. Region: the Amber Temple (narrative/amber_temple/vosk.dialogue). Portrait and sprite `vosk`
(moods: neutral, `smile`; an unmasked jackal portrait is an art need). True name: **Ashkarreth**.

## Who he is

A fiend of contracts. The vestiges can offer but cannot write, so for centuries he has been the temple's broker:
he witnesses each bargain and takes a small commission in souls. He witnessed Strahd's, and is proud of it ("still
running after four hundred years"). Now he wants a second vessel for the Thirst whose amber lies empty: a rival or
heir to the prince, which would be very good for business.

- **Wants:** a signature on the hollow amber. Failing that, any signature.
- **Fears:** his true name, which the forgetful warden wrote down and never burned.
- **Secret:** he isn't a scholar, his tea doesn't smell, his spectacles have no glass, and his shadow has ears.

## How he talks

- Silky, generous, academic; the vocabulary of a notary: terms, fees, considerations, concluded.
- Never quite lies about a bargain; he leaves out the part you didn't ask about. He lies freely about everything else
  (he will "remember" Aurel Mirescu from the way Silvain says the name).
- Pet phrases: "Do come and find me." "People never read the terms." "A small commission."

## What makes him funny

Total, cheerful professionalism about damnation. He treats the end of a soul like a filing error.

## Reactions

- **Scholars:** flatters them as colleagues. Silvain catches him reciting their own face back to them.
- **Clerics:** amused; they always see him first.
- **Ireena:** "the same face, a different signature" (Tatyana); he knows exactly what she is.
- **Gifts taken:** his nose lifts like a dog's at a kitchen door.
- **His true name:** the smile goes for the first time; bound, he answers truthfully and leaves for good.

## Sample lines

- "Every sarcophagus is a standing offer, and every offer has terms. People never read them."
- "I introduced them. He did the selling. I only took my fee."
- "Concluded. A pleasure doing business."
