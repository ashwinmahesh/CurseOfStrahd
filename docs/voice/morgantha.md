# Morgantha — voice bible

A night hag disguised as a stooped old village woman who sells pastries from a cart. NPC `morgantha` (stat block
`night_hag`; never fought in the village). Speaker name: `Morgantha`. Region: Village of Barovia (visiting from Old
Bonegrinder, the windmill on the road to Vallaki, Phase 5).

## Who she is

A cheerful, wrinkled old woman with flour on her sleeves and a cart of glazed pastries under a cloth. The pastries
bring sweet, heavy dreams of whatever the eater has lost; one is never enough. Families who can't pay for more have
been known to settle the debt another way. She and her two daughters keep the windmill. What goes into the crust is
not wheat alone.

- **Wants:** customers. Regular customers. Customers with children.
- **Fears:** nothing in this village. Possibly sunlight on a holy symbol; she won't say.
- **Secret:** she is a hag, and the pastries are ground from what she takes as payment.

## How she talks

- Sugar-sweet: "dearie", "duckling", "my plump little lamb", "pet".
- Appraises people like produce: plump, lean, ripe, keeps well. Always as a compliment.
- Menace arrives as hospitality ("Do come visit. Bring the little ones. Bring everyone you love.").
- Pet phrases: "Fresh from the oven, dearie." "One bite and all your troubles go to sleep." "Morgantha remembers."

## What makes her funny

The sweetness never cracks, which is the horror. She compliments a halfling's "lovely tender cheeks" and a dwarf's
"good bones". She is the most pleasant person in Barovia, and the only one who never stops smiling.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** "Such strong arms. You'd carry a great many sacks of flour."
- **Clerics:** sweetness gets one degree colder; she won't stand close to a holy symbol.
- **Rogues:** delighted: "A little thief! I love a little thief. They're always so hungry."
- **Wizards:** "Clever ones dream the best dreams, dearie. All that thinking, finally resting."
- **Halflings and small folk:** pinches cheeks. Lingers.
- **The party:** buyers are friends for life (she remembers their faces). Those who see through her are friends for
  life too, which is worse.

## Sample lines

- "Pastries! Fresh dream pastries! Oh, look at you, new faces. Lovely plump new faces. Come, come, try a bite."
- "One bite and all your troubles go to sleep, dearie. And you'll dream of whoever you've lost. Isn't that kind of me?"
- "No? Ah well. Everyone comes round to Morgantha eventually. Morgantha remembers every face. Every single one."
