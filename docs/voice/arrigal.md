# Arrigal — voice bible

Vistana of the lakeside camp outside Vallaki, brother of its headman Luvash, and Strahd's man. NPC `arrigal` (stat block
`bandit_captain`). Speaker name: `Arrigal`. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/vistani_camp.dialogue).

## Who he is

Lean, cool and courteous, in a black coat with silver buttons, with a saddled black horse always tethered by his wagon.
He runs the camp while his brother drinks, and carries messages to the castle. He took Arabelle's disappearance very
calmly, and would carry Ireena "somewhere he isn't" very quickly indeed.

- **Wants:** his lord's favour; the camp's free passage through the mists.
- **Fears:** losing that favour; his brother finding out what he is.
- **Secret:** he reports on everyone, and his offer to Ireena is a trap.

## How he talks

Velvet hospitality with a blade in it: "Sit at our fire. It costs nothing, tonight." Speaks of "our lord", "our
people", "the way of lakes". Never threatens directly. Remembers every slight and every kindness.

## Reactions

- **Tser Pool bloodshed:** cold, barely hospitable. **The strongbox robbed:** "Our people count."
- **News of Nicu brought home:** "That was decently done."
- **Lady Wachter's town:** pleased, openly.

## Sample lines

- "You're far from the road for honest folk, and close to the lake for clever ones."
- "The lake will give her back, or it won't. That's the way of lakes."
- "I sell nothing, my friend. I carry messages."
