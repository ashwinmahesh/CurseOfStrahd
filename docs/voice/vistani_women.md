# The Vistani women (Alenka, Mirabel, Sorvia) — voice bible

Three Vistani women at the corner table of the Blood of the Vine. NPCs `alenka`, `mirabel`, `sorvia` (stat block
`commoner`; they never fight). Speaker names: `Alenka`, `Mirabel`, `Sorvia`. Region: Village of Barovia. They speak as a
trio in one conversation (narrative/village_of_barovia/vistani.dialogue).

## Who they are

Travelling Vistani in bright layered skirts and too much silver, drinking the tavern's worst wine and enjoying the
gloom like a play. Their people can pass through the mists that trap everyone else, and they are on comfortable terms
with the lord of the land: they watch newcomers and tell him what they see. They also, sincerely, send newcomers to
Madam Eva at Tser Pool, who reads the Tarokka for strangers.

- **Wants:** wine, amusement and good stories to carry back. A coin or two.
- **Fears:** losing the devil's favour, the one thing that keeps the mists open for their people.
- **Secret:** they report to Strahd. They feel a little bad about it, Sorvia most of all.

## How they talk

- **Alenka** (eldest, leader): honeyed and theatrical, speaks for the three. "Sit, sit, strangers. The wine is terrible
  and the company is excellent."
- **Mirabel** (middle): mocking, quick, laughs at everyone, including herself. The one who calls Ismark "the Lesser" to
  his face.
- **Sorvia** (youngest): blurts true things, then gets elbowed. Reads palms, badly and accurately.
- Shared: "our people", "the mists open for us", "the cards", "Madam Eva". Never modern slang.

## What makes them funny

The sisterly cross-talk: Alenka performing, Mirabel heckling, Sorvia ruining the performance with the truth. Sorvia's
palm readings, which are always half a joke and half a prophecy.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** Mirabel flirts, outrageously. Alenka tells her to stop. She doesn't.
- **Clerics:** polite and a bit wary; Sorvia asks for a blessing for her horse.
- **Rogues:** professional appreciation. Alenka moves her rings.
- **Wizards:** Sorvia asks to see a trick; Mirabel bets them they can't make the wine taste better.
- **Elves:** curious; Sorvia says elves are rare in the valley and asks, too directly, how old they are.
- **The party:** a show to watch and report on. If the party buys them wine, they share more than they should.

## Sample lines

- Alenka: "Sit, strangers. You came out of the house at the end of the village. People don't. So: buy us a cup, and tell us how."
- Mirabel: "Ismark the Lesser has found friends. Look at him, sitting up straight. Pour him another, Arik, he's celebrating."
- Sorvia: "Your line of life is long. It's just that something keeps trying to cut it. There, see? Teeth marks."
