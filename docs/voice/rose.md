# Rose (Rosavalda Durst) — voice bible

NPC `rose`. Ghost child, ten years old, she/her. Appears twice: as the house's illusion in the road at the village
edge (`death_house/rose_thorn`), and as her real ghost in the attic children's room (`death_house/rose_thorn_ghosts`).
Dialogue also in `death_house/attic` (the dollhouse) and `death_house/dungeon` (her farewell at the crypt).

## Who she is

The Dursts' daughter. When the cult's gatherings grew loud, her parents locked Rose and her little brother Thorn in
the attic "so the robed people wouldn't see them", promised to come back up, and never did. The children starved
there. Rose still believes her mother will unlock the door when the noise downstairs stops.

- **Wants:** her mother to come back. Failing that, for someone to stay. Under it: to be a good big sister.
- **Fears:** the basement, the singing, being forgotten again. Saying the cult's words out loud.
- **Secret:** she knew where the grown-ups went at night. She found the hidden stair in the dollhouse with a hairpin,
  and she has never told anyone but Thorn.

## How she talks

- Proper and a little bossy, the way a child talks when she's been told to be a lady. Full sentences.
- Quotes her mother constantly: "Mother says..." It's how she keeps her mother alive.
- Corrects Thorn. Apologizes for Thorn. Protects Thorn.
- When she understands she is dead, she gets calm and practical, which is worse than tears.
- No modern idiom, no baby talk. Short lines: 10 to 25 words.

## What makes her funny

Prim authority aimed at the wrong people ("Thorn, say sorry first"). Taking a ghost's bargain as seriously as a
dinner-party rule. She is never the joke; the house is.

## Reactions

- **Clerics:** folds her hands at once and prays the way she was taught, then forgets the words halfway.
- **Fighters and soldiers:** trusts them immediately; big, armored grown-ups are what she has been waiting for.
- **Rogues:** delighted by tricks, suspicious of pockets.
- **Wizards:** wants to know if magic can open locked doors. It can. She is quietly furious about that.
- **Being told she is dead:** gently, she accepts it and asks to be taken to her crypt. Bluntly, she protects Thorn.
- **The illusion in the road** speaks only the lure: the monster, the basement, Walter. It never remembers the party.

## Sample lines

- "Mother said she'd unlock the door when the noise downstairs stopped. Is it over? Is the noise over?"
- "Thorn, say sorry first. It's rude to climb inside people without asking."
- "Mother hid her purse under the bottom stair. She said it was for emergencies. I think this is one."
