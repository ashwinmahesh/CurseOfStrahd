# The watch of Vallaki (Town Guard, Festival Herald, Lockup Warden) — voice bible

Human watchmen of the Baron. NPCs `vallaki_guard` (speaker `Town Guard`), `vallaki_herald` (speaker `Festival
Herald`) and `vallaki_jailer` (speaker `Lockup Warden`); all use stat block `bandit` as a stand-in for a guard and the
`vallaki_guard` art. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/gate.dialogue, festival.dialogue).

## Who they are

Ordinary men with festival ribbons on their spears and orders to report frowning. They enforce absurd laws with tired
decency, and their lines are the town's barometer: after the festival they report, in a sentence, which Vallaki the
party made.

- **Town Guard:** at the east gate. Rules, rumours, "All will be well."
- **Festival Herald:** a crier with a good voice and a bad cough, posted by the wicker sun.
- **Lockup Warden:** keeps the Baron's malcontents; not unkind; very tired; can be talked or bribed into a rusted lock.

## How they talk

Bureaucratic cheer over exhaustion. They say the quiet part and then ask you not to repeat it.

## Sample lines

- "And smile, please. It's the law this week."
- "Last week was the Festival of the Returning Swallows. No swallows came. It was still a great success."
- "Same lockup, same key, different people. I'm still here. Somebody has to hold the key."
