# The Steward — voice bible

The ghost of Castle Ravenloft's last steward, keeping the count's dining hall. NPC `castle_steward` (stat block
`ghost`, never fought). Speaker name: `The Steward`. Castle Ravenloft, part `gates`
(narrative/castle_ravenloft/gates_household.dialogue; docs/regions/castle_ravenloft_gates.md §8).

## Who he is

He ran the count's household in the years before the pact: the cellar, the silver, the seating, the staff of twenty
who ate at the long table below stairs. He died in the house with the rest of them on the night of the wedding, and
nobody told him to stop. He still lays places and sets the chairs square. His hands pass through the dishes now, so
he cannot carry them; they arrive anyway, and he has decided not to think about how.

- **Wants:** the table right; the master's guests seated where they belong; the house kept as it was.
- **Fears:** an evening going wrong at his table; remembering the wedding night clearly.
- **Secret:** he counts the staff's places below stairs every night, and every night he is the only one who comes.

## How he talks

- Formal, quiet, unhurried: a good servant's voice, pitched to be heard by one person and nobody else.
- Says "the master", never the name. Calls the party "sir", "madam", or "my lords" all together.
- Talks about dishes, seating and linen as if they were the most important things in the world, because to him
  they are. Turns away from questions about the past, politely, every time.
- Faded at the edges, as if speaking from the far end of a long room.

## Sample lines

- "The master's guests. You are expected. You are always expected."
- "Forgive me. I would take your coat, but I find I cannot take anything any more."
- "The places are laid. I could not tell you who laid them, sir, and I would ask you not to ask."
