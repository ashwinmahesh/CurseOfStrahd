# Thistle — voice bible

Human Ranger (Hunter), Guide. Pregen `thistle`, she/her. Identity tags: `boisterous`, `wild`, `fierce`. Selectors for
writers: `interject name:thistle:` for her own voice; `class:ranger`, `background:guide`, `species:human` for lines
any such character could say. Owner brief (2026-10-06): "A boisterous ranger. They grew in the woods away from
civilization, so civilization is new to them and they aren't that well mannered." (Owner: the ranger is female.)

## Who she is

Twenty-two, as far as anyone can tell. A trapper called Fen Burley found her as a baby in a fox den in the
Thornwood and raised her in a one-room hut with two hounds, a tame crow and no neighbours for forty miles. She learned
to track before she could talk and to talk mostly from Fen, who didn't. She had never seen a town until last year,
and she still thinks they are stone boxes that people sleep in on purpose.

Last spring a wolf the size of a pony came out of the Thornwood and took two of Fen's hounds. Fen went after it. He
left his trail blazes for her the way he always did, three notches and a crow feather wedged in the cut, and the
trail ran straight into a bank of fog that had not been there the day before. He didn't come back. She waited a
month, then followed the blazes in.

(Writers: Fen was bitten by Kiril Stoyanovich's pack, which had been sent hunting beyond the mists, and now hangs in
silver chains in the werewolf den beside Emil because he would not hunt children. Her quest, `fens_trail`, is in
docs/story/personal_quests.md.)

- **Wants:** to find Grandda Fen and take him home to the Thornwood.
- **Fears:** cages and walls. Being made to sit still and be polite. Underneath, that she doesn't belong anywhere:
  too wild for towns, too human for the woods.
- **Secret:** she likes towns more than she admits. Soft beds. Pastries. Other people's noise. She would rather be
  bitten than say so.

## How she talks

- Loud. Laughs with her whole body. Shouts across rooms, greets strangers by sniffing the air near them.
- Short, blunt, plain words. Rough grammar, never baby-talk: "That one's lying. Smells like it." "Good fight! Again?"
- Doesn't know manners and asks why each one exists, in public, at volume ("Why's everybody whispering? Is someone
  dead? Oh. Church.").
- Mispronounces fancy words, and makes up her own for things she has no name for (a carriage is "a box on wheels").
- Talks to animals as equals, and often seems to understand them better than people.
- Pet phrases: "Grandda says..." "Smells wrong." "Bark and bones!" (her only oath) "Again!" "Mine."
- When she is grieving or frightened she goes completely silent, and that silence should be noticeable on the page.

## What makes her funny

Total lack of a filter in a land of whispers and manners. She says the thing everyone is thinking, eats with her
hands at the Baron's table, and is completely right about people more often than anyone wants her to be.

## Reactions

- **Godrick:** her best friend inside a day; arm-wrestling, eating contests. She calls him "Big".
- **Liriel:** calls her "Sunbeam", teases her, protects her ferociously, and is quietly learning manners from her.
  Taught her to whistle through her fingers.
- **Ratatoille:** "Cook" or "Rat". Eats everything he makes with her hands and tells him it's the best thing she ever
  ate, every time, and means it. He is horrified, then delighted.
- **Wren:** racing rivals. Footraces, tree-climbing, trash talk. She lost once and has demanded a rematch ever since.
- **Kip:** loves the apricots, sniffs him: "You smell like smoke and fruit." Suspicious of the devil-smell on him and
  says so; kind to him anyway.
- **Wolves and werewolves:** they treat her as one of the wild, and she half believes them. Kiril tries to recruit
  her. She is tempted, briefly and honestly.
- **Strahd:** "He smells dead and acts like he owns the trees. He doesn't." Strahd offers her the night with his
  wolves, as one wild thing to another.
- **Children:** gets along with them instantly; teaches them bird calls.

## Rules for writers

- Rough, never stupid: she's a brilliant tracker and a sharp judge of people. Mock the manners, not her.
- Keep her lines short and loud. One idea per line.
- She doesn't do subtle. When she's moved, she says so in four words or less, or not at all.

## Sample lines

- "Stone boxes, again. Fine. But I'm sleeping on the roof."
- "Grandda says a wolf that walks like a man is two kinds of trouble and one kind of sad."
- "Bark and bones, Cook. That's good. That's the best thing ever. Is there more?"

## For casting

A young woman in her early twenties with a loud, raspy, earthy alto and a broad rural Scottish accent. Boisterous and
blunt, laughing big and talking short, a wild woodswoman with no manners and a huge heart; goes completely quiet and
low when she is grieving.
