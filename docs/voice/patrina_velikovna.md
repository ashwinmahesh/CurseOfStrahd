# Patrina Velikovna — voice bible

Kasimir Velikov's sister: a dusk elf who climbed to the Amber Temple to learn what the lord of the valley knew, then
went to his castle to learn the rest. Her people stoned her for it. Her spirit came back to the temple's reading
hall as a banshee. NPC `patrina_velikovna` (stat block `banshee`). Speaker name: `Patrina`. Region: the Amber
Temple (narrative/amber_temple/patrina.dialogue). Portrait and sprite `patrina_velikovna` (moods: neutral, `angry`).
Kasimir: data/npcs/kasimir_velikov.json, narrative/vallaki/kasimir.dialogue (he tells the party of her:
`kasimir_sister_told`).

## Who she is

The cleverest of the dusk elves and the only curious one. She still believes she was right to go to the castle:
the lord was the only one in the valley who answered her letters, and for a while he treated her as an equal. What
she cannot forgive is the crowd, and her brother standing at the back of it with empty hands.

- **Wants:** to hear Kasimir say it to her face. Not to be brought back.
- **Fears:** being only a ghost who weeps; being bought back "like a coat out of pawn".
- **Secret:** the stones hurt less than the silence afterward.

## How she talks

- Proud, quick, cutting; scholar's precision with a knife in it. Short sentences when angry, long ones when reading.
- She turns the same crumbling page for ever. Her wail is grief made into a weapon.
- Never calls the Vistani anything but the Vistani; calls Kasimir "little brother" only when she has forgiven him.

## Reactions

- **Scholars:** a flicker of fellowship, then contempt for anyone who reads to understand rather than to win.
- **Clerics:** "Your gods don't climb this high." A prayer can still lay her to rest (Religion 16).
- **Strangers speaking for Kasimir:** scorn, then, if they are honest about his fear (Persuasion 15), release.
- **Kasimir himself:** fury, then, when he says it plainly, peace. She tells him to go and be brave somewhere she
  can't see.

## Sample lines

- "Do you know how long it's been since anyone in this valley had the nerve to climb up here and read?"
- "That's the trouble with being clever. Someone always finds a use for it."
- "Three centuries, and it took you one breath."
