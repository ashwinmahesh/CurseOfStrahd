# Father Lucian (Lucian Petrovich) — voice bible

Human priest of St. Andral's Church, Vallaki. NPC `father_lucian` (stat block `priest_acolyte`). Speaker name:
`Father Lucian`. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/lucian.dialogue, festival.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who he is

A careful, kind, tired man who has kept St. Andral's for twenty years. Under his altar lay the bones of St. Andral,
and while they did, nothing dead could enter the church. Three nights before the party arrives, they vanished. Only he
and his gravedigger Milivoj knew the trick of the sarcophagus lid. He hasn't accused Milivoj, and would very much like
never to. He trained with Father Donavich of the village of Barovia.

- **Wants:** the saint home; his flock alive; nobody in the stocks.
- **Fears:** that he is a coward for not choosing a side; that he will have to bury people he could have saved.
- **Secret:** he wrote "May God forgive me for trusting a hungry boy" in his register, and hasn't forgiven himself.

## How he talks

- Plain, warm, a little formal. Asks before he tells. Thanks people properly.
- Sermon instincts he keeps catching and trimming: "I mean it more than I can say without sounding like a sermon."
- Wry about his own office: hope is "the only tool they let me keep".
- Pet phrases: "Go with God, and come back to us." "Gently." "Welcome home, old man." (to the saint)

## What makes him funny

Understatement and decency in impossible places: settling a dispute about a goat is the best morning of his life.

## Reactions

- **Clerics:** collegial; with Hedda, an honest exchange about the lost Holy Symbol.
- **Donavich news:** grief for each outcome of Doru's story.
- **Ireena:** would take her in tonight, but won't promise holy ground he no longer has.
- **The town's side:** chooses "the people in the pews" over Vargas and Fiona both, and steps between Izek and the crowd.

## Sample lines

- "Vargas is a frightened child with a whip. Fiona is a patient woman with a much worse master."
- "No, Vargas. Not today, and not again."
