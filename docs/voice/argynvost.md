# Argynvost (the echo) — voice bible

What remains of the silver dragon who built Argynvostholt and founded the Order of the Silver Dragon. NPC `argynvost`
(no stat block; he does not fight). Speaker name: `Argynvost`. Region: Argynvostholt (argynvost.dialogue,
beacon.dialogue). Appears as frost drawing a long head across the chapel's coloured window, and a voice.

## Who he is

A silver dragon who liked people enough to wear their shape and keep a house full of them. He founded the Order to
keep watch over the valley and lit a beacon in his tower every night so that farmers in the dark would know someone
was awake. He died in his own courtyard when the conqueror took the house; his skull was carried off to the castle as
a trophy. What's left of him lingers in the chapel, held here by his knights' oath as much as they are. He is fading.

- **Wants:** his knights released; the beacon lit; his skull home with his bones.
- **Fears:** that his knights have become what they fought; that he is too faint now to be heard.
- **Secret:** he loved Vladimir like a son and blames himself for the oath sworn over his body.

## How he talks

- Warm, unhurried, a little amused. Old enough that nothing hurries him, kind enough that nothing sours him.
- Simple images: snow, lamps, watchfires, rain on a roof. Calls the living "friends".
- Pet phrase: "Keep the watch."
- Never commands. Asks.

## What makes him funny

A grandfather's delight in small mortal things (heartbeats, a cleric's stubbornness, a thief's interest in his
desk) and his dry understatement about being dead and headless.

## Reactions

- **Clerics:** he kept the chapel for his knights; on bad days, he admits, they prayed to him.
- **Sages:** pleased to be asked; sorry nobody wrote it all down.
- **Ireena:** "You have an old face, child. Older than you are. I'm sorry for it."
- **The beacon lit:** the only time he is loud.

## Sample lines

- "Visitors with heartbeats. I had forgotten the sound. Like rain on a roof. Please, keep doing it."
- "I lit it every night so the valley would know someone was awake. It is very dark out there without it."
- "There's a little fire left in my bones. Not much. Enough for one night, and one night is all they need."
