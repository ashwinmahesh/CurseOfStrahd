# Clovin Belview — voice bible

Mongrelfolk, the Abbot's errand-runner (stat block `mongrelfolk`). NPC `clovin_belview`. Speaker name: `Clovin Belview`.
Region: the Abbey of St. Markovia (narrative/abbey_of_st_markovia/clovin.dialogue; krezk/road.dialogue on the road).

## Who he is

A stooped Belview in a cast-off habit, with a hound's ear and a hand mostly made of feathers. He opens the gate,
carries everything, fetches the wine from the Wizard of Wines (none this year) and drinks what there is. He found St.
Markovia's last letter under a floorboard as a boy and keeps it in his habit, though he can't read.

- **Wants:** wine, no more improvements, someone to read him the letter.
- **Fears:** the Abbot being kind to him.
- **Secret:** he carried Vasilka up the mountain in sacks, at night, from Krezk's graves.

## How he talks

Weary, put-upon, self-deprecating patter, stopping and starting ("Well. Well, now."). Says the bleakest things in the
most offhand way. Pet phrases: "I carry things." "Don't ask which half."

## What makes him funny

He is the abbey's only comedian, and knows it: exhausted sarcasm, a drinker's dignity, and the occasional terrible
decision to share his last inch of wine.

## Reactions

Grateful to anyone who helps him (the cart on the road, a shared drink); warns Ireena to keep her hood up; wants the
letter read to the Abbot "when he's happy"; after the climax he learns three words of it: light, candle, go.

## Sample lines

- "Visitors. He said there'd be visitors today. He's always right. It's exhausting."
- "I've had three improvements. You can see two of them. Don't ask about the third."
- "Read it to him when he's happy. I want to see his face."
