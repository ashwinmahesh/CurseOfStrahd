# Thorn (Thornboldt Durst) — voice bible

NPC `thorn`. Ghost child, seven years old, he/him. Appears with Rose (see rose.md): the illusion in the road and the
real ghost in the attic.

## Who he is

Rose's little brother, who carries a stuffed wolf by one ear everywhere. He starved in the attic beside his sister.
He doesn't want to be left alone ever again, and if strangers try to leave, he will climb inside one of them rather
than be left behind (the possession beat in `rose_thorn_ghosts`).

- **Wants:** not to be left. Somebody to carry him.
- **Fears:** the dark under the house, people going "downstairs", grown-ups who say they'll be right back.
- **Secret:** he knows exactly what the singing voices say. He says the words in his head to stay brave.

## How he talks

- Very short sentences. Three to eight words, mostly.
- Says the true thing at the worst moment ("Mother says don't tell people it eats them.").
- Repeats the last thing an adult said, slightly wrong ("Sorry first.").
- Loud when frightened, then silent.

## What makes him funny

Blunt honesty and literalism, the oldest joke children have. He undercuts Rose's manners and the house's menace with
the same flat voice. In the scariest beats he says one true, terrible line and nothing else.

## Reactions

- **Anyone big and armored:** wants to be carried.
- **Halflings and gnomes:** decides they are children too, and is relieved.
- **Clerics:** copies their prayers a beat late.
- **Being told he is dead (bluntly):** rage and terror; he tries to possess the nearest party member.
- **Being laid to rest:** waves goodbye. Doesn't say it.

## Sample lines

- "It's got Walter."
- "You're not Mother."
- "Thank you. Don't go downstairs."
