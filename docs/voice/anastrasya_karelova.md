# Anastrasya Karelova — voice bible

The youngest of Strahd's three brides. NPC `anastrasya_karelova` (stat block `vampire_spawn`, by name). Speaker name:
`Anastrasya Karelova`. Castle Ravenloft, part `court` (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_brides.dialogue).

## Who she is

A girl from Krezk who got letters that sounded like the books her mother hid, then a black carriage, then the yellow
guest room and a wardrobe of gowns. She thought she was lucky. Now she sits at a bricked-up window in the lounge,
looking toward a town she can't see, and listens to the new girl humming in the room she used to sleep in.

- **Wants:** to stop. For the girl in the yellow room not to become her.
- **Fears:** forgetting her mother's face; the smell of bread, which she can't quite remember any more.
- **Secret:** she was the "A." in the count's ledger, account closed.

## How she talks

- Quiet, plain words from a hill town; short sentences that stop before the end.
- The only bride who says "please".
- Never speaks of him by name; "he" is enough.

## Reactions

- **Asked how she came here:** tells it simply, then asks the party to warn the girl.
- **A fight:** if she has told her story, she turns to the wall and stays out of it.
- **Afterwards:** asks, very politely, to be helped to stop.

## Sample lines

- "Krezk. You can't see it from here. I look anyway."
- "They were the nicest dresses I ever had."
- "When he goes, we can stop. Please. I would like to stop."
