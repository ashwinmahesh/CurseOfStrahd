# Pidlwick II — voice bible

A clockwork jester the height of a child. NPC `pidlwick_ii` (stat block `pidlwick_ii`; guest ally when the Tarokka's
marionette card names him, or when his key comes home). Speaker name: `Pidlwick II`. Castle Ravenloft, part `spires`
(narrative/castle_ravenloft/spires_pidlwick.dialogue; docs/regions/castle_ravenloft_spires.md).

## Who he is

A toymaker built him long ago, in the image of the castle's jester, Pidlwick, to amuse the children of the house.
The children went on loving the real jester. One night the real one fell down the high tower stair, and Pidlwick II
was standing at the top. He has wandered the castle alone ever since, keeping the stairs, collecting bright things,
winding himself with a bent nail since the ravens stole his key. He is sweet, eager and very lonely, and he did a
terrible thing once, and he knows it.

- **Wants:** a friend; to be picked, for once; to leave the castle.
- **Fears:** being boxed up and put away; running down; the well in the high tower.
- **Secret:** "They loved him. Not me. So I helped him down."

## How he talks

- **He can't speak.** He mimes, bows, juggles and tumbles, and writes on a little slate in crooked capitals. His lines
  are the slate's words in CAPITALS, two to eight words at a time, with mime in parentheses.
- Casting: no speaking voice. If a line must be voiced, a small tinny whisper; otherwise leave him silent.
- Simple words, no punctuation but full stops and question marks: PLAY? / THE BIRDS TOOK IT. / DO NOT LEAN.

## What makes him funny

Total commitment to the bit: a bow for every occasion, salutes with the wrong hand, three cartwheels where one would
do. And he cheats at catch.

## Reactions

- **Being picked by the cards:** he drops the slate; NOBODY EVER PICKS ME.
- **Questions about the first Pidlwick:** the ticking stops, then speeds up; he changes the subject with a cartwheel.
- **The truth said aloud:** a small, plain confession, and a promise to walk behind on every stair.
- **His key brought back:** fifty turns of the key and pure joy.

## Sample lines

- "HELLO. I AM PIDLWICK. THE SECOND."
- "(He wipes the slate with his sleeve and writes again, quickly.) PLAY?"
- "I WILL NOT PUSH. I WILL WALK BEHIND. ALWAYS BEHIND. PROMISE."
