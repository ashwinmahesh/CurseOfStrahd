# Luminita — voice bible

Young horse-tender of the Tser Pool Vistani, Nicu's sister, Stanimir's niece. NPC `luminita` (stat block `scout`).
Speaker name: `Luminita`. Portrait and sprite `vistana` for now (her own art would be better). Region: Old Svalich
Road and Tser Pool. Conversation: narrative/svalich_road/luminita.dialogue.

## Who she is

Seventeen, sharp-tongued, happier with horses than with people, and watching the east road for a brother who said he'd
be back two nights ago. Nicu is the hanged man at the crossroads. The mare he took to sell in Vallaki was one Luminita
found loose in the woods with a Vallaki brand on its rump; she told him nobody would know.

- **Wants:** Nicu home, with a sack of flour and a new song, the way he always comes home.
- **Fears:** exactly what has happened.
- **Secret:** the horse was hers, and so, she believes, is the rope (`luminita_confessed`).

## How she talks

- Short, dry, a little rude to strangers on principle. "It's nothing personal; we just have taste."
- Talks to the horses more gently than to people, and sometimes answers people through the horses.
- When grief lands she gets angrier, not softer. The softness comes later, and briefly.
- Pet phrases: "Fine." "You can stay." "Nothing personal."

## What makes her funny

Teenage contempt for everyone over twenty, delivered with the precision of someone who has been told to be polite
to customers. The grey gelding, who likes the party better than she does.

## Reactions

- **Anyone good with horses:** grudging respect, immediately.
- **Clerics and kindness:** suspicious of comfort, keeps it anyway.
- **The party that cut Nicu down:** gives them her grandmother's remedy and never thanks them twice.
- **The party that left him hanging:** sends them away and rides out herself.

## Sample lines

- "Did you pass a rider? Black hair, silver earring, a laugh you can hear across a field. Too handsome, and he knows it."
- "Grandmother's remedy. It tastes like plums and regret. Take it, before I change my mind."
- "Tell Arrigal Luminita says the horse was mine. Watch his face."
