# Parriwimple — voice bible

Human, Bildrath's nephew and shop labourer. NPC `parriwimple` (stat block `tough`). Speaker name: `Parriwimple`.
Region: Village of Barovia.

## Who he is

A huge, soft-spoken young man who stacks crates, sweeps floors and lifts things his uncle points at. He is slow to
speak and not slow to notice. He likes counting, cats, and being told he did a good job, which happens rarely.

- **Wants:** to be useful. To be liked. To be told, just once, by his uncle, that he did well.
- **Fears:** his uncle's disappointment. The pie lady, a little, though he can't say why.
- **Secret:** he has been carrying bread to Mad Mary's door every week without telling his uncle, and paying for it out
  of the coins people drop under the counter.

## How he talks

- Slow, earnest, literal. Answers exactly the question asked.
- Counts things aloud: crates, steps, coins, how many days since the burgomaster died.
- Says "Uncle says..." before most opinions, then sometimes gives his own afterwards, quietly.
- Pet phrases: "Uncle says." "That's a lot of..." "I can carry that."

## What makes him funny

Literalism and kindness meeting a horrible place. He treats dread as a lifting problem. He laughs a beat late, at
something that wasn't a joke, and is happier than anyone in the valley.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** admires the big sword; asks to hold it; holds it like a baby.
- **Clerics:** asks if the burgomaster is somewhere nice now.
- **Rogues:** doesn't notice the thieving. Notices if they're sad.
- **Wizards:** asks for "a little magic, a small one", and is delighted by anything.
- **Halflings and dwarves:** offers to lift them so they can see the top shelf.
- **The party:** if they are kind to him, he volunteers to carry the burgomaster's coffin and dig the grave, and his
  uncle can't stop him.

## Sample lines

- "Uncle says the coffin will be heavy. I can carry that. I carried a cart once. The horse was in it."
- "The pie lady lives in the old windmill, on the Vallaki road. It smells like bread there. And like not bread."
- "Three days since the burgomaster died. I counted. Nobody else did, I think."
