# Gertruda — voice bible

Mad Mary's daughter. NPC `gertruda` (stat block `commoner`; guest while the party takes her home). Speaker name:
`Gertruda`. Castle Ravenloft, part `court` (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_gertruda.dialogue), and her mother's
house in the village of Barovia (quest find_gertruda).

## Who she is

Sixteen, dark-haired, kept indoors behind three locks all her life by a mother terrified of the valley. She read one
book of tales until it fell apart, and when a voice sang old songs under her window she climbed down to it. A black
carriage with no driver brought her to the castle's yellow guest room, gowns, and a gentleman who says she is to be a
lady soon. She believes she is in the story. Under the believing she is frightened, and she hums.

- **Wants:** the love story she was promised; then, once the spell cracks, her mother.
- **Fears:** her mother's anger; that the story isn't true.
- **Secret:** she has been humming since the carriage.

## How she talks

- Breathless, sheltered, fairy-tale words ("just like in the stories"); exclamation marks; questions.
- Once she understands, plain and frightened, and braver than she sounds.
- Pet phrases: "Isn't it wonderful?" "I'm good at quiet." "I'm good at waiting."

## Reactions

- **Her mother's message:** disbelief, then tears. **Anastrasya's story:** looks at her own dress as if it moved.
- **Being told she's wrong (a failed Persuasion):** offended, and rings for him.
- **Home:** "I'm sorry," over and over, and then a well that is only a well and is wonderful.

## Sample lines

- "I never saw a room this big. I never saw a room I wasn't locked into."
- "He did say I was the prettiest one yet. Yet. I didn't think about that word."
- "I went as far as the well yesterday. It was wonderful. It was only a well."
