# Escher — voice bible

A vampire spawn, once Strahd's favourite companion, now set aside. NPC `escher` (stat block `vampire_spawn`, named
Escher in the fight `escher_fight`). Speaker name: `Escher`. Castle Ravenloft, part `spires`
(narrative/castle_ravenloft/spires_escher.dialogue; docs/regions/castle_ravenloft_spires.md).

## Who he is

A young nobleman who came to the castle as a guest, charmed the host, and was kept. Strahd made him what he is, dressed
him, showed him things, and then, as he does with everyone, grew bored. Escher is locked in a suite of velvet and dust
high in the towers with his coats and a mirror that shows nothing. He is vain, idle, quick, and furious underneath
the drawl.

- **Wants:** out of the castle; to matter again; Strahd hurt, preferably by someone else.
- **Fears:** Strahd, absolutely; the sun; being forgotten, which he has already been.
- **Secret:** Strahd once took him to the top of the high tower to see the heart that beats for him, the way one
  shows a lover a scar. Escher has never told anyone, because nobody has asked.

## How he talks

- Arch and lazy, every sentence a little bored with itself; then a flash of real anger, quickly covered.
- Calls Strahd "our host" or "he", never by name. Calls the party "darlings" when he wants something.
- Prices everything. Nothing is free in this house, least of all conversation.
- Pet phrases: "Do keep up." "Draw your own conclusions." "I did warn you."

## What makes him funny

He is a prisoner performing boredom for an audience that never comes, and when one finally does he can't stop
performing. His complaints are about draughts, fashion and the smell of the living.

## Reactions

- **A promise of freedom:** disbelief, then he pays for it with his ring, because a gift is the only dignity left.
- **Gold:** contempt, and he takes it.
- **Flattery that fails:** the price doubles.
- **Threats:** he talks, then never speaks to them again; a threat that doesn't land, and he fights.
- **Pidlwick II:** gossip. He tells the jester's story with relish.

## Sample lines

- "One of Strahd's former. Do keep up."
- "He collects people, you see. He just doesn't play with them for very long."
- "Do shut the door on your way out. The draught is dreadful, and I can't even die of it."
