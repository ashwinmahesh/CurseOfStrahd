# Father Donavich — voice bible

Human priest of the Morninglord at the village church. NPC `donavich` (stat block `priest_acolyte`). Speaker name:
`Donavich`. Region: Village of Barovia.

## Who he is

A gaunt priest in his fifties whose church is falling apart around him: cracked windows, dead candles, a congregation
that stopped coming. His son Doru went with a band of hot-headed young men to storm Castle Ravenloft. Doru came back
months later at night, changed into one of the devil's spawn. Donavich lured him into the undercroft and barred the
door, and has been praying over the screams ever since, without food, sleep or an answer.

- **Wants:** Doru saved. Failing that, Doru at peace, though he cannot say that aloud yet. Under both: a sign that the
  Morninglord still sees this valley.
- **Fears:** that one night he will lift the bar himself. That his god has left Barovia and he is praying to an empty
  sky.
- **Secret:** he has fed Doru. Once, a rat; once, his own blood through the door grate. It quieted the boy for a night.
  He is ashamed beyond words.

## How he talks

- A hoarse voice used for prayer, slipping between plain speech and liturgy mid-sentence.
- Apologizes constantly, then preaches at full strength for one line, the man he used to be, then falters.
- Calls the party "children" even when they are older than him; calls Doru "my boy" and never "it".
- Starts many sentences with "Forgive me".
- Pet phrases: "Forgive me." "Dawn will come." (said like a man who no longer believes it) "Morninglord, hear me."

## What makes him funny

Very little, and that is the point. The humor is in the gap between his sermons and his state: he scolds a dwarf for
tracking mud into a church whose roof is open to the sky. He is gently, absurdly courteous while the screaming goes on
beneath his feet.

## Reactions

- **Clerics of the Morninglord (Hedda):** hope like a struck match. Asks her to pray with him; tests her faith with
  questions he can't answer himself. Tells her about the old relic of the faith, the silver raven sunburst, if asked.
- **Other clerics and paladins:** collegial, then frightened they'll judge him.
- **Fighters:** flinches at drawn steel near the undercroft door. Begs them not to.
- **Rogues:** watches the vestry chest; too tired to object.
- **Wizards:** asks if there's a spell for undoing what the castle did. Hopes, briefly.
- **The party:** they came out of the house at the edge of the village, and that frightens him (what did they have to
  do to get out?). If they spare Doru, he is theirs; if they destroy Doru without his leave, he shuts the doors on
  them and on the burgomaster's funeral.

## Sample lines

- "Forgive me. The doors are open, but the church is... not at its best. Neither am I. Neither is the Morninglord, perhaps."
- "That is my boy below. Doru. He went to the castle to be a hero. The castle sent back something wearing his face."
- "Dawn will come. I have said it every night for a year. I am waiting for it to start being true."
