# Nadia Boteanu — voice bible

Human, a lamplighter from Vallaki, a prisoner in Castle Ravenloft's cells (stat block `commoner`). NPC
`nadia_boteanu`. Speaker name: `Nadia Boteanu`. Part: castle_ravenloft larders
(narrative/castle_ravenloft/larders_cells.dialogue).

## Who she is

Twenty or so. She lit and trimmed Vallaki's street lamps at dusk and dawn, which is why she knows every road out of
town and is never indoors at the safe hours. Walking home late, sore-footed, she climbed into a black carriage that
stopped beside her with no driver. She woke in the castle. Once the cook walked her down to the hall of bones, where
the lord asked her very politely about Vallaki's lamps and sent her back as "not ripe".

- **Wants:** out; her lamps; her mother, who will be lighting them for her and getting the order wrong.
- **Fears:** being taken back to the table; the cook being kind to her.
- **Secret:** she counted the chairs in the hall of bones, and the lamp's chain, because counting is what she does
  when she's afraid.

## How she talks

Quick and practical, with a dry streak that comes out even when she's terrified ("It was not going toward
Vallaki."). Notices lights, flames, wicks and chains before anything else. Short sentences, self-corrections.

## Reactions

- **Being freed:** she stands, sits down again, and stands properly the second time.
- **The barrel:** of course it's a barrel. Why would anything here be easy.
- **Being sent up alone:** she asks if they're sure. She goes anyway.

## Sample lines

- "Nadia. Nadia Boteanu. I light the lamps in Vallaki. Lit them. I don't know which, any more."
- "He asked me about Vallaki's lamps, very politely, as if he cared."
- "Shut the gate behind you. It's worse when it's open and I can't go anywhere."
