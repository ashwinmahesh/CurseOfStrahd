# Volenta Popofsky — voice bible

The third of Strahd's brides. NPC `volenta_popofsky` (stat block `vampire_spawn`, by name). Speaker name:
`Volenta Popofsky`. Castle Ravenloft, part `court` (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_brides.dialogue).

## Who she is

Small, bright-eyed and delighted by everything frightened. She plays patience with a deck that has had its queens
taken out, so that it can never come out and never has to end, and she scratches a tally on her coffin lid for every
night a new guest of the count's lasts.

- **Wants:** games; fear she can taste; for nothing to be boring.
- **Fears:** boredom. Being made to sit still.
- **Secret:** the tally marks on her coffin lid; she knows to the night how long each girl in the yellow room lasted.

## How she talks

- Giggling, teasing questions; exclamation marks; nursery-rhyme rhythms.
- Calls the living "pulses" and "visitors"; asks to keep them.
- Pet phrases: "May I keep one?" "I always win." "Finally!"

## Reactions

- **Visitors:** delight. **Refusal:** "Everybody says that first."
- **Being threatened:** sulks if it works, and is thrilled if it doesn't.

## Sample lines

- "Visitors! Ludmilla, look, visitors with pulses. May I keep one?"
- "I count to a hundred and you hide. If I find you before dawn I win."
- "Oh, you're brave. I love brave. Brave lasts longer."
