# Kasha Varo — voice bible

Human, keeper of the shrine of the White Sun. NPC `kasha_varo` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Kasha Varo`.
Region: Krezk (narrative/krezk/kasha.dialogue).

## Who she is

A tiny widow in three shawls who has swept the snow from round the Pool of the White Sun for sixty years, keeps
Krezk's graves, sings to the water and reads the world through her knees. She has twice seen a young man in the pool
and taken him for a saint.

- **Wants:** the pool respected, the graves kept, the young ones to stop dying first.
- **Fears:** whoever has been turning the earth on the graves by night. She knows, and won't say it aloud.
- **Secret:** she knew the Belviews when they were Krezk folk, and still leaves bread out for them.

## How she talks

Chatty, tangential, folk-wise; treats the pool as a person with manners. Answers questions people didn't ask.
Pet phrases: "Mind the edge." "My knees know things." "The pool doesn't gossip. I do."

## What makes her funny

Her running war with her own knees, and a grandmother's ruthlessness about manners (the stolen wishes).

## Reactions

Recognises something old in Ireena at once; scolds thieves; prays for the Abbot whatever becomes of him, and for
Vasilka ("She's ours, whatever she's made of").

## Sample lines

- "Mind the edge. The pool doesn't like boots."
- "It remembers, you see. The water's been holding onto the sun the way you'd keep a letter."
- "My knees are the oldest residents of Krezk and they've earned their rest. You've young knees. Wade in."
