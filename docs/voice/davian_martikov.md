# Davian Martikov — voice bible

Human wereraven, master of the Wizard of Wines, Keeper of the Feather. NPC `davian_martikov` (stat block `wereraven`;
guest build `wereraven`). Speaker name: `Davian`. Region: Wizard of Wines (narrative/wizard_of_wines/davian.dialogue).
The Tarokka's Raven ally (`tarokka.ally.npc == davian_martikov`).

## Who he is

The white-bearded, black-eyed patriarch of a family that has pressed wine on the same hill for longer than the valley
has had a lord, and has watched that lord, as ravens, nearly as long. Three months ago the druids of Yester Hill took
his winery. He hid the last of the family's three vine stones and led his children and grandchildren into the trees.
Last spring he sent his sons Adrian and Elvir to watch Yester Hill. Only Adrian came back, and Davian has barely looked
at him since.

- **Wants:** his house, his stones, his vines green again. Under that, and unsayable: to stop blaming the wrong son.
- **Fears:** dying with the vines dead and the family split. Being a burden; being pitied.
- **Secret:** it was his errand that sent the boys up that hill. He can't forgive Adrian because he can't forgive the
  man who gave the order.

## How he talks

- Grand and blunt at once: an old merchant-king who has outlived his courtesy. Short declaratives, then a dry turn.
- Wine and vine for everything: people are vintages, grief is sediment, patience is a cask.
- Begins and ends with "Hm." Gives orders as "terms". Never says thank you; pays debts instead.
- Pet phrases: "Speak." "Hm." "Come back thirsty." "Wizards are poor at visiting."

## What makes him funny

Pride with nowhere to stand: a king on an upturned cask, issuing terms to strangers in a wood. Dry insults that are
also compliments ("You're shorter than they said"). Counting spoons.

## Reactions

- **Raven harmed on the road:** cold, but he can't afford to send the party away. Softens if Adrian accepts an apology.
- **Urwin's friends (Keepers met):** warm from the first word, in his way.
- **Clerics:** respectful; he's outlived his own faith but likes people who keep theirs.
- **Thieves (the strongbox):** a test of character more than of money.
- **Elvir's truth:** he breaks slowly, all at once, and goes to his son without a speech.
- **As the ally:** joins only when his house is his again; leaves the press to Adrian if reconciled, with a grumble if not.

## Sample lines

- "Strangers, at my fire. Either you've come to rob me, and you're three months late, or you've come to help."
- "A winery without wine is a farm with airs."
- "Adrian. The press is yours. My knees are finished with it."
