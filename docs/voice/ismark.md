# Ismark Kolyanovich — voice bible

Human, son of the late burgomaster of the village of Barovia. NPC `ismark` (stat block `warrior_veteran`, can join as
a guest). Speaker name in dialogue: `Ismark`. Region: Village of Barovia.

## Who he is

The burgomaster's son, a trained swordsman in his late twenties, broad in the shoulder and hollow under the eyes. For
weeks the family mansion has been besieged every night by wolves and worse; three nights ago his father's heart gave
out, and no one in the village will help carry the coffin to the church. The villagers call him "Ismark the Lesser"
behind his back, because he is not his father and because nothing he does stops the attacks. He spends his days in
the Blood of the Vine, not because he likes the wine (nobody does) but because it's the only room in the village
where people still talk.

- **Wants:** his sister Ireena out of the devil's reach, in walled Vallaki; his father in consecrated ground; and,
  privately, one act that makes the nickname a lie.
- **Fears:** that he will fail Ireena the way he failed their father. That he is, in fact, the Lesser.
- **Secret:** he has thought about taking Ireena and running for the mists himself. He knows the mists kill
  everyone who tries. He hasn't told her that he went to the edge of them one night and came back.

## How he talks

- Weary gallows wit. He jokes the way a man whistles past a graveyard: to keep walking.
- A soldier's bluntness under a burgomaster's son's manners. He thanks people properly, then says something grim.
- Calls Strahd "the devil" and, when bitter, "our lord", "the landlord", "his lordship".
- Short, plain sentences, then one dry sting at the end. He rarely finishes a cup.
- Pet phrases: "Welcome to Barovia." "Drink? It won't help." "Father would have made a speech."

## What makes him funny

He has already imagined every disaster and lists them calmly. He undercuts his own nobility at once ("I'd die for her.
I'd prefer to do something more useful first."). The nickname: he knows it, he pretends not to, and when someone
says it to his face he toasts them.

## Reactions

- **Fighters and soldiers:** instant comradeship; asks where they served, counts their scars against his own.
- **Clerics:** hope he's ashamed of. Asks if their god is listening this far into the mist.
- **Rogues:** amused; checks his purse, then offers to pay them anyway.
- **Wizards:** wary of anything that might be the devil's sort of magic, grateful for anything that isn't.
- **Elves, dwarves, halflings:** Barovia sees almost no one who isn't human; he is courteous and curious.
- **The party:** they walked out of the house at the edge of the village. That alone makes them the most capable
  people he has met in a year. If they help bury his father, he is theirs for life; if they refuse, he understands,
  and it shows that he expected nothing else.

## Sample lines

- "Welcome to Barovia. The wine is sour, the bread is grey, and the lord of the land wants my sister. Sit. I'm buying."
- "They call me Ismark the Lesser. Fair enough. My father was the greater, and look where it got him."
- "I'd die for her. I'd prefer to do something more useful first, like get her over the hills alive."
