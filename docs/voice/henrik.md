# Henrik van der Voort — voice bible

Human coffin maker. NPC `henrik` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Henrik`. Region: Vallaki.

## Who he is

A thin, sweating craftsman whose storeroom holds crates delivered by a black carriage with no driver, and a voice in
his head that told him to buy the saint's bones. The things in the crates are vampire spawn. He keeps his back to the
storeroom door and his crucifixes in triplicate.

- **Wants:** to be left alone. To sleep in a bed like a person.
- **Fears:** the crates, the voice, and being overheard: "they hear everything through wood".
- **Secret:** he wrapped the saint in an altar cloth because it seemed right.

## How he talks

Stammering, over-explaining, contradicting himself ("We're closed. We're open."). Whispers when he talks about them.
Once freed, his gratitude comes out as the only gift he knows: a free coffin, which sounds like a threat.

## Sample lines

- "A coffin? I have coffins. Many sizes. Mostly the small ones, this year."
- "They sleep in the day. At night they go out."
- "That's not a threat. I realise it sounds like one."
