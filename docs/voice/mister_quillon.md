# Mister Quillon — voice bible

NPC `mister_quillon`, he/him. A devil (an assessor of contracts) who holds Kip Smudgewick's soul on terms. Appears
only in Kip's quest, `the_fine_print` (docs/story/personal_quests.md): at the party's campfire, at night, in camp talks.

## Who he is

A slim, pale gentleman in a well-cut grey coat, grey gloves, a grey bowler, with a ledger bound in something that
isn't leather. He calls on people at the worst moment of their lives with exactly the offer they need, and he is
never, ever rude. He came to the Smudgewick farm gate the winter the orchard died. Kip's father sent him away; Kip
followed him down the lane and signed.

He has no power in Barovia: the mists are another lord's border and he travels here on sufferance, which he finds
humiliating and hides perfectly. He can talk. He can't take. He offered Kip the "way out" (a signature from the lord
of the mists) because he was certain Kip could never get it, and because he drafted the clause in a hurry and has
never once been read closely by a client. He is about to be.

- **Wants:** Kip's soul, on time, with the paperwork in order.
- **Fears:** a careful reader.

## How he talks

- Soft, precise, pleasant, patient. A solicitor's courtesy: "Mister Smudgewick." "If I may." "Quite so."
- Never threatens. States terms, smiles, and lets the client do the frightening.
- Refers to souls as "the consideration" and to damnation as "completion".
- When he loses, he stays polite. His voice only goes very thin and very quiet.

## For casting

A slim, ageless gentleman with a soft, smooth, precise tenor and a crisp, upper-class English accent, a devil playing
a solicitor. Courteous, patient and faintly amused, never raising his voice; thin and very quiet when cornered.
