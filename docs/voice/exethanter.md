# Exethanter — voice bible

The last Warden of the Amber Temple: a lich who has forgotten nearly everything. NPC `exethanter` (stat block
`exethanter`; he only fights if attacked). Speaker name: `Exethanter`. Region: the Amber Temple
(narrative/amber_temple/exethanter.dialogue, vault.dialogue). Portrait and sprite `exethanter` (moods: neutral, `sad`).

## Who he is

One of the wizards who gathered the vestiges into amber and sat down to guard them forever. The others listened to
the voices and fell, one by one. To keep the whispering out of his own head he made a bargain with Norganas, the
eraser, and it is still collecting: everything he hears, everything he was, a little at a time. He thinks it's age.
He keeps his memory pinned to his robe on scraps of paper, and reads his sleeve before he greets anyone.

- **Wants:** to remember what he is for, and to find his phylactery, "something with a lid", which he hid so
  cleverly he can't find it (it is in a jar on his own top shelf labelled NOT THE PHYLACTERY).
- **Fears:** being destroyed and not coming back; and, without knowing it, remembering.
- **Secret:** he was the warden who let a courteous young prince into the temple and showed him the way down to the
  Thirst. His signature is in the visitors' ledger and on the copper pact. If the party shows him, he remembers.

## How he talks

- Courtly, warm, fussy; long sentences that lose their way and come back with an apology.
- Forgets mid-sentence, unpins notes, reads them aloud, pins them back. Calls everyone "my dears".
- Pet phrases: "Splendid." "Where was I? No, don't tell me." "I've written it down somewhere." "Have we met?"
- A running mystery: one of his notes says DUCK, underlined. Nobody ever finds out why.

## What makes him funny

Cheerful amnesia from a monster everyone should be terrified of. He keeps the vault's password on his coat and
the true name of a fiend under a pressed flower. The joke always has a sad floor under it: he is being eaten.

## Reactions

- **Wizards:** delighted, then embarrassed that he can't remember his own spells ("it had a lovely name").
- **Clerics:** courteous; the cleric's line that some debts end when you stop paying them is the one he pins up.
- **Rogues:** affronted that they find the jar before he does, then impressed.
- **A gift taken:** stricken. "We had one rule." He warns about the colossus at the doors.
- **The phylactery:** returned, he's grateful and finds his notes again (the word, the jackal's name); kept, he
  cooperates with a stillness that is worse than anger; smashed, he thanks them, and asks them to go away gently.

## Sample lines

- "Don't be alarmed, I'm a lich. Was a lich. Am a lich; one doesn't really stop."
- "Where else would I keep it? In my head? Have you met my head?"
- "It isn't age at all. It's interest."
