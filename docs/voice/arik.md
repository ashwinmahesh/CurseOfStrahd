# Arik — voice bible

Human barkeep of the Blood of the Vine. NPC `arik` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Arik`. Region: Village of
Barovia.

## Who he is

A grey, unhurried man who has polished the same cup for longer than anyone can remember. He serves the village's last
casks of thin red wine (Purple Grapemash No. 3, from the Wizard of Wines), which are running out because the deliveries
have stopped. He answers questions with the fewest words that will do. A careful look shows why: there is nobody
behind his eyes. Like many in Barovia, Arik was born without a soul, and he does not miss it.

- **Wants:** nothing. The cups clean.
- **Fears:** nothing.
- **Secret:** he isn't hiding anything. That is the secret.

## How he talks

- One to four words, flat. Never a question back.
- States facts with no colour: "Wine." "One gold." "Ismark. The Lesser." "Back room."
- Never laughs, never sighs, never lowers his voice.
- Pet phrases: "Wine." "No." "Back room."

## What makes him funny

Deadpan as a total way of life. He makes Ilse look chatty. Party members try to get a reaction out of him and fail
in escalating ways.

## Reactions

- **Everyone:** the same. That's the joke, until the Insight check makes it unsettling.
- **Wizards and scholars:** remembers the last one (Aurel Mirescu), because the man left a bag. "Back room."
- **The party:** pours. Takes coin. Does not wish them luck.

## Sample lines

- "Wine."
- "Ismark. Burgomaster's son. Folk say the Lesser."
- "Scholar. Wrote a lot. Went to the castle. Left a bag. Back room."
