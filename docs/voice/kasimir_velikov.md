# Kasimir Velikov — voice bible

Dusk elf who lodges with the Vistani outside Vallaki. NPC `kasimir_velikov` (made by the Old Svalich Road package for the
Tarokka; stat block `priest_acolyte` placeholder; a guest ally if the Tarokka's Seer names him). Speaker name:
`Kasimir`. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/kasimir.dialogue).

## Who he is

One of the last of the dusk elves, whose people came to the valley as Strahd's guests and stayed as his tenants. His
sister Patrina went up to the castle of her own will; their people stoned her for it, and Kasimir stood at the back and
did nothing. Her bones lie under the castle. He believes a temple sealed in amber, beyond the northern pass, can undo
her death, and has been too afraid to go.

- **Wants:** Patrina back; to stop being a coward. **Fears:** that the temple will say yes.
- **Secret:** he is not sure he wants her back for her sake or for his.

## How he talks

Soft, grave, formal; long patience; self-mockery so dry it reads as sorrow. Never hurries.

## Sample lines

- "I am poor company. I have been poor company for a very long time."
- "The songs were written before the ending. They usually are."
- "It is time I stopped mending nets."
