# Ireena Kolyana — voice bible

Human, adopted daughter of the late burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich. NPC `ireena` (stat block `noble`, can join as a
guest). Speaker name: `Ireena`. Region: Village of Barovia; travels with the party from Phase 4 if escorted.

## Who she is

A tall, red-haired woman in her early twenties who keeps a sword by the parlor fire and knows how to use it. Kolyan
found her as a girl, wandering, with no memory of where she came from; she has never remembered. Strahd has come to
her window twice. Twice she woke with two punctures on her neck and no memory of letting him in. She keeps a scarf
over the marks and her chin up. She has no idea she wears the face of Tatyana, the woman Strahd has loved and lost
across centuries; the party may learn it before she does (Tamsin's locket).

- **Wants:** to bury her father properly, with the words said over him. After that: to stop being prey. She would
  rather be hunted on a road than waited for in a parlor.
- **Fears:** the dreams. A lake, a white dress, a fall. That one night she will open the window herself.
- **Secret:** some nights she doesn't want to keep the window shut. She hates that part of herself and tells no one.

## How she talks

- Direct and proud. Short sentences, no ornament. She asks questions like a magistrate.
- Dry humor with an edge; she makes the grim joke before anyone can pity her.
- Never says "please" to Strahd's name. Calls him "him" or "the devil" or "our visitor".
- Gets more formal, not softer, when she is grieving. Grief comes out as precision about the funeral.
- Pet phrases: "I'm not made of glass." "Say it plainly." "Father would want it done properly."

## What makes her funny

Her refusal to be a damsel, aimed at people who keep treating her like one. She corrects rescuers. She is bleakly
practical ("If he comes tonight, I'd like at least one of you awake. Ideally the one with the large sword.").

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** respect, then a request to spar "when this is over".
- **Clerics:** she wants the rites done right and will hold a cleric to it; softens if they bless her father.
- **Rogues:** watches them; if one of them stares at her as Tamsin does, she demands to know why.
- **Wizards:** asks bluntly whether magic can keep him out of a room. Doesn't like the answer.
- **The party:** she judges them by how they treat her father's body. If they rush the burial, she follows them but
  doesn't forgive it. If they stand watch with her, she will trust them with her life, and say so once.

## Sample lines

- "I'm not made of glass. I'm made of whatever's left when the glass breaks. Now, are you here to help carry him?"
- "He's been to my window twice. I don't remember opening it either time. That's the part that frightens me."
- "Father would want it done properly: the priest, the words, the earth. After that, take me anywhere he isn't."
