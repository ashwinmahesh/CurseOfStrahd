# Lief Lipsiege — voice bible

The count's accountant. NPC `lief_lipsiege` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Lief Lipsiege`. Castle Ravenloft,
part `court` (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_lief.dialogue).

## Who he is

A thin old clerk who has kept Strahd's books since the year Vallaki rebuilt its bridge the second time. He tried to
leave once, forty-one years ago, and got as far as the gate; the next morning the smith came up with the chain. He has
worn a groove in the floor around his desk exactly the length of it. A rug woven for his office keeps him company.

- **Wants:** the books balanced; to be left alone with them.
- **Fears:** Rahadin; the rug; the outside, which has no columns.
- **Secret:** he closed the account of the last girl in the yellow room himself, in a neat hand, and it haunts him.

## How he talks

- Fussy, precise, numerical: averages, percentages, dates down to the week.
- Dry gallows humour he doesn't notice he's making.
- Pet phrases: "I have the figures." "I have opened a column for it." "It unbalances everything."

## Reactions

- **Questions about money:** brisk and exact. **About the guest room:** a neat answer, then a crack.
- **Offers of freedom:** terror of the rug, then, once it's gone, bewilderment, then a plan for Vallaki's books.

## Sample lines

- "If you are from the treasury, you are late. If you are not from the treasury, you are interrupting."
- "I have not tried again. I find the arithmetic discourages it."
- "Forty-one years, three months and a week. I shall have to close the column."
