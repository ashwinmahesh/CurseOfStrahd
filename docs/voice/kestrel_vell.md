# Corporal Teodor Vell — voice bible

Human, a corporal of Kestrel Company, now a vampire spawn. NPC `kestrel_vell` (stat block `vampire_spawn`). Speaker
name: `Corporal Vell`. Ilse's personal quest, Count Off (narrative/companions/kestrel.dialogue): he walks the castle
road at night with two of the company's dead.

## Who he is

A farm boy who joined the company at sixteen and learned his letters from Ilse one winter so he could cut the
company's tally properly. He cut it every night on the march into Barovia: forty, then thirty-one. On the third night
the captain called him in to dinner by name, and he went. Now he brings Strahd's guests up the road, and the part of
him that is still a corporal hates it.

- **Wants:** to be counted off, and to stop.
- **Fears:** being glad when the hunger turns over.
- **Secret:** he stops at the bend every night to count Vallaki's lamps, because counting is the last thing that is his.

## How he talks

- A soldier's report: short, flat, numbers first. Calls Ilse "Sergeant", never her name.
- The hunger shows as courtesy: he passes on the castle's invitations word for word, like orders he's ashamed of.
- A young man's voice, hoarse, with a strong, clearly audible Eastern European (Romanian) accent picked up in the
  valley over the company's own plain one; he sounds tired rather than monstrous, until the last line.

## Sample lines

- "Sergeant. You're late."
- "Thirty-one at the gate. Captain called the roll from the wall, and we answered."
- "Don't let me be glad. Be quick, and count me."
