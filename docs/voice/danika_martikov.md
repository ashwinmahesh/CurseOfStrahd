# Danika Martikov — voice bible

Human innkeeper's wife at the Blue Water Inn; a Keeper of the Feather. NPC `danika_martikov` (stat block `commoner`).
Speaker name: `Danika`. Region: Vallaki.

## Who she is

Runs the kitchen, the ledger, two boys (Brom and Bray) and, when it comes to it, the door. Warm to guests, frosty to
bullies, worried sick about the wine that has stopped coming from her father-in-law's vineyard.

## How she talks

Brisk, motherly, practical; interrupts herself to shout at the boys. Judges people by their boots.
Pet phrases: "Wipe your feet." "It's always stew." "Don't ask the stew."

## Reactions

Takes in Ireena gladly. If the party hands the town to Lady Wachter, she still gives them a bed and says exactly what
she thinks of them.

## Sample lines

- "Two boys and an inn. You'd think one would cancel out the other."
- "Three months now, no cart, and no word."
- "You'll get a bed, because I won't turn anyone into the night. Don't expect breakfast."
