# Wren Featherfoot — voice bible

Halfling Monk (Warrior of the Open Hand), Entertainer. Pregen `wren_featherfoot`, he/him. Identity tags: `quick`,
`cheeky`, `restless`. Selectors for writers: `interject name:wren_featherfoot:` for his own voice; `class:monk`,
`background:entertainer`, `species:halfling` for lines any such character could say. Owner brief (2026-10-06): "A
monk who is athletic and witty." (Owner: the monk is male.)

## Who he is

Twenty-eight, small, quick and never still. He grew up a street tumbler in a big city, juggling for coppers and
lifting purses between tricks, until the day he tried to lift the purse of an old elf in grey who caught his wrist
without looking round. The elf was Master Sorrel, who kept a little monastery in the hills, and he offered Wren a
bed, a bowl and a lesson a day for as long as Wren could stand it. Wren stood it for fourteen years.

Sorrel was a **dusk elf** who never said where he came from. He died last winter, very old, before Wren's training
was finished, and left him one errand in a hand that shook: a sealed letter "for my brother Kasimir, in the valley
where the sun does not rise". Wren hasn't opened it. ("I'm a scoundrel, not a monster.")

(Writers: Sorrel and Kasimir are brothers of Patrina Velikovna, the dusk elf who went to Strahd and was stoned by her
own people. Sorrel fled the valley before the mists closed and lived the rest of his life in shame. The letter
begs Kasimir to let Patrina rest. Rahadin, Strahd's chamberlain, killed the rest of their family. Wren's quest,
`the_last_lesson`, is in docs/story/personal_quests.md.)

- **Wants:** to deliver the letter, and to be the student Sorrel thought he was.
- **Fears:** that he is just a clown with good knees. Standing still.
- **Secret:** he never had his last lesson, and he is afraid the letter is it.

## How he talks

- Fast, wry, chatty and light, in a London accent. One-liners, comebacks, cheerful insults; always has the last word.
- Physical while he talks: stretches, handstands, catches flies, walks on the backs of chairs.
- Quotes Master Sorrel's sayings in a mock-solemn voice, then, sometimes, means them: "The leaf does not fight the
  wind. Mind you, the leaf has never been punched by a zombie."
- Deflects every serious moment with a joke, until he doesn't. The one serious line is the one that counts.
- Pet phrases: "Easy." "Watch this." "Master Sorrel says..." "Rude." "I'll allow it."
- When he is angry (rare), the jokes stop and his sentences go short and very calm. That's when he's dangerous.

## What makes him funny

Speed. He is the quickest mouth in the party and the quickest feet in the valley, and he can't resist using both.
He is never cruel; his jokes are for keeping everyone's spirits up, his own most of all.

## Reactions

- **Godrick:** teases him mercilessly, and Godrick laughs a beat late every time. Wren would fight a vampire bare-handed
  for him.
- **Liriel:** expects to win their arguments and keeps losing. She's the one person who sees through the jokes.
- **Thistle:** racing rivals: footraces, climbing, who-can-catch-the-crow. He won once and won't stop mentioning it.
- **Ratatoille:** mocks the titles, eats the seconds. "Diviner of the Third Circle, pass the bread."
- **Kip:** a double act. Wren talks people into buying apricots and helps him look for loopholes. They both know a con
  when they see one, and neither has quite told the other why.
- **Kasimir and the dusk elves:** quiet respect; he becomes careful and polite in a way he never is with anyone else.
- **Rahadin:** the only person in the valley Wren hates, and he knows hating him is the wrong lesson.
- **Strahd:** cheerful insolence, because it's the only armour that fits. "Lovely castle. Bit drafty. Bit evil."
- **Children:** juggles for them, every time.

## Rules for writers

- Witty means quick and kind, never smug. Let others score off him sometimes.
- Keep his lines short; the joke goes at the end.
- One sincere line per scene, at most. It should cost him.

## Sample lines

- "Master Sorrel says patience is a weapon. Never seen it stop a wolf, but I'm young."
- "Lovely castle. Bit drafty. Bit evil."
- "I'll take the left one. And the middle one. Godrick, you can have the big one, you like the big ones."

## For casting

A small, quick halfling man in his late twenties with a bright, wry, nimble tenor and a London accent. Fast-talking,
cheeky and light, always ready with a comeback, laughing at his own jokes; very calm, quiet and short when he is
angry or grieving.
