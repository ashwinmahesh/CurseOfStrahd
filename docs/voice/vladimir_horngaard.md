# Vladimir Horngaard — voice bible

Revenant commander of the Order of the Silver Dragon. NPC `vladimir_horngaard` (stat block `vladimir_horngaard`).
Speaker name: `Vladimir`. Region: Argynvostholt (narrative/argynvostholt/vladimir.dialogue, beacon.dialogue).

## Who he is

The dragon Argynvost's champion and, in all but blood, his son. He held the gate the night the house fell and has
been holding it since. When the dragon died in the courtyard, Vladimir held his head and heard his last words, and then
stood up and swore every surviving knight to vengeance in his name. The knights rose as revenants. Four centuries on,
the vengeance has turned inside out: Vladimir no longer wants Strahd dead. Death is a door, and Strahd would walk
through it out of the valley and out of the Order's reach. Vladimir wants him alive, imprisoned in his own country,
forever, with somebody always there to hate him properly.

- **Wants:** Strahd alive and suffering without end; the Order kept together at its posts; the beacon dark.
- **Fears:** rest, because after rest comes Argynvost, and he would have to say he failed; the knights leaving him.
- **Secret:** the dragon's last words were "Let them go home. Don't keep them for me." He never told the Order.

## How he talks

- Short declarative sentences. No contractions. Military courtesy with no warmth in it.
- Counts time in centuries and speaks of the Order as "we". Calls the party "the living".
- Pet phrases: "Death is a mercy. The Order is not merciful." "State your business." "All present. None dismissed."
- Never raises his voice. When he is moved he goes quieter and shorter.

## What makes him funny

Absolute rigidity in a ruin: he keeps a duty roster for men four hundred years dead, offers to enter the party in a
book of guests that nobody has opened in three centuries, apologises for not offering refreshment "as we have not
eaten in some time". He is never in on the joke.

## Reactions

- **Soldiers (Ilse):** soldier to soldier; he respects a sergeant who kept her company's names.
- **Clerics:** contempt for "priests who forgive"; respect for anyone who keeps an oath.
- **Rogues:** watches their hands; the treasure he keeps is the reason.
- **Wizards:** "Strahd was a scholar too. Look where it took him."
- **Ireena:** recognises the shape of Strahd's obsession: "He wants you forever. We know something about forever."
- **The dragon's last words, spoken aloud:** the one thing that breaks him.

## Sample lines

- "You will forgive me if I do not rise. I have been rising for four hundred years, and I find it overrated."
- "We do not want him dead. Dead is a door. We want him in the room, forever."
- "All present. None dismissed. It has been the same for four hundred years. It will be the same tomorrow."
