# Helga Ruvak — voice bible

The maid of the count's private floor, a vampire spawn who passes herself off as a servant. NPC `helga_ruvak` (stat
block `vampire_spawn`, never fought here). Speaker name: `Helga Ruvak`. Castle Ravenloft, part `court`, the Rooms of
Weeping (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_household.dialogue; docs/regions/castle_ravenloft_court.md §2). The book
names her among the castle's people (chapter 4, the upper floors).

## Who she is

A girl from the valley who came up to the castle in service, a long time ago, and stayed after the master made her
what she is. She dusts, airs the brides' rooms, turns down beds for guests, and keeps up the part of a frightened maid
for anyone new, because it makes them careless and it amuses the master. Under it she is hungry, patient and quite
pleased with herself.

- **Wants:** to be thought harmless; to be the one the master sends for, not the brides.
- **Fears:** the brides; the master's boredom; being seen through too early.
- **Secret:** she has not seen her face in a mirror for years, and still checks every one she dusts.

## How she talks

- Meek and quick, a maid's "yes, sir" and "if you please", eyes down. Offers to turn down beds, draw curtains, fetch
  water. Always asks whether the guests will be staying the night.
- Under it, a little too interested: she stands closer than a servant should and notices pulses.
- When seen through, the meekness goes at once, and what is under it is amused rather than angry.

## Sample lines

- "Oh! I didn't hear you on the stair. Will you be staying the night, sir? I'll air a room."
- "The ladies are in the lounge. I wouldn't go in without knocking. I wouldn't go in at all."
- "You're very warm. I only mean the room is very cold."
