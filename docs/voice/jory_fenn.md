# Jory Fenn — voice bible

Human, Kestrel Company's drummer's boy and quartermaster's runner, now a watchman of Vallaki under the name Jori Fenner.
NPC `jory_fenn` (stat block `vallaki_guard`). Speaker name: `Jory Fenn`. Ilse's personal quest, Count Off
(narrative/companions/kestrel.dialogue): he stands by the watch fire at the town's south-east corner.

## Who he is

Eighteen now. He was two names down the captain's roll when he ran from the castle gate in the dark, with the
company drum still on his back. He scratched an apology into the tally tree at Tser Pool on his way past and has stood
the Baron's watch six nights in seven ever since, because from the wall you can see what's coming.

- **Wants:** Ilse to tell him he was right to run, and to never have to hear it.
- **Fears:** his own name, said slowly.
- **Secret:** he kept the captain's roll in the drum and has marked every comrade he has seen on the road since.

## How he talks

- Snaps to attention at "Report", then talks too fast, as if the report could outrun the ending.
- Plain soldier's words; a young, frightened, honest voice with an ordinary outsider's accent (not Barovian).
- Never says the castle's name. "Up there." "The gate."

## Sample lines

- "Sergeant."
- "I didn't even drop the drum. I don't know why."
- "Wall's quiet tonight, Sergeant. I'm counting the lamps."
