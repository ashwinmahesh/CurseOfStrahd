# Kiril Stoyanovich — voice bible

Werewolf, leader of the hill pack by the castle's favour. NPC `kiril_stoyanovich` (stat block `kiril_stoyanovich`).
Speaker name: `Kiril`. Region: werewolf den (narrative/werewolf_den/kiril.dialogue).

## Who he is

A huge, bare-chested man in a wolf pelt worn like a cloak, with a grin full of long teeth. He went up to the castle,
came back with its blessing and a new law, and took the pack from Emil. The master wants an army that runs on four
legs; Kiril means to give him one, raised from children stolen off the farms below Krezk. The ones who survive their
first moon will be wolves. The rest will be supper.

- **Wants:** a pack so big nobody in the valley can tell it no. He tells himself that includes the castle.
- **Fears:** being made a dog. He is one, and on some level he knows it.
- **Secret:** the master has promised him the whole valley's children, and Kiril has started to wonder what the
  master does with a hound when he gets bored of it.

## How he talks

- Loud, jovial, brutal. Food words for people ("meat", "supper", "the master's guests, unchewed").
- Calls Strahd "the master" when wolves are listening and "the old man on the hill" when he thinks they aren't.
- Every exchange is a contest; he laughs at his own threats and genuinely enjoys anyone who threatens him back.

## What makes him funny

His host's manners: offering people their lives the way you'd offer a guest a chair, and sounding hurt when they
don't take it.

## Reactions

- **The party:** "the master's guests": they are not to be eaten without leave, which annoys him.
- **Ireena:** delighted. The master will want to hear about this.
- **Emil:** contempt with something old and sore under it.
- **Gold:** a wolf can be bought; a wolf just can't stay bought.

## Sample lines

- "Meat with swords. The master's guests, if I'm not mistaken. He likes his guests unchewed."
- "The old law kept us thin and hiding in the rocks. The new law eats."
- "Sensible meat. I like sensible meat. Now get out of my hills."
