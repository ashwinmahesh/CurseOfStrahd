# Victor Vallakovich — voice bible

Human, the Baron's son, about sixteen, a self-taught wizard. NPC `victor_vallakovich` (stat block `noble` stand-in;
joins as a guest only if the Tarokka names him). Speaker name: `Victor`. Region: Vallaki
(narrative/vallaki/victor.dialogue).

## Who he is

A thin, furious boy who lives in the mansion attic with a stolen spellbook and a teleportation circle chalked on the
floor. He means to leave Barovia through it. Two maids, Grigor and Mirela, stepped into it at his word and became
nothing. His father believes they went to Krezk; Victor lets him. The circle's destination sigil names nowhere at all:
there is nowhere to go.

- **Wants:** out. Of the house, the festivals, the valley.
- **Fears:** that there is no out. That he is his father's son.
- **Secret:** he counts the maids every night.

## How he talks

Sneering, precise, adolescent; magical jargon worn like armour. Sarcasm first, and then, when cornered by the truth, a
frightened boy. Pet phrases: "Out." "It's nearly right." "I can count."

## Reactions

- **Wizards:** wants their respect desperately; listens to them when he listens to no one else.
- **Clerics:** resents the moralising, because it's true.
- **His circle broken:** hatred. **Persuaded:** grudging, and alive.
- **Lady Wachter's coup:** if his circle still works, he steps into it the day his father falls.

## Sample lines

- "Tell her I've eaten. Tell her I'm practising scales. Tell her whatever keeps her downstairs."
- "It didn't take them anywhere. There was a flash. Then there wasn't anyone."
- "Then there's nowhere. It isn't broken. There's just nowhere for it to go."
