# Adrian Martikov — voice bible

Human wereraven, Davian's eldest son, the family's real vintner. NPC `adrian_martikov` (stat block `wereraven`; a
temporary guest for Yester Hill). Speaker name: `Adrian`. Region: Wizard of Wines (narrative/wizard_of_wines/adrian.dialogue,
also yester_hill/approach.dialogue and summit.dialogue as a guest). He was the raven that walked beside the party on the
Svalich road (svalich_road/events:ravens): `raven_fed` / `raven_harmed` were him.

## Who he is

Lean, quiet, sardonic, and better at the work than his father will admit. Last spring a root pinned his younger
brother Elvir in the bracken below Yester Hill; Elvir couldn't change and ordered him to fly for help. Adrian obeyed,
came home alone, and has said only "I flew" ever since, because the rest sounds like an excuse.

- **Wants:** to go back up that hill. His father's eyes on him at supper.
- **Fears:** that his father is right about him.
- **Secret:** Elvir gave the order in their father's voice, and Adrian has never been sure whether he obeyed his
  brother or his father.

## How he talks

- Dry, quick, understated. Jokes at his own expense first, his father's second.
- Craftsman's precision about wine; vague about himself.
- Pet phrases: "Probably." "I'll take sorry." "Father is usually right about houses."

## What makes him funny

Deadpan self-deprecation, and the running grudge with a father he plainly adores.

## Reactions

- **Fed him on the road:** friendly at once ("twice now you haven't shot me").
- **Struck him:** cold; accepts an apology with a joke.
- **Elvir's message:** goes very still; asks the party, not himself, to tell their father.
- **At Elvir's bones (as a guest):** asks to carry him.

## Sample lines

- "Strangers on our lane, upright and armed. Either you're lost or you're the help. Please be the help."
- "Without the stones it's grape juice with ambitions."
- "He was always the stubborn one. A thorn through his leg, and he's still giving orders."
