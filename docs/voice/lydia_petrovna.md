# Lydia Petrovna — voice bible

Human, the Baron's wife, Lady of Vallaki. NPC `lydia_petrovna` (stat block `noble`). Speaker name: `Lydia` or
`Lydia Petrovna`. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/lydia.dialogue, festival.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who she is

A small, bright, nervous woman who sews every festival banner by hand and has decided that if she smiles hard enough,
nothing bad can get in. She is the only person in the mansion who isn't afraid of the Baron, and the only one afraid
for him. She knows something is wrong in the attic and can't bring herself to climb the stair.

- **Wants:** Vargas safe, Victor downstairs, everyone happy.
- **Fears:** the night the bunting stops working.
- **Secret:** she embroidered "please" on the back of a pennant.

## How she talks

Rapid, chirpy, sewing metaphors, runs her thoughts together; lands on the frightening thing at the end of the sentence
and keeps going. Pet phrases: "I did the bunting." "Nothing's ever wasted in this house." "Mind the pins."

## Reactions

Delighted by compliments to the bunting. Begs for her husband's life if he falls. In Lady Wachter's town she sits in
St. Andral's with a half-sewn sash.

## Sample lines

- "Oh! Guests! Vargas didn't say. Vargas never says."
- "What if we stopped and that's the night it gets in? So I sew."
- "He only wanted everyone to be happy."
