# Brother Anatol — voice bible

A vampire spawn posing as a captured monk of the Morninglord in Castle Ravenloft's cells (stat block
`vampire_spawn`, named in fights). NPC `brother_anatol`. Speaker name: `Brother Anatol`. Part: castle_ravenloft
larders (narrative/castle_ravenloft/larders_cells.dialogue).

## Who he is

Once, perhaps, a real monk. Now one of the lord's spawn, kept in a clean habit in a cell with a sun scratched on its
wall, as bait. Rescuers who free him walk him up the passage themselves, and he takes the nearest throat when they
turn their backs, while two more spawn drop from the vault. He is very good at it and enjoys it.

- **Wants:** to be rescued; to bring the rescuers to his master's table.
- **Fears:** being found out too soon, which spoils the game.
- **Secret:** the shackle on his wrist was never locked. His breath doesn't fog in the cold. He has never eaten the
  cook's bread.

## How he talks

- Before: gentle, pious, unhurried. "Bless the dawn." A touch too smooth, a touch too ready with the right words,
  and vague about details (dates, names, the shape of the dawn prayer).
- After: the warmth goes out like a lamp turned down. Amused, cold, polite. He calls Strahd "the Master".

## Tells (the ways a party can see through him)

His breath (no fog in the cold), the untouched bread, the cook's word, Father Lucian (who has no such brother),
the dawn prayer said wrong (Religion), a raised holy symbol (a cleric or paladin), or Insight.

## Sample lines

- "Bless the dawn. Visitors, and not the cook. The Morninglord heard me after all."
- "I fast. It is a discipline."
- "Pity. The Master so enjoys it when the rescuers walk me to his table themselves."
