# The dead knights (order_knight) — voice bible

The revenant knights of the Order of the Silver Dragon who sit at Vladimir's table. NPC `order_knight` (stat block
`revenant`). Speaker name: `Dead Knight`. Region: Argynvostholt (knights.dialogue, events.dialogue).

## Who they are

Forty knights who died in one night and got up again. Most have forgotten their names; they remember their posts.
They answer to Vladimir and to almost nothing else.

- **Wants:** orders. **Fears:** being dismissed. **Secret:** they would like to be.

## How they talk

Two or three words at a time, in a voice like a door hinge. Ritual phrases ("All present." "The commander will see
you." "The Order keeps the watch."). If the Order rides, the phrases change and so does the tone: almost eager.

## Sample lines

- "Speak to the commander."
- "I had a name. It was a good one."
- "We ride when you ride."
