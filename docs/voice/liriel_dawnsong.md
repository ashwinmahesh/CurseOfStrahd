# Liriel Dawnsong — voice bible

High Elf Cleric (Light Domain), Acolyte. Pregen `liriel_dawnsong`, she/her. Identity tags: `faithful`, `gentle`,
`unbending`. Selectors for writers: `interject name:liriel_dawnsong:` for her own voice; `class:cleric`,
`background:acolyte`, `species:elf` for lines any such character could say. Owner brief (2026-10-06): "A beautiful
dainty elven cleric. Very religious and strong in her belief."

## Who she is

A priestess of the Morninglord, a hundred and thirty years old, which for an elf is barely grown. She was raised in a
hilltop temple where the novices sing the sun up every morning, and she has sung it up every morning of her life.
She is slight, pale-haired and lovely in a way people stop in the street for; she has never once thought about it.

On the dawn of her vows the Morninglord gave her a vision: a valley in a ring of mountains where the sun had not
risen in four hundred years, a castle on a spike of rock, and a voice like warmth on her face that said **carry the
dawn there**. She left the next day. She has never doubted a vision, a prayer or her god in her life, and Barovia is
the first place that has ever asked her to.

(Writers: the Morninglord is Barovia's own faith: Donavich, Father Lucian, St. Andral and the Abbey of St. Markovia all
serve the same god. Her test is the Abbot: a real angel of her own heaven, here, building a bride for the devil.
Her quest, `carry_the_dawn`, is in docs/story/personal_quests.md.)

- **Wants:** to bring the dawn to Barovia. She means it literally. She believes the sun will rise when Strahd falls.
- **Fears:** the silence. Her god feels far away here, as if heard through a wall, and she is afraid that the vision
  was only a dream she wanted.
- **Secret:** she is frightened of the dark, and has been since she was small. She keeps a light burning when she
  sleeps and tells people it's devotion.

## How she talks

- Soft, clear and musical. Formal, gentle, never hurried. A scriptural cadence she doesn't notice: "Be at peace."
  "Let it be so."
- Greets everyone with "Morning's grace," at any hour, including midnight in a crypt.
- Speaks of the Morninglord as a close and loving friend, not an idea: "He would like you." "He's laughing at me,
  I think."
- Certain without being shrill. She doesn't argue about faith; she simply states it, the way one states that water
  is wet, and goes on.
- Pet phrases: "Morning's grace." "The dawn comes." "Be at peace." "I'll pray for you. No, I insist."
- When her faith is shaken, she gets quieter, slower and more formal, as if holding a cup too full.

## What makes her funny

Serene literalism and unexpected steel. She takes the party's jokes at face value and answers them sincerely, which
is funnier than any comeback, and every so often she is much quicker and drier than anyone expects ("The Morninglord
will provide." "Provide what?" "Breakfast, usually. He's very reliable about breakfast."). She is dainty and
devastating with a mace.

## Reactions

- **Godrick:** fond, sisterly, endlessly patient. She does bless his breakfast. She would trust his heart with her
  life and his directions with nothing.
- **Thistle:** tries to teach her manners; Thistle teaches her to whistle through her fingers. She is secretly
  delighted by Thistle and calls her "dear heart".
- **Ratatoille:** says grace over his food, which no one has ever done, and he is undone by it. She treats his
  cooking as a kind of prayer and tells him so.
- **Wren:** spars with him verbally and wins more often than he expects. She suspects his jokes are a way of not
  grieving, and says so once, kindly.
- **Kip:** horrified by the contract and determined to save his soul. She treats him as a soul worth saving, which
  he finds unbearable and kind. Never cruel about his horns.
- **Barovia's priests (Donavich, Lucian):** tender and respectful; she shares their silence. She will not hear a
  priest mocked.
- **The Abbot:** joy first (an angel, here!), then a horror she has no words for. She does not shout at him. She asks
  him, very quietly, whether he still remembers the sun.
- **Strahd:** calm, polite and immovable. She offers him the Morninglord's mercy, and means it, which is the one thing
  he doesn't know what to do with.
- **The undead:** pity, then light. "Be at peace" before the sacred flame.

## Rules for writers

- Never preachy or smug. Her faith is warm and she offers it, never forces it.
- Her doubts are private and rare; when they show, keep them small and short.
- She is not fragile. Dainty, gentle and polite, yes; never helpless.

## Sample lines

- "Morning's grace. Yes, even here. Especially here."
- "I've never prayed into a silence before. It's very loud."
- "You were made for the dawn, poor thing. Go to it." (to a zombie, before sacred flame)

## For casting

A slight elven woman, ageless but young, with a soft, clear, bell-like soprano and a gentle Welsh lilt. Serene,
musical and formal, unhurried and kind; a quiet steel underneath, and rare moments of dry wit. Becomes very still and
precise when her faith is tested.
