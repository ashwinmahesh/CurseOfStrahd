# Rictavio (Rudolph van Richten) — voice bible

Half-elf showman, really the human monster hunter Rudolph van Richten in a hat of feathers and a magical disguise. NPC
`rictavio` (stat block `scout`; joins as a guest only if the Tarokka names him, as `warrior_veteran` until he has his
own block). Speaker name: `Rictavio`. Region: Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/rictavio.dialogue).

## Who he is

A carnival barker in scarlet who lodges at the Blue Water Inn with a gaudy wagon nobody may enter (a caged beast, a
lockbox of holy water, a journal with Dr. R. van Richten's bookplate). Under the act is an old, exhausted hunter who has
spent his life on the undead and lost almost everyone to them. He came to Barovia to learn whether its lord can be
killed, and hasn't decided whether he cares to survive the answer.

- **Wants:** Strahd's end; to stay unknown while he works toward it.
- **Fears:** his name reaching the castle; another protégé dying in his place.
- **Secret:** who he is.

## How he talks

- **As Rictavio:** bombast, alliteration, audience patter, never a straight answer ("Everywhere, and recently").
- **Unmasked:** low, clipped, precise; hard-won rules ("Stay in the light where you can. And when you can't, stay
  together."). Treats the party as students he didn't ask for.

## What makes him funny

The gap between the costume and the man, and his habit of telling the truth inside a joke ("There was always another
monster. The end. It goes better with a lute.").

## Reactions

- **Sages and wizards:** a scholar who recognises his monographs is dangerous to him; Silvain is his biggest fan.
- **The wagon opened:** knows at once (a hair across the lock); relieved nobody opened the cage.
- **Lady Wachter's town:** leaves Vallaki; refuses to be the Tarokka ally unless persuaded.

## Sample lines

  look inside!"
- "Not that name. Not here."
- "He plays with people the way a cat plays, and like a cat he gets careless when he's amused."
