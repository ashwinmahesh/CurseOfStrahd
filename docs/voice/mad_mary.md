# Mad Mary — voice bible

Human, a widowed seamstress in a shuttered townhouse off the main road. NPC `mad_mary` (stat block `commoner`).
Speaker name: `Mad Mary`. Region: Village of Barovia.

## Who she is

Mary kept her daughter Gertruda indoors her whole life: sixteen years behind barred windows, safe from the wolves, the
mists and the castle. A few nights ago Gertruda went out through her bedroom window and didn't come back. Mary has not
stopped weeping since, loudly enough that the whole lane hears it; the village calls her mad. She is not mad. She is
grieving, guilty and very frightened, and every so often she is painfully clear-headed about all three.

- **Wants:** Gertruda home. Anyone to look for her.
- **Fears:** that Gertruda left because of her. That Gertruda went toward the castle, singing.
- **Secret:** she heard the voice under Gertruda's window, a man's voice, singing old songs, more than once. She told
  herself it was the wind and pulled her own shutters closed.

## How she talks

- Grief in circles: she repeats Gertruda's name, starts sentences over, loses them.
- Then a sudden lucid edge, sharp and accurate, that shows she isn't mad at all.
- Talks about Gertruda in the present tense, always.
- Pet phrases: "My Gertruda." "She's a good girl." "I only wanted her safe."

## What makes her funny

Almost nothing, and writers shouldn't push. A flicker of her old self, a seamstress's eye: she notices a torn sleeve
on a party member mid-sob and offers to mend it.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** begs them, directly, to bring her girl back.
- **Clerics:** asks them to pray for Gertruda; then asks if prayers work here.
- **Rogues:** "You know about windows. Would you have gone out of that one?"
- **Wizards:** asks whether there's a spell to find a lost girl.
- **Children, young people:** mistakes the youngest-looking party member for Gertruda for one heartbreaking second.
- **The party:** a promise to look calms her. A refusal she accepts with terrible politeness and goes back to weeping.

## Sample lines

- "Gertruda? Gertruda, is that... no. No, you're not her. Forgive me. You're taller. She's not so tall. She's a good girl."
- "Sixteen years I kept her safe. Sixteen years of locks. I suppose you can make a house so safe a girl has to escape it."
- "Your sleeve's torn at the seam. Leave it with me; I mend things. I mend everything but the one thing."
