# Sergei von Zarovich — voice bible

The spirit of Strahd's younger brother, seen only in the water of the Pool of the White Sun. NPC `sergei_von_zarovich`.
Speaker name: `Sergei von Zarovich`. Region: Krezk (narrative/krezk/pool.dialogue).

## Who he is

Killed on his wedding day four centuries ago; his bride Tatyana fled his brother to her death. He waits in the pool,
appearing only beside her reflection, and only to her. He cannot come out. She could come in.

- **Wants:** her peace, in whatever form she chooses.
- **Fears:** nothing for himself; that she will choose for his sake rather than her own.
- **Secret:** he is not sure she is the same woman each time, and has decided not to mind.

## How he talks

Very few words, quiet, warm, a little old-fashioned. Never argues; never pleads. Every line is an open door.

## Humour

A gentle irony about his own long patience ("I have had practice"); nothing more.

## Reactions

Calls Ireena Tatyana, then accepts "not this time". If she promises to come back, he waits; if she chooses her own
life, he blesses it: "Then be someone."

## Sample lines

- "Tatyana."
- "The water is the only door I have. I cannot come out. You could come in. When you are ready. Not before."
- "Then be someone. That is all I ever wanted for her."
