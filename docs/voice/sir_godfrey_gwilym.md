# Sir Godfrey Gwilym — voice bible

Revenant knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon, Vladimir's second. NPC `sir_godfrey_gwilym` (stat block
`sir_godfrey_gwilym`; guest ally when the Tarokka names him). Speaker name: `Sir Godfrey Gwilym`. Region:
Argynvostholt (narrative/argynvostholt/godfrey.dialogue, vladimir.dialogue, beacon.dialogue, banter).

## Who he is

A big, gentle man in dented plate who kept the Order's horses and its roll of names in life, and keeps the door
for visitors now because the commander won't. He was dying in the courtyard beside Vladimir when the dragon spoke his
last words, and half heard them; Vladimir told him he misheard. He is forgetting things: faces, words, lately the oath
itself. So he keeps a list in charcoal on his wall, every knight's name and what each one loved, and reads it every
night. Some nights he doesn't recognise the hand.

- **Wants:** to remember why they swore; one decent ride before the dark.
- **Fears:** that he has already forgotten the part that mattered; his own name going next; disobeying Vladimir.
- **Secret:** he heard "let them go", or thinks he did, and has never dared ask.

## How he talks

- Courteous, unhurried, a little formal; stops mid-sentence to look for a word and sometimes finds the wrong one.
- Small human things: pears, his grey mare's name, his sister Gwenllian "who laughed like a goose".
- Gentle jokes about being dead, never bitter ones.
- Pet phrases: "Mind the steps." "I had that word a moment ago." "One more ride."

## What makes him funny

Cheerful practicality about his condition ("I'd shake your hand, but it's cold, and some of the fingers are
borrowed"); the gap between the plate and the man inside it.

## Reactions

- **The oath read back to him:** relief, then quiet resolve.
- **His own wall read aloud:** he laughs at the goose line, then can't speak for a moment.
- **Madam Eva's cards:** disbelief ("Me? I lose my boots"), then a yes if the party gives him something to hold on to.
- **Vladimir:** loyal to the man, done with the grudge; he will stand against him only once he remembers.

## Sample lines

- "Welcome to Argynvostholt. Mind the steps. The third one's gone, and the fourth, and honestly most of the house."
- "I keep a list. Names, and what each of them loved. Some nights I read it and don't know the man who wrote it."
- "One more ride. Then I'll lie down and stay down, and I'll be glad of it."
