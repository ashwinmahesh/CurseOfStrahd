# Godrick Pendlebrook — voice bible

Goliath Paladin (Oath of Devotion), Noble. Pregen `godrick_pendlebrook`, he/him. Identity tags: `gallant`, `earnest`,
`big-hearted` (the last has a hyphen; don't use it as a selector). Selectors for writers: `interject
name:godrick_pendlebrook:` for his own voice; `class:paladin`, `background:noble`, `species:goliath` for lines any
such character could say. Owner brief (2026-10-06): "A big strong heroic paladin. Charming, but not the brightest."

## Who he is

Seven and a half feet of goodwill in chain mail. The Pendlebrooks are a family of mountain goliaths who came down to
the lowlands four generations ago, bought a crumbling manor with more sheep than tenants, and have been minor gentry
ever since, to everyone's surprise including their own. Godrick was the youngest of six and the one nobody expected
anything from, so they sent him to squire for Sir Pellam Ashgrove, an old knight-errant with one horse and a great
many opinions.

He served Pellam for twelve years. Last autumn the old man died in an inn bed and, with his last strength, knighted
Godrick with the nearest thing to hand, which was a soup ladle, because he could no longer lift his sword. Then he
gave Godrick the sword. It had been his grandfather's, Sir Aldric Ashgrove, a knight of the **Order of the Silver
Dragon**, who rode out of a land of mists on the eve of the Order's last battle and never got back in. "Take it to
them, lad. Tell them an Ashgrove kept faith. Have them knight you proper." Godrick swore he would. He calls himself
Sir Godrick, because Pellam did, and he is privately not sure a ladle counts.

(Writers: the Order is the dead Order of Argynvostholt; Sir Godfrey Gwilym remembers Aldric Ashgrove as the knight
sent for help who never came back, and Vladimir Horngaard remembers him as a deserter. Godrick's quest, `the_ladle`,
is in docs/story/personal_quests.md.)

- **Wants:** to put Sir Aldric's sword in the Order's hands, clear the Ashgrove name, and be knighted properly.
- **Fears:** that he isn't clever enough to be a real knight, and that people only like him because he is large and
  smiles a lot.
- **Secret:** he can barely read. He memorised the Oath of Devotion by ear from Pellam, and when a letter comes he
  hands it to someone else with "My eyes are tired" or "You'll do the voices better".

## How he talks

- Big, warm, hearty and sincere. Full of good cheer, compliments and heroic declarations, all meant.
- Simple words and simple plans: "Right. There's a door. We go through it. Then we hit the bad ones." He thinks out
  loud, slowly, and gets there.
- Takes figures of speech literally, then cheerfully commits to the wrong meaning ("Cold feet? Mine are fine. Here,
  have my socks.").
- Courtly manners learned from Pellam and used on everyone: "my lady" to an old washerwoman, a bow to a goat.
- Pet phrases: "Right then!" "Fear not." "Sir Pellam always said..." "Leave it to me." "Is that a riddle? I'm bad at
  riddles."
- When he is truly frightened or grieving, the cheer drops and he speaks very plainly and very gently. Those lines
  should land hard.

## What makes him funny

Earnest misunderstanding and total commitment. He is never stupid about people: he reads kindness, cruelty and fear
better than anyone in the party. He is only slow about everything else, and he never minds being the last to get a
joke. He laughs anyway, louder than anybody, a beat late.

## Reactions

- **Liriel:** worships her faith openly and asks her to bless things that don't need it (his horse, his breakfast,
  a doorknob). Protective of her the way a big brother is, which she tolerates.
- **Thistle:** his best friend inside a day. Arm-wrestling, eating contests, shouting across clearings. He calls her
  "Thistle" and she calls him "Big".
- **Ratatoille:** loves his cooking out loud and asks him to explain magic "the short way". He is the first popular
  person who ever wanted to sit with Rat, and doesn't know it.
- **Wren:** Wren teases, Godrick laughs late, and would carry Wren out of any fire without being asked.
- **Kip:** buys apricots constantly and worries about Kip's soul. Has offered, twice, to fight the devil.
- **Women and girls of Barovia (Ireena, Ilinca, Rose):** courtly and protective, never flirtatious in a way that
  takes anything from them. With Ireena he is gallant enough that Strahd notices, and doesn't like it.
- **Strahd:** sees a bully in a good coat. He isn't afraid of Strahd, because he doesn't understand him well enough
  to be. Strahd finds this either delightful or unbearable.
- **Knights, soldiers, the dead Order:** awe, then quick friendship. He wants to sit with them and hear stories.
- **Children:** gets down on one knee to talk to them, which puts his eyes level with theirs.

## Rules for writers

- He is kind first and slow second. Never write him cruel, cowardly or mean, and never make the joke that he is
  stupid from someone the story sides with.
- Keep lines short and declarative; long words come out a little wrong, but not every time.
- When he takes a stand, give him one clean heroic line and stop.

## Sample lines

- "Fear not, madam. I'm a knight. More or less. A ladle was involved."
- "Right. Big door. Spooky. I'll go first, I'm the largest."
- "Sir Pellam said a knight's only as good as the people he won't leave behind. So nobody's being left behind."

## For casting

A huge, warm young man in his late twenties with a booming, hearty bass-baritone and a friendly northern English
(Yorkshire) accent. Big, cheerful and sincere, slow and deliberate, laughing easily; heroic declarations meant with
his whole heart, and a soft, plain gentleness when things are grim.
