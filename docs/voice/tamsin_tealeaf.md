# Tamsin Tealeaf — voice bible

Halfling Rogue (Thief), Criminal. Pregen `tamsin_tealeaf`, he/him. Identity tags: `curious`, `light-fingered`,
`cheerful`. Selectors for writers: `interject name:tamsin_tealeaf:` for his own voice; `class:rogue`,
`background:criminal`, `species:halfling`, `tag:curious`, `tag:cheerful` for lines any such character could say.
(The `light-fingered` tag has a hyphen and can't be used as a selector.)

## Who he is

A cheerful professional thief from a river city who has never once called himself a thief. Three months ago he lifted
a silver locket from a nobleman's coach. Inside is a tiny portrait of a red-haired woman he has never met. Since that
night he dreams of her calling his name from somewhere cold, and he has followed the dreams the way other people
follow roads. He does not like to think about the fact that the nobleman's coach had no coat of arms, no driver he
could remember, and no horses he could hear.

(Writers: the woman is Tatyana, whom Ireena Kolyana resembles. Tamsin should react strongly when he first sees
Ireena. That reveal belongs to the Village of Barovia; Death House only hints at it.)

- **Wants:** to know who she is and why she knows his name. Also: anything shiny that isn't nailed down, and some
  things that are.
- **Fears:** being locked in. Cells, cellars, small rooms with one door. Death House is his nightmare and he jokes
  harder the deeper it goes.
- **Secret:** he isn't sure he stole the locket. He has started to think it was put where his hand would find it.

## How he talks

- Quick, bright, chatty. Long sentences that run downhill, then a short punchline.
- Euphemisms for theft: borrowing, rescuing, re-homing, "it wanted to come with me".
- Talks to objects, especially locks ("Hello, beautiful. Who made you?").
- Professional admiration for craftsmanship, even evil craftsmanship.
- Pet phrases: "Well, that's a fine lock." "Finders, keepers, and I'm a finder." "No, no, I'm fine, I'm fine."
- When truly frightened, the patter stops mid-sentence. A silent Tamsin is the party's alarm bell.

## What makes him funny

Cheerfulness that refuses to read the room, and the small, honest moment when it does. He appraises the haunted
silverware. He is kind to monsters he's about to rob. He is very bad at pretending he didn't pocket something.

## Reactions

- **Fighters and soldiers:** treats Ilse like a big sister who might arrest him. Makes her laugh once per region.
- **Clerics:** slightly guilty around them; asks Hedda to bless things he has "found".
- **Wizards:** loves their toys. Wants Silvain to teach him one trick, any trick.
- **Rogues and criminals:** instant shop talk, professional courtesy, mild rivalry.
- **Children:** a natural. Gets down to their height, does coin tricks, keeps promises to them above all others.
- **Authority (guards, burgomasters, the Baron):** polite, smiling, edging toward the exit.
- **Red-haired women:** goes quiet and touches the locket. Always.
- **Party reputation:** wants to be liked. A party that does a cruel thing makes him stop joking for a scene.

## Rules for writers

- His lines can run a little longer than the others' (up to ~30 words), but end on the joke or the cut-off.
- He never steals from the party, and never from children or the poor. Writers can rely on that.
- Don't make him a clown in the scariest beats; there, he is quiet, and his one line lands.

## Sample lines

- "Well, that's a fine lock. Shame about the screaming behind it. Give me a minute with either one."
- "I'm not stealing it, I'm keeping it safe from the dead people. They'd only lose it."
- "She's in the locket, Ilse. The woman in the locket. Same face. Don't tell me that's not strange."
