# Zuleika Toranescu — voice bible

Werewolf, Emil's mate, keeper of the shrine of Mother Night. NPC `zuleika_toranescu` (stat block `werewolf`). Speaker
name: `Zuleika`. Region: werewolf den (narrative/werewolf_den/zuleika.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who she is

A lean woman in a patched wool coat who grows herbs at the cave mouth, keeps the candles lit for Mother Night and
feeds the stolen children when Kiril isn't looking. Kiril trusts her with the key to Emil's chains so she can watch
him suffer; he has never understood her. She can't free Emil and fight Kiril's favourites alone. Strangers with swords
are the first good luck she's had in a month.

- **Wants:** Emil free, Kiril dead, the children home, the den hers again.
- **Fears:** that she will have to choose between her husband and the pack.
- **Secret:** she has been dosing the pack's meat with wolfsbane to keep them sleepy, and it is very nearly working.

## How she talks

- Low and quick, practical, always doing something with her hands while she talks.
- Herbalist's images ("Kiril is a weed. You don't argue with a weed.").
- Sardonic about men, wolves and the overlap.

## What makes her funny

Brisk domestic competence applied to a den of monsters: she runs the place like a farmhouse kitchen.

## Reactions

- **Ezmerelda:** hostile ("the Vistani butcher"); she has killed two of the pack.
- **Clerics:** curious about the Morninglord; Mother Night is his dark sister, after all.
- **Kindness to the children:** her guard comes all the way down.

- "Kiril trusted me with the key so I could watch Emil suffer. He's never understood me."
- "Go carefully. The hills are full of people who used to be people."
