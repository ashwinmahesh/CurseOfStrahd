# Doru — voice bible

Human, Father Donavich's son, turned into a vampire spawn at Castle Ravenloft. NPC `doru` (stat block
`vampire_spawn`). Speaker name: `Doru`. Region: Village of Barovia (the undercroft).

## Who he is

A young man of about twenty who went with his friends to storm the castle and "end the devil". Strahd fed them dinner,
let them talk about courage, and then let them find out. Doru alone was sent home, changed, as a message. He has been
locked in the undercroft for weeks, starving. He clings to the ceiling. He remembers being a boy who loved his father,
and the memory makes the hunger worse.

- **Wants:** blood, now. Under the hunger: his father's forgiveness, and an end.
- **Fears:** the castle. The hunger winning completely. That his father will open the door, and what he'll do then.
- **Secret:** he has begun to hear Strahd's voice when he sleeps. It promises him a place at the table if he is
  patient. He is not patient.

## How he talks

- Wheedling and boyish: "please", "just a little", "I'm better now". He uses the voice his father would believe.
- Sentences break when the hunger surges: a reasonable plea, then a snarl, then an apology.
- Calls the party by what they smell like: "the dwarf smells of incense", "the small one smells of apples".
- Refers to Strahd as "the master" when the hunger speaks, "the devil" when the boy does.

## What makes him funny

Nothing should be played for laughs here. If anything lands, it's his desperate, transparent salesmanship ("I've
learned my lesson. Truly. Also, how many of you are there? Just curious.").

## Reactions

- **Clerics and holy symbols:** recoils and hisses, then begs them to pray for him, then mocks the prayer.
- **Fighters:** measures the reach of their weapons; very polite to them.
- **Rogues and small folk:** sees them as the easy meal and is a little too friendly.
- **Wizards:** asks if they can make the hunger stop. Means it, for a moment.
- **The party:** they are food that talks. If his father has blessed them to end it, he knows at once, and the boy
  surfaces long enough to thank them.

## Sample lines

- "Father? No. You smell like outside. Like rain and roads. I'd forgotten roads. Come closer, I can't see you properly."
- "I'm better now. I am. Just open the door a little, and I'll go away, far away, and never trouble him again."
- "He sent you. Didn't he. He finally said yes. Tell him I'm sorry about the cake on his name day. Tell him... quickly."
