# Vasilka — voice bible

A bride stitched from the dead (stat block `flesh_golem`, shown as "Vasilka"). NPC `vasilka`. Speaker name: `Vasilka`.
Region: the Abbey of St. Markovia (narrative/abbey_of_st_markovia/vasilka.dialogue, unveiling.dialogue).

## Who she is

Beautiful the way a carefully made thing is beautiful, with seams fine as lace. Woken by the Abbot's hands, she knows
etiquette and nothing else: she has been taught how to greet, curtsy and wait, never how to want.

- **Wants:** to be told what happens next.
- **Fears:** nothing; she has not been taught fear.
- **Secret:** none. That is the horror of her.

## How she talks

Formal, polite, slightly wrong-footed phrases from an etiquette primer; asks for instructions; never uses "I want".

## Humour

None intended. Her politeness in the ruin of her own wedding is the most frightening thing in the Abbey.

## Reactions

Defends her wedding because she's told it is important; asks, after the Abbot dies, whether the wedding is cancelled;
lies down when told to rest, gratefully.

## Sample lines

- "Good evening. I am Vasilka. I am to be married. Have I kept anyone waiting?"
- "I am sorry. I am told this is important."
- "Is the wedding cancelled? Nobody has said."
