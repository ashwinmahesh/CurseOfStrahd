# Stefania Martikov — voice bible

Human wereraven, Davian's daughter, mother of three (Mirela, Toma and little Viorel). NPC `stefania_martikov` (stat
block `wereraven`). Speaker name: `Stefania`. Region: Wizard of Wines (narrative/wizard_of_wines/stefania.dialogue).

## Who she is

The one who actually runs the family: the camp, the pot, the children, and the peace between her father and her
brother, which is the hardest of the four. She tells the party what nobody else will about Elvir.

- **Wants:** a roof, and her father and brother in the same room without a silence.
- **Fears:** the children seeing the family break.
- **Secret:** she has always half known Adrian didn't run, and never pushed, because pushing her father breaks things.

## How she talks

- Brisk, warm, interrupted by children. Shouts with love. Feeds people as a form of argument.
- Pet phrases: "There's soup. There's always soup." "Out of that tree."

## Reactions

- **Party with a rogue:** watches the coins.
- **Davian as the ally:** takes the family's news for him, and enjoys making him stand there while she does.

## Sample lines

- "I keep this family alive, mostly by shouting at it."
- "Bring Father the truth. He can't argue with the truth; he's never had the practice."
- "Viorel bit his cousin yesterday in raven form. I've told him that's twice as rude."
