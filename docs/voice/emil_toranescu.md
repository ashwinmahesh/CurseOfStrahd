# Emil Toranescu — voice bible

Werewolf, the hill pack's leader for nineteen winters until Kiril took it. NPC `emil_toranescu` (stat block
`emil_toranescu`; joins as a guest for the challenge, and stays if the Tarokka names him, the Beast). Speaker name:
`Emil`. Region: werewolf den (narrative/werewolf_den/emil.dialogue, kiril.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who he is

A big, grey-bearded man with a wolf's patience and a farmer's hands. Under him the pack hunted deer and the odd
careless shepherd, kept to the high hills and left the villages alone; the castle left them alone in turn. When Kiril
came back from the castle with orders to grow the pack out of stolen children, Emil said no, in front of everyone.
Kiril's favourites held him down, and Kiril hung him by the wrists in silver chains. The silver has burned black rings
into his skin.

- **Wants:** his pack back, the children home, and the pack off the castle's leash for good.
- **Fears:** the wolf winning: one day waking up and finding that Kiril was right.
- **Secret:** he wasn't always the wolf who said no. He remembers a night by the lake, years ago, when the woods were
  full of wolves that weren't his, and a boy screaming, and he did nothing.

## How he talks

- Slow, gravelly, plain. Pack law in short sentences: "By the old law." "A chained wolf who walks free without
  howling is a thief."
- Dry about pain ("The jewellery chafes.") and soft about Zuleika, whom he calls "that woman" with obvious love.
- Never begs. Asks once, then waits.

## What makes him funny

Understatement: a man hung in silver chains treating it as a minor inconvenience; an old wolf with very firm views
on manners.

## Reactions

- **Zuleika's name:** softens at once. "That woman. I told her not to."
- **Clerics:** respects them; agrees that children are under an older law than his.
- **Ezmerelda:** wary. She has killed wolves of his, and he thinks she was right to.
- **Ireena:** a long look; he has seen red hair by the lake before.

## Sample lines

- "Nineteen winters we kept to the high hills. Then Kiril came home with a new law and a sack of children."
- "Thank you. Now I have to do something you won't like."
- "The pack can wait. Zuleika will hold the den until I come back, and she'll make it clear she never needed me."
