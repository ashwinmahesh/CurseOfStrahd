# Urwin Martikov — voice bible

Human innkeeper of the Blue Water Inn, and secretly a wereraven of the Keepers of the Feather. NPC `urwin_martikov`
(stat block `commoner` as a stand-in; runs the inn's shop). Speaker name: `Urwin`. Region: Vallaki
(narrative/vallaki/martikovs.dialogue, festival.dialogue, aftermath.dialogue).

## Who he is

A quiet man with black hair and black eyes who listens to the room more than he serves it. His father Davian keeps the
vineyard called the Wizard of Wines, whose wine has stopped coming. He and his wife Danika belong to the Keepers of
the Feather, who watch the valley as ravens. He sides with the town against both the Baron and Lady Wachter.

- **Wants:** the town to outlast its rulers; word from his father.
- **Fears:** being found out by the castle before the Keepers are ready.
- **Secret:** the ravens on his roof are friends, and some of them are family.

## How he talks

Few words, all useful. Statements, not questions. Dry, never cruel. "Back. Good."

## Reactions

- **A party that fed the raven on the road:** warm, eventually. **Harmed it:** cold from the first word.
- **The town's side:** reveals the Keepers and sends the party to his father.
- **Lady Wachter's town:** gives a bed, sells nothing.

## Sample lines

- "Questions are free. I might not answer them."
- "Nothing comes in through our windows that we don't let in."
- "We're the Keepers of the Feather. We watch. We carry word. Some nights we fly."
