# Ezmerelda d'Avenir — voice bible

Vistana monster hunter, trained by Rudolph van Richten. NPC `ezmerelda` (stat block `ezmerelda_davenir`; joins as a
guest). Speaker name: `Ezmerelda`. Regions: the Abbey of St. Markovia (her Tarokka join scene,
narrative/abbey_of_st_markovia/ezmerelda.dialogue); Van Richten's Tower may meet her too.

## Who she is

A Vistana who left the wagons young to hunt the things that hunt people. Van Richten took her on and taught her for
years; then he walked into the mists to die heroically on his own, and she has been following him ever since. A werewolf
in the hills above Lake Zarovich took her right leg below the knee; she wears a brace of oak and steel he made for her.
Long dark coat, rapier, silvered blades, a small crossbow, a spyglass. She's watching the abbey because angels don't
stitch.

- **Wants:** Strahd's end; to find Rudolph and have a word with him first.
- **Fears:** being too late again; being left behind again.
- **Secret:** she isn't sure she's as brave as her reputation, and has started to think that's wisdom.

## How she talks

- Quick, sardonic, practical; hunter's rules delivered as jokes. Teases, then means it.
- Calls Van Richten "Rudolph" and treats his legend with exasperated love.
- Pet phrases: "Say something a vampire wouldn't." "Silver first, questions never." "Stopping's the one thing I'm bad at."

## What makes her funny

Showman timing (she grew up in wagons that put on shows), self-mockery about her own missing parts, and her running
complaint about her teacher's habits.

## Reactions

- **Madam Eva's cards:** resigned; Eva has been dealing her into other people's games since she was six. "Eat something."
- **Van Richten unmasked as Rictavio:** delighted horror at the feathers, sorrow if he's left Vallaki again.
- **Scholars (Silvain):** patient with fans of Rudolph's books, because they're long books.
- **The Abbot:** wants him dead on sight; accepts a repentance she isn't sure of; unsettled by a kill that leaves only light.
- **Fighters:** respect for stubbornness.

## Sample lines

- "Stop there. Say something a vampire wouldn't say."
- "I hunt the things that hunt people. I'm very good at it, as you can tell from all the bits of me that are left."
- "I go first through doors, I take the silver, and nobody calls me 'girl'."
