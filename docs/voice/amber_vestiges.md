# The vestiges of the Amber Temple — voice bible

Sixteen talking sarcophagi (NPCs `vestige_<name>`, no stat blocks; speakers `vestige_<name>`), the Amber Sentinel
(`amber_sentinel`), and the Thirst, whose amber is empty. Region: the Amber Temple
(narrative/amber_temple/vestiges.dialogue, sentinel.dialogue). Gifts: data/dark_gifts/. Shared portrait
`vestige_amber` (a glow in amber; an art need); the Narrator describes what is in each block.

## What they are

The last embers of dead gods and older things, sealed in amber by the wardens. They cannot move, and they cannot
make anything; they can only offer, and every offer comes from what they were. They are honest about the gift and
nearly honest about the price: each one names its cost if asked, in its own framing, so a player who asks always
knows. The lid inscriptions (History 13) are the wardens' blunt warnings.

## Rules for writers

- Each voice is one idea taken to its end, never a generic tempter. Keep lines short; let the Narrator describe.
- Temptations should land: each vestige finds what a character actually lacks (Hedda's lost warmth, Ilse's dead
  company, Tamsin's woman in the locket, Silvain's friend Aurel, Kasimir's sister).
- Costs are real, mechanical and permanent (data/dark_gifts), and always stated before acceptance.
- No winks, no modern words. The scariest of them (Tarakamedes, Yrrga, Tenebrous, Sykane) aren't funny at all; the
  comedy lives in Taar Haak's five arguing heads, Dahlver-Nar's borrowed voices and Exethanter upstairs.

## The voices

| Vestige | Idea | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Zantras, who crowned beggars | adoration | a theatrical monarch speaking to an audience; flatters the front row |
| Fekre, the mother of fevers | sickness as love | cooing, motherly, "my lamb", "sweetling" |
| Great Taar Haak | war | five heads, capitals, interrupting each other, betting on blood |
| Shami-Amourae, the honeyed hand | pleasure | warm, intimate, "darling"; never raises its voice |
| Yog, the unbroken | endurance | monosyllables through the floor, long pauses |
| Drizlash | the watcher in the walls | skittering repetition: "I see, I see, I see" |
| Khirad, who reads the stars backward | foresight | a weary astronomer, always sorry afterward |
| Norganas, the eraser | forgetting | gentle, trailing off, forgetting its own sentences |
| Savnok, the locked mouth | secrets | almost silence; the box speaks in ellipses |
| Seriach, master of the burning hounds | the hunt | a huntsman's growl, proud of its dogs |
| Sykane, the hollow belly | hunger | whining, gasping, polite until it isn't |
| Tarakamedes, the dragon in the grave | death | slow, enormous, patient; "everyone comes down to me" |
| Tenebrous, the last shadow | the dark | whispers, "shh", hates light as noise |
| Yrrga, the eye that opened outside | terrible truth | clinical pity for the blind |
| Zhudun, the dead star | cold light | grand and lonely; "I was a sun once" |
| Dahlver-Nar, the mouth of borrowed teeth | collection | many voices, repeating words in threes |
| The Amber Sentinel | the wardens' rule | capitals, three or four words: SPEAK THE WORD. NOTHING LEAVES. |

## Sample lines

- Zantras: "Then go back to being furniture. I'll be here when you tire of it."
- Tarakamedes: "Walk away, then. Every road you take goes downhill to me."
- Norganas: "Goodbye. I won't remember you. You'll find it harder to return the favour."
