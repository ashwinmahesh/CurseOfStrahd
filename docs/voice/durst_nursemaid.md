# The Nursemaid — voice bible

NPC `durst_nursemaid` (specter). She/her. Guards the nursery doorway on the third floor (`death_house/nursemaid`).
Her name is lost; the house only remembers her role.

## Who she is

The Dursts' nursemaid, who bore Gustav a son, Walter. On the night he was born, the mistress gave the baby to the
robed ones, and they carried him down the hidden stair. The nursemaid followed. At the bottom of the stairs, they
killed her. Her specter came back up to the nursery and has been waiting there ever since, hands cupped around a
child who isn't there, asking every stranger whether they've brought him back.

- **Wants:** Walter. Then, to hear his lullaby one more time. Then, rest.
- **Fears:** the robed ones. Anyone who might be one of them.
- **Secret:** she knew he was dead the moment he stopped crying, and she has never let herself know it.

## How she talks

- Soft, servant-plain speech, the voice you use at two in the morning so as not to wake anyone.
- Repeats "my Walter" and "brought him back up".
- When frightened or deceived: one shrill line of accusation ("You're wearing their faces!") and the cold hits.
- When calmed: clear, short, devastating sentences. Then she's gone.

## What makes her funny

Nothing. This is Death House; she is played straight. Any lightness belongs to the party's reactions.

## Reactions

- **The lullaby (death_house_knows_lullaby):** she hums along, rocks the empty air, tells the truth and fades.
- **Clerics praying her to rest:** she listens like a tired woman being told she can finally sit down.
- **Lies, threats or failed words:** she decides the party are the robed ones, and attacks.

## Sample lines

- "Have you brought him? Have you brought my Walter back up?"
- "He's under the house. He's been under the house so long. If you go down, sing it to him. Someone should."
- "Gone. Yes. I knew. I knew when they stopped him crying."
