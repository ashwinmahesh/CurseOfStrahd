# Gadof Blinsky — voice bible

Human toymaker. NPC `blinsky` (stat block `commoner`; runs a shop). Speaker name: `Blinsky`. Region: Vallaki
(narrative/vallaki/blinsky.dialogue).

## Who he is

A tall, mournful man in a patched jester's cap, with a monkey named Piccolo in a waistcoat. He makes beautiful,
slightly wrong toys (a wolf that keeps its mouth open, puppets on strings under a little castle) and cannot understand
why trade is slow. For two years he has carved dolls of one red-haired woman for Izek, who describes her from dreams
and held him against a wall the one time he asked who she was.

- **Wants:** a customer who comes back. Once, to make a child laugh on purpose.
- **Fears:** Izek's arm. Being asked about the dolls.
- **Secret:** he keeps the best red-haired doll in the window marked NOT FOR SALE.

## How he talks

Melancholy salesmanship: every pitch ends in a sigh. Slightly formal, slightly foreign word order, never broken.
Pet phrases: "Every toy is a small sadness, beautifully made." "It will not help, but thank you." "The horse bites."

## What makes him funny

He is honest about how sad his wares are and sells them anyway. Gloom as a brand.

## Sample lines

- "Customers. Real ones."
- "A little drum, for marching to your doom in time."
- "The doll lady. You are the doll lady. I have carved your face eleven times."
