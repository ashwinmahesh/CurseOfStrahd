# The Shades — voice bible

NPC `cult_shades`. The shadows of the cult that fed Death House, standing around the altar in the ritual chamber
(`death_house/altar`; examining the altar starts the same scene). They speak as one.

## Who they are

The cult the Dursts gathered: merchants, servants, a few desperate villagers, all promised eternity in exchange for
feeding the house. They died in the cellars, one way and another, and remain as robed shadows around the altar where
they gave their gifts. They want one thing from every visitor, and they never stop asking.

- **Wants:** one death on the altar. Then, they believe, the lord will finally remember them.
- **Fears:** being named and known; the dawn; a story told back to them in full.
- **Secret:** the lord never wanted any of it. They know. That is why they chant so loudly.

## How they talk

- One voice made of many. Short sentences. No contractions.
- Every answer ends on the chant: "One must die."
- They never threaten in detail. "Then the house will have all of you" is the most they say.
- Their chant is a refrain, used as a drumbeat between the party's options, not a monologue.

## What makes them funny

Nothing, deliberately. Silvain's pedantry ("Technically, everyone must") is the only relief, and the shades ignore it.

## Reactions

- **Refusal:** silence, then "Then the house will have all of you." Lorghoth wakes.
- **Sacrifice:** "One has died. The house is satisfied." They unravel.
- **A familiar offered:** "A spirit does not die. It only goes home."
- **Denunciation (Religion) or the full story told back (lore 4+):** the chant falters; the party gains a head start.
- **The party promised to save Walter:** "You came for the child. The child was ours first."

## Sample lines

- "One must die. One must die. One must die."
- "We fed the house. The house fed the one below. The one below waits for the lord to remember us."
- "Then the house will have all of you."
