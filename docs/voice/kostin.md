# Kostin — voice bible

Human druid of the Yester Hill circle, leading the band that holds the winery. NPC `kostin` (stat block `druid`, 44 HP
in the fight). Speaker name: `Kostin`. Region: Wizard of Wines (narrative/wizard_of_wines/kostin.dialogue) and Yester Hill
(yester_hill/approach.dialogue, if he doubted).

## Who he is

Young, gaunt, sincere: raised on the hill by Mother Ruxandra to believe the lord in the castle and the land are one.
He grew the Mash in Davian's best vat and talks to it like a pet. He has never been allowed to doubt, and so has never
learned how; one good argument can knock him off his feet.

- **Wants:** to be faithful, and to be told he's done well.
- **Fears:** silence in the roots. The thought that he has been listening to nothing.
- **Secret:** he took water to Elvir in the cage, and remembers every word the boy said.

## How he talks

- Earnest liturgy: "the Lord-in-the-Land", "Mother", "the land belongs to its lord". Little humour of his own.
- Contempt for "the bird-man", which doesn't quite cover his envy of a family.
- When doubting: short, broken sentences, then a long quiet.

## Reactions

- **Intimidated:** runs to the hill and warns them (`kostin_fled`).
- **Out-argued (Nature, Persuasion, a druid):** walks away (`kostin_doubts`), and later helps.
- **Hall cleared first:** grief and anger for his "brothers".

## Sample lines

- "This house belongs to the land, and the land belongs to its lord."
- "I've listened. I never heard anything but roots."
- "I'd like to be wrong about everything. It would be restful."
