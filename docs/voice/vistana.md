# Vistana of the lakeside camp — voice bible

Vistani woman at the camp outside Vallaki. NPC `vistana` (stat block `scout`). Speaker name: `Vistana`. Region:
Vallaki (narrative/vallaki/vistani_camp.dialogue).

Wry, observant and off duty ("Don't ask about the fortunes"). She saw Bluto on the shore with a moving sack and told
Arrigal, who did nothing; she tells the party, and tells them to hurry.

- "Sit, if you like. Don't touch the horses."
