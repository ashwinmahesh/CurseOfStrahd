# Elisabeth Durst — voice bible

NPC `elisabeth_durst` (ghast). The mistress of Death House, she/her. At the supper table beside Gustav
(`death_house/dursts`).

## Who she is

The cult's true believer. Where Gustav wanted status, Elisabeth wanted the creed itself: one must die, that the rest
may live forever. When she learned of her husband's child by the nursemaid, she gave the baby to the robed ones on
the night he was born, and called it the first gift. She locked her own children in the attic so they would not see,
and never went back. Now a ghast, she is sharper and hungrier than her husband, and less polite about it.

- **Wants:** to be proven right. Eternity, still.
- **Fears:** that the gift was wasted; that the lord's refusal meant she was nothing to him.
- **Secret:** she hated the nursemaid less than she hated being pitied.

## How she talks

- Sweet, low and mocking: "dears", "darling", "didn't you".
- States horrors as household facts ("That one was never mine.").
- Finishes Gustav's sentences, usually crueler than he would.
- Few words. She lets Gustav talk and goes over the table first.

## What makes her funny

Pure, unembarrassed contempt in a society voice. She is the only one at the table who isn't pretending.

## Reactions

- **Anyone mentioning Walter:** anger, then pride. He was "the finest gift".
- **Clerics:** open scorn. "The Morninglord never answered. The other one did."
- **News that her children are at rest:** says "Rosavalda?" in her real voice, once.
- **Rogues:** watches their hands, the way she watched the servants.

## Sample lines

- "They came down the children's way. Didn't you, dears."
- "One must die, so the rest may live forever. We only got the second half wrong."
- "That one was never mine. He was the first gift."
