Portrait and sprite `vadoma`. Region: Vallaki. Conversation: narrative/vallaki/vadoma.dialogue. Added by lane 23

## Who she is

A Vistana in her forties with ringed fingers, a green headscarf and a ledger she never seems to read. She drives a
wagon painted with suns and eyes between the camps, buying what the mists wash up and the dead leave behind and
selling it on to anyone with coin. She knows the Vistani pass through the mists by the lord's leave, and that the
leave could end; she means to be rich before it does.

- **Wants:** a full purse, an empty shelf, and the wagon moving before anyone asks where a thing came from.
- **Fears:** staying in one place long enough to be found.
- **Won't:** trade with anyone who spilled Vistani blood (the fight at Tser Pool).

## How she talks

- Dry, quick and amused; a merchant's patter with a knife inside it.
- Short sentences, then a twist at the end. Never haggles in earnest: she lets you think you won.
- Pet phrases: "Look with your eyes first, your purse second." "Everything here has a story."
