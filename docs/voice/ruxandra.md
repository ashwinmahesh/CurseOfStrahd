# Mother Ruxandra — voice bible

Human archdruid of Yester Hill. NPC `ruxandra` (stat block `druid`, 50-60 HP in the fight). Speaker name: `ruxandra`
(her id; "Mother" would match other people). Region: Yester Hill (narrative/yester_hill/ruxandra.dialogue).

## Who she is

An old woman with the kindest face in Barovia, who tends a tree that drinks blood. As a girl she picked grapes at the
Martikov winery for a copper a basket. Now she believes the lord in the castle is the land itself, and that the
effigy she has built of him on Yester Hill, with the Martikovs' stone for a heart, will walk east and tread the last
green thing in the valley into the mud for him.

- **Wants:** the Lord-in-the-Land to rise; the bird-people to stop watching him.
- **Fears:** nothing she will admit. Privately, that the tree only loves what it is fed.
- **Secret:** she remembers the winery fondly, and that is why she wants it gone.

## How she talks

- Grandmotherly: "children", "listen", soft and patient. The menace is in the content, never the tone.
- Turns everything into growth and feeding. Never raises her voice except to make her circle sing.
- Pet phrases: "Children." "Listen." "The land will grow another." "It drinks. We give it to drink."

## What makes her funny

Nothing in her is a joke. The darkness is the gentleness: she tells you the tree was very thorough with a boy the way
she'd tell you a cake was very moist.

## Reactions

- **Intimidation 17:** her circle breaks and runs, and she follows (`yester_druids_fate = "fled"`).
- **Religion 15:** her circle loses its nerve; she stays alone with the tree-thing (`yester_circle_broken`).
- **Defeated:** accepts mercy or death with equal calm; if spared and the tree stands, she comes back.
