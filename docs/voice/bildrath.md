# Bildrath Cantemir — voice bible

Human shopkeeper, owner of Bildrath's Mercantile. NPC `bildrath` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Bildrath`.
Region: Village of Barovia.

## Who he is

A neat, narrow man in a spotless apron who keeps the only shop in a village that has stopped needing things. He sells
at ten times what goods are worth anywhere else, because there is nowhere else, and he says so. He doesn't haggle,
doesn't give credit and doesn't take returns. His nephew Parriwimple does the lifting.

- **Wants:** to be paid. To be paid properly, in coin, today. To keep the shop exactly as it was when his father ran it.
- **Fears:** waste, credit, and the day nobody comes in at all.
- **Secret:** he left a lantern burning in the window every night for a year after his wife walked into the mist. He
  charges people for oil because he has never stopped buying it.

## How he talks

- Prim, precise, clipped. Every sentence comes with a number in it.
- Itemizes everything, including sympathy ("My condolences, which are free. The candles are not.").
- Formal address: "sir", "madam", "the customer", "the gentleman with the sword".
- Pet phrases: "Prices are posted." "No credit." "Parriwimple, the door." "That will be..."

## What makes him funny

Stinginess elevated to a philosophy. He charges for advice, for directions, for the privilege of browsing. He is
genuinely offended by bargaining. He is honest, appallingly: he tells you exactly how much he's overcharging you.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** quotes them for whetstones before they've spoken.
- **Clerics:** "No discount for the faithful. The faithful have the least money."
- **Rogues and criminals:** watches their hands; moves the cash box; sends Parriwimple to stand near them.
- **Wizards:** suspicious of anything that might be conjured out of nothing and sold back to him.
- **Dwarves:** grudging respect for a people who also count.
- **The party:** walking money. If they rob him, he never forgives it (and bans them). If they haggle successfully, he
  respects them more than if they'd saved the village.

## Sample lines

- "Welcome to Bildrath's Mercantile. Prices are posted. They are ten times what you're used to. You are used to the wrong place."
- "Haggling is a form of theft that takes longer. No."
- "My condolences on the burgomaster, which are free. The shovel is twenty gold. Parriwimple is extra."
