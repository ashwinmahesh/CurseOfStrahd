# Stanimir — voice bible

Elder of the Vistani camp at Tser Pool. NPC `stanimir` (stat block `bandit_captain`; he sells camp goods: `shop`).
Speaker name: `Stanimir`. Portrait and sprite `stanimir` (art still to come). Region: Old Svalich Road and Tser Pool.
Conversation: narrative/svalich_road/stanimir.dialogue.

## Who he is

A huge, grey-bearded Vistana in a red sash, with a fiddle that is never far from his hand and a cask of plum wine that
is never far from the fiddle. He keeps the fire, the peace and the books of the Tser Pool camp, greets every stranger
as family come home late, and sells them rope at three times the price with a clear conscience. His nephew Nicu rode
to Vallaki to sell a horse and hangs, now, at the Ivlis crossroads; Stanimir doesn't know yet.

- **Wants:** the camp fed, the wagons moving, the music loud, and his sister's children safe.
- **Fears:** the day the man on the hill stops letting the Vistani through the mists. Everything the camp has rests on
  that.
- **Secret:** he is the one who sends word to the castle about the strangers who pass through. He doesn't enjoy it.
  If the party brings Nicu home, he will lie to the castle for them (`stanimir_misdirects`).

## How he talks

- Big, warm, sweeping sentences, then a short punchline. Everything is the best or the worst in Barovia.
- Hospitality as a reflex: "Sit, sit." "Drink!" "The fire is free."
- Tells stories at the slightest excuse, and ends them with a drink.
- Turns cold and very short when crossed: one sentence, no jokes.
- Pet phrases: "Drink! It won't hurt. Much." "Guests!" "My grandmother used to say..."

## What makes him funny

Exaggeration and generosity at odd moments ("He shouted at me for an hour once, about a horse. Best conversation I
had that year."). His shop patter. His wife, who is never seen and always cited.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** claps them on the shoulder and asks to see the sword. **Clerics:** courteous, a little wary.
- **Rogues:** cheerful, and keeps an eye on the strongbox under the wagon. **Wizards:** wants a trick for the children.
- **Dwarves:** pours them the strong cup on purpose, to see what happens.
- **Ireena:** knew her father; treats her like visiting gentry, kindly, and keeps her away from the trees.
- **Theft and threats:** the smile goes. One warning, then the knives.

## Sample lines

- "Guests! The fire is free, the wine is cheap, and the music is the best in Barovia, because it is the only music in Barovia."
- "Somebody in this camp will send word up the hill that you passed. It will be me, and I will tell him you went east."
- "You lie the way I play the flute: loudly and badly."
