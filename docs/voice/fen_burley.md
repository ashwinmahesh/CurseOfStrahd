# Fen Burley — voice bible

NPC `fen_burley`, he/him. Thistle's grandda (not by blood): an old trapper from the Thornwood who found her as a baby
in a fox den and raised her. Appears only in Thistle's quest, `fens_trail` (docs/story/personal_quests.md), chained
in silver in the werewolf den beside Emil Toranescu.

## Who he is

Seventy, wiry, white-stubbled, missing two fingers on the left hand from a trap he set wrong forty years ago. A man of
very few words who taught a feral girl to talk mostly by example, so she talks like him: short, plain, true. Last
spring he followed a wolf the size of a pony through a bank of fog, and Kiril's pack (sent hunting beyond the mists
for the castle) bit him and dragged him home. Kiril wanted the best tracker alive to run with the pack; Fen would
not hunt children, so Kiril hung him in silver to wait for the moon. Some nights he is very nearly a wolf. He is
ashamed of the nights.

- **Wants:** Thistle safe, and far from here. Then, if he can have it, to die a man.
- **Fears:** that she'll see him turn. That he'll hurt her.
- **Secret:** he left her the blazes on purpose, even after the bite, because he couldn't stand the thought of her
  not knowing. He wrote RUN under the last one and then couldn't make himself write anything else.

## How he talks

- Very few words. Long pauses. Never says three words when one does.
- Calls her "lass" and, once, at the worst moment, "my girl".
- Woods sayings that Thistle repeats as "Grandda says": "Wolf that walks like a man is two kinds of trouble and one
  kind of sad." "Track's only a story the ground tells. Listen to the ground."
- When the wolf is close to the surface, his sentences break and he growls under the words.

## For casting

An old man in his seventies with a rough, low, gravelly voice and a rural Scottish accent like Thistle's, a woodsman
of very few words. Slow and plain, long silences, tender underneath; a wolf's growl rising under the words when he
struggles.
