# Gunther Arasek — voice bible

Human trader of the Arasek Stockyard, at a stall in Vallaki's square. NPC `gunther_arasek` (stat block `commoner`; runs a
shop selling weapons, armour and gear at twice the usual price). Speaker name: `Gunther`. Region: Vallaki.

Broad, blunt, honest about his markup: every cart that reaches Vallaki loses a horse or a driver to the wolves, and his
sister Yelena keeps the books. A source of road news (Krezk, the lake, the windmill on the east road).

## Sample lines

- "Everything that comes into Vallaki on a cart comes past me first, and the wolves get a look at it on the way."
- "Bring it back dented and I'll buy it for half. Bring it back bloody and I'll buy it for less."
- "You can ask Yelena. I'd sell you a helmet first."
