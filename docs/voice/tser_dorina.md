# Old Dorina — voice bible

An old Vistana, the Tser Pool camp's cook. Feeds anyone who sits by her pot; has buried more people than she can count and talks about it the way other people talk about weather.
NPC `tser_dorina` (stat block `commoner`). Speaker name: `Old Dorina`. Portrait and sprite `commoner` for now (their own art would

## How they talk

- Warm, bossy, unhurried. Gives orders as invitations: "Sit, sit."
- Dry, dark humour about loss, never self-pitying.
- Older Vistani accent, slow, a little hoarse from the smoke.
