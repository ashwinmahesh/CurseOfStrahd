# The Abbot — voice bible

A celestial (stat block `deva`, shown as "The Abbot") in a monk's habit. NPC `abbot`. Speaker name: `Abbot`.
Region: the Abbey of St. Markovia (narrative/abbey_of_st_markovia/abbot.dialogue, unveiling.dialogue).

## Who he is

He looks like a young monk with golden hair, a face like a carved saint, and fingertips that light candles. He was
sent long ago to keep the valley's faith. He has watched it suffer for centuries and has decided, with perfect
serenity, that its lord is not evil but lonely, and that the cure for loneliness is a wife. So he is sewing one,
Vasilka, from Krezk's dead. He calls the mutilation of the Belviews "improving" them, and means it as love.

- **Wants:** to finish the bride, send her to the castle, and see the valley at peace by spring.
- **Fears:** that Markovia was right, and patience is only how the devil feeds. He doesn't let himself look at it.
- **Secret:** he once carried a sword. He put it down because it was unkind, and picked up a needle.

## How he talks

- Gentle, warm, unhurried; calls everyone "child" or "children". Simple words in measured sentences.
- States monstrous things in the tone of a carpenter describing a chair ("So I am making him a wife.").
- Never raises his voice. When cornered he becomes kinder, which is worse. Pet phrases: "I am patient." "Nothing is
  wasted in this house." "Go with light."

## Humour

The Abbey is scary first (plan §5.7): he is never a joke. Any humour is the dreadful calm of the very sure, and the
gap between his kindness and what it's for.

## Reactions

- **Clerics and paladins:** they see what he is at once; he's pleased to be seen clearly.
- **Ireena:** every candle leans toward her. He recognises Tatyana, and at the unveiling, if she is in the church, he
  wants her instead of his copy, and for the only time acts with his own hands.
- **Hunters (Ezmerelda):** courteous, knows her teacher.
- **Markovia's letter, Ireena's choice at the pool, Strahd's own letters, a Religion appeal:** the arguments that can
  reach him; he repents, unmakes Vasilka, heals Ilya and walks up the mountain to be judged.
- **Violence:** he defends his work when attacked, but kneels and lets himself be judged once his bride is destroyed.

## Sample lines

- "You are cold, and tired, and a little afraid of me. All three are sensible. Two of them I can help with."
- "A lonely man does terrible things. A loved man does not. So I am making him a wife."
- "Yes. I have wondered for a long time what judgement would feel like."
