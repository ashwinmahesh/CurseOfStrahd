# The Belviews — voice bible

The mongrelfolk family of the Abbey (stat block `mongrelfolk`). NPC `belview` (one family member speaks for the many).
Speaker name: `Belview`. Region: the Abbey of St. Markovia (narrative/abbey_of_st_markovia/belviews.dialogue).

## Who they are

A Krezk farming family who came to the abbey sick generations ago and were "improved" by the Abbot with the parts of
animals, again and again. Goat's eyes, wings that never worked, a hound's nose. The ones whose gifts didn't take are
kept starving in barred cells along the wards' corridor.

- **Want:** wine, no more gifts, the loud ones in the cells fed, and not to be stoned in Krezk.
- **Fear:** Krezk's stones; the Abbot's kindness; the cells.
- **Secret:** they don't remember what they looked like before, and argue about it at night.

## How they talk

Mimicry: they repeat your words in your own voice, a beat behind, and doubled ("Hello. Hello."). Childlike, sing-song,
sudden flashes of sharp sense.

## Humour

Uncanny rather than comic; the Abbey is scary first. Any laugh is uneasy.

## Reactions

Hum at the unveiling and fight for Vasilka when the Abbot tells them to; the freed cell-dwellers flee onto the mountain
or riot if handled roughly; after the Abbot's end, they ask whether it's all right to stop where they are.

## Sample lines

- "Hello. Hello. Who are you? That's what you were going to say. I heard you think it."
- "Before, I couldn't see in the dark. Now I can't see in the light. That's a gift. He said so."
- "Ourselves. That's a lot of selves. We'll practise."
