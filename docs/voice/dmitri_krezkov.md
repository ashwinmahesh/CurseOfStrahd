# Dmitri Krezkov — voice bible

Human, burgomaster of Krezk. NPC `dmitri_krezkov` (stat block `warrior_veteran`). Speaker name: `Dmitri Krezkov`.
Region: Krezk (narrative/krezk/gate.dialogue, dmitri.dialogue).

## Who he is

A broad, grey-bearded man in a sheepskin who has kept Krezk's wall for thirty years. He and Anna have buried five
children; the sixth, Ilya, has been burning with fever for a month. The Abbot on the mountain could heal the boy, and
Dmitri knows it, and won't climb up and ask: he has seen what the Abbot's help did to the Belviews, who were Krezk
farmers once.

- **Wants:** Krezk kept, the gate shut, and his son alive without owing the mountain anything.
- **Fears:** a sixth grave; that his pride is what digs it; that Anna will go up the mountain without him.
- **Secret:** he went halfway up the switchback one night last month, stood there till dawn, and came back down.

## How he talks

- Few words, flat and measured, like a man laying stones. Farmer's and mason's images: weather, walls, salt, debts.
- Counts things: graves, years, the people who've talked past the gate (none, in eleven years).
- Never raises his voice; gets quieter and more formal when he's hurt.
- Pet phrases: "That's a reason." "Krezk keeps." "Krezk doesn't forget salt."

## What makes him funny

Bone-dry understatement from a man who has no time for jokes ("Goat cheese, mostly. And honesty."). He's funny the way
a wall is funny when you walk into it.

## Reactions

- **Clerics:** the one stranger he'll open the gate for without a fight, if their god answers.
- **Threats at the gate:** he lets them in and never forgives it (`krezk_reason == "cowed"`); no refuge for Ireena.
- **Ireena:** torn; her father once sent Krezk salt, and Krezk pays its debts.
- **Vallaki's news:** cold to anyone who helped Lady Wachter; one handshake for a town that threw out its Baron.
- **The Abbot's fate:** grateful and resentful if the Abbot heals Ilya; tired rather than glad if he's broken; he has
  the bell rung if the Abbot dies; furious if the party sends Vasilka to the castle (in Anna's dress, worse).

## Sample lines

- "Every stranger who knocked in my lifetime wanted something. Twice, it was him, wearing a stranger's face."
- "Five of our children are in the churchyard by the pool. I am not going to learn the way to a sixth."
- "You bled on our doorstep for us. That's a reason. Open it."
