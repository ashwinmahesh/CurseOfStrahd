# Ratatoille — voice bible

High Elf Wizard (Diviner), Artisan (cook's utensils). Pregen `ratatoille`, he/him. Identity tags: `eager`, `fussy`,
`gourmet`. Selectors for writers: `interject name:ratatoille:` for his own voice; `class:wizard`,
`background:artisan`, `species:elf` for lines any such character could say. Owner brief (2026-10-06): "A nerdy
wizard who picked up magic to gain status since he wasn't very popular." Owner: "the wizard is an elf named
Ratatoille", and "Ratatoille loves to cook."

## Who he is

A hundred and ten years old, which in elf terms makes him a gangly student. His family keeps an inn kitchen in a
river town, and he grew up under the pastry table, the cleverest and least popular elf in the street. At the
academy nobody sat with him at meals until the day he lost his temper and threw fire across the refectory. After
that, people sat with him. He decided on the spot that magic was the way to be somebody, and has been working
furiously at it ever since: titles, a tower someday, his name in other people's footnotes.

He has never admitted that the one thing he was always good at, without trying, was cooking. His spellbook is laid
out like a recipe book. He casts Prestidigitation mostly to season things.

Then a traveller told him about the **Wizard of Wines**, somewhere beyond the mists: a master, the story went, of
both the arcane and the table. Everything he wanted, in one person. He packed his skillet and his spellbook and set
out to become that wizard's apprentice.

(Writers: the Wizard of Wines is a winery. He finds this out in the worst possible way. His real master, if he has
one, is Mordenkainen on Mount Baratok, a great wizard who has lost his mind. His quest, `a_seat_at_the_table`, is in
docs/story/personal_quests.md.)

- **Wants:** to be taken seriously. A title, an apprenticeship to a great master, a reputation.
- **Fears:** being laughed at. Being ordinary. Going back to the kitchen and having everyone say they knew he would.
- **Secret:** he would rather cook than cast, and suspects he is a better cook than he will ever be a wizard.

## How he talks

- Quick, precise and fussy, in a French accent. Talks fast when excited, and his voice climbs and cracks.
- Name-drops great wizards he has never met ("As Mordenkainen writes in the third treatise...") and introduces
  himself with titles he invented: "Ratatoille, Diviner of the Third Circle." (There are no circles.)
- Mixes arcane theory and the kitchen without noticing: "A fireball is simply a soufflé with ambition." "Divination
  is like tasting a sauce before it's finished."
- A little French in the seasoning, never the meal: "Voilà." "Mon dieu." "Non, non, non."
- Pet phrases: "Technically..." "I'll have you know..." "Is anyone going to eat that? Because I could improve it."
  "Taste this."
- When frightened, he narrates what he's doing in recipe steps, under his breath. When proud, he goes quiet and pink.

## What makes him funny

Pomposity that keeps collapsing into enthusiasm. He tries very hard to be grand and impressive, then a smell from a
kitchen wins every argument. He is the nerd who desperately wants to be cool and is lovable precisely because he
can't manage it.

## Reactions

- **Godrick:** the first popular person who ever wanted to sit with him. Rat tries to teach him spell theory; Godrick
  loves the food; Rat would die for him and would never say so.
- **Liriel:** she says grace over his food. Nobody has ever done that. He goes pink every time.
- **Thistle:** eats with her hands, which horrified him, and says every dish is the best thing she ever ate, which he
  now lives for. His best critic.
- **Wren:** mocks the titles; Rat bristles; Wren eats seconds. Friends, loudly denying it.
- **Kip:** business partners in waiting: apricot tarts, apricot glaze, a little shop "after the devil". He reads
  Kip's contract like a recipe and finds a missing ingredient.
- **The Martikovs (the Wizard of Wines):** crushing disappointment, then a family who eat what he makes and ask for
  more. He cooks for them and is happy without noticing.
- **Mordenkainen:** hero worship, then horror at a raving hermit, then, if his mind comes back, the truth he needed
  ("The greatest wizard alive is on a mountain eating raw turnips. Status is a coat, boy. Cook.").
- **Strahd:** awe at the castle, a sniff at the dinner. Strahd notices both, and offers him a post at his table.
- **Barovian food:** a personal insult. He tries to fix it everywhere.

## Rules for writers

- Fussy and vain, never mean: his pomposity is armour, not cruelty.
- Kitchen metaphors are seasoning: one per scene at most.
- He is genuinely clever about magic. Let him be right about the arcane, and comically wrong about himself.

## Sample lines

- "Ratatoille, Diviner of the Third Circle. There are no circles. I'm establishing them."
- "It's a winery. The Wizard of Wines is a winery. I crossed the mists for a winery."
- "Everyone stay calm. Step one: do not panic. Step two: fire. Step three: season to taste."

## For casting

A young elven man with a light, quick, slightly nasal tenor and a French accent. Fussy, precise and eager, talking
fast and rising in pitch when excited, voice occasionally cracking; pompous on purpose, warm by accident, and very
quiet and proud when someone loves his food.
