# Madam Eva — voice bible

The seer of the Tser Pool Vistani, who reads the Tarokka. NPC `madam_eva` (no stat block: she never fights; the
camp fights for her). Speaker name: `Madam Eva`. Portrait and sprite `madam_eva` (moods: neutral, `sly`, `sad`).
Region: Old Svalich Road and Tser Pool. Conversation: narrative/svalich_road/madam_eva.dialogue.

## Who she is

An old woman in a patched and painted tent beside Tser Pool, so old that Stanimir's grandmother remembers her as old.
She has been reading the Tarokka for whoever the road brings for longer than anyone in the valley can account for.
The cards, through her, decide where the three lost treasures lie, who will stand with the party, and where the devil
will be waiting at the end. She does not explain how she knew the party was coming. She did.

- **Wants:** the curse ended, and the man on the hill at rest. She will not say so in those words. She wants the party
  to succeed and refuses to be seen caring, because she has sent people up that road before.
- **Fears:** that she is reading for the dead again: that this party will go the way the others went, and she will
  have dealt them the cards that killed them.
- **Secret:** she is far older than a mortal should be, and she is kin to the devil by blood. She knew him "when he
  was young and only ambitious". Writers can hint (her age, her knowledge of the castle's rooms, the sadness when she
  speaks of him); no one in this region learns it outright.

## How she talks

- Short sentences, present tense, flat statements. She does not say "I think"; she says "The cards say", or simply
  says it as fact.
- Blunt about money, death and manners. Bargains like a horse trader and prophesies like a judge.
- Speaks of the cards as if they were difficult relatives: patient, rude, fond of an audience, never wrong.
- Calls women "child" regardless of age, the party "you", Stanimir "my nephew" (he is not, exactly).
- Never names Strahd if she can help it: "him", "the devil", "the man on the hill", "your enemy".
- Pet phrases: "Sit." "Listen." "The cards are patient." "Turn it over." "That will do."
- Verses (data/tarokka/outcomes.json) are riddles in the same voice: concrete images, no rhyme, one or two sentences,
  a place you could find if you knew the valley.

## What makes her funny

Deadpan bluntness from a mystic. She charges for candles, scolds a vampire's victims for posture, and treats the end
of the world as a matter of scheduling. Her jokes never have a wink in them; she doesn't think they are jokes.

## Reactions

- **Fighters:** approves of anyone who listens without fidgeting. Calls Ilse "sergeant".
- **Clerics:** "priestess"; respectful of the Morninglord, honest that his light is far away. Never mocks faith.
- **Rogues:** "little thief", fondly. Knows exactly what is in every pocket in her tent.
- **Wizards:** amused by scholars who want to know how it's done; tells them nothing, kindly.
- **Ireena:** stops shuffling. Speaks to her as to someone she has met before, many times ("You have come round again").
  This is the one person she is gentle with.
- **Payment:** gold is "honest, at least"; a true secret is worth more; a threat gets a laugh and a reading she makes
  you regret; theft gets named aloud.
- **Party reputation:** if the party brought Nicu home (`nicu_kin_told`) she waives the price; if they bloodied the camp,
  she reads anyway, coldly, because the cards don't care who deserves them.

## Rules for writers

- Keep her lines under 30 words. A verse can run to 40.
- She never explains a verse. If the party asks, she repeats it, more slowly.
- She never lies about the cards. She lies freely about herself.

## Sample lines

- "The cards are free. The one who turns them is not."
- "Earth is a door, child. Most doors open both ways, if someone pays the toll."
- "When you meet the devil on the road, as you will, be polite. It confuses him."
