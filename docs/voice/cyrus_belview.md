# Cyrus Belview — voice bible

Mongrelfolk, the cook of Castle Ravenloft (stat block `mongrelfolk`, named in fights). NPC `cyrus_belview`. Speaker
name: `Cyrus Belview`. Part: castle_ravenloft larders (narrative/castle_ravenloft/larders_cyrus.dialogue).

## Who he is

A Belview from the abbey above Krezk, "improved" by the Abbot a piece at a time: a boar's tusk on one side of his
mouth, one long hare's ear, a goat's hoof that clicks on the flagstones. The Abbot sent him to the castle long ago as
a present for a wedding that never happened, and he stayed, because somebody has to cook. He keeps the kitchen fire
lit, the soup on and the bread rising, and he feeds the prisoners in the cells, whom he calls the Master's guests.

- **Wants:** the Master pleased; his cooking praised; news of Clovin and the family.
- **Fears:** the Master's silence; an empty larder; Father (the Abbot) deciding he needs another improvement.
- **Secret:** he laid the bricks over the wine merchant in the cellar, and he is proud of the brickwork. He gives the
  crying girl extra bread so she won't be ripe for the table yet, and he doesn't know why he does it.

## How he talks

- Cheerful, sing-song and servile. Short sentences that bounce. Talks to his pots and his fire.
- Sometimes calls himself "Cyrus" instead of "I", more when he's nervous or proud.
- Kitchen words for everything: guests are "fresh" or "ripe", trouble "boils over", a plan "rises".
- Never says what the Master is. Says what the Master likes.

## What makes him funny

Hospitality applied to the wrong things with total sincerity ("Not that stool. That stool bites."). The horror is in
what he finds normal; play the lines straight and let the listener catch up.

## Reactions

- **Kindness about his family** wins him at once: he presses soup on the party and will help the girl.
- **Money:** he collects shiny things (buttons, spoons, coins). He can be paid. If someone tried words first and
  failed, he wants more.
- **Threats:** he folds, or he screams for the watch. Then he cowers and won't be talked round.
- **His box:** he counts his buttons every night and knows when someone has been in it.

## Sample lines

- "Guests! Nobody told Cyrus there were guests up here. Sit, sit."
- "Everything is for the table, in the end. That's what a kitchen is."
- "A girl fits in a barrel. Cyrus has checked."
