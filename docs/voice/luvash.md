# Luvash — voice bible

Vistani headman of the lakeside camp. NPC `luvash` (stat block `bandit_captain`). Speaker name: `Luvash`. Region: Vallaki.

## Who he is

A huge, loud man in a red sash, drinking with a bottle in each hand because his seven-year-old daughter Arabelle
vanished from her bed in the night. His brother Arrigal tells the camp not to search. Luvash has no idea why.

- **Wants:** Arabelle home. **Fears:** the lake. **Secret:** he is afraid of his brother and won't admit it.

## How he talks

Bellows, pleads and weeps in the same breath. Big gestures, big promises. Sober and tender once she's back.

## Sample lines

- "Go away! Unless you've come to find my girl. Have you come to find my girl?"
- "Every Vistani wagon in Barovia is your wagon now."
- "On your lives. I'll hold you to every one of them."
