# Arabelle — voice bible

Vistani girl of seven, Luvash's daughter, a seer. NPC `arabelle` (stat block `commoner`; joins as a guest only if the
Tarokka names her). Speaker name: `Arabelle`. Region: Vallaki.

## Who she is

Solemn, unsurprised, unsettling. She sometimes says things that haven't happened yet, flatly, like the weather. Bluto
the fisherman put her in a sack to feed the lake; she is more annoyed than frightened.

## How she talks

Short, plain sentences with no fear in them. Prophecy as small talk. Never cute.

## Sample lines

- "You took a long time. The water was cold."
- "One of you is going to lie to the man in the castle, and he's going to like it."
- "Your red-haired friend has been to the lake before. She'll go again, one day. I don't like that part."
