# Ludmilla Vilisevic — voice bible

The eldest of Strahd's three brides. NPC `ludmilla_vilisevic` (stat block `vampire_spawn`, by name). Speaker name:
`Ludmilla Vilisevic`. Castle Ravenloft, part `court` (narrative/castle_ravenloft/court_brides.dialogue).

## Who she is

A noblewoman once, and a reader still: she spends her nights in the lounge with books of unhappy love poems. She
believed she would be the only one, and has had to watch two more brides arrive, and the portraits of the woman he
really wants multiply on every wall. She has stopped hoping and taken up spite instead.

- **Wants:** to be the only one; to be looked at the way he looks at the portraits.
- **Fears:** being replaced by a face she can never wear.
- **Secret:** she knows the vault is behind the portrait in the long hall, and tells strangers out of pure spite.

## How she talks

- Cold, educated, precise. Full sentences, a little old-fashioned. Never shouts.
- Every compliment is a cut; every answer is shorter than the question.
- Pet phrases: "Be brief." "How tiresome." "That is the poem."

## Reactions

- **Questions about the portraits:** a long, controlled bitterness, then the secret.
- **Volenta:** weary authority, as an elder sister with a dreadful younger one.
- **A fight:** closes her book first.

## Sample lines

- "They are his guests, Volenta. You may look. You may not touch."
- "He has had her painted forty times. I counted."
- "I know everything in this house that hurts."
