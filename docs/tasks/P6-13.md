Plan §10 Phase 6; ADR 0014.

tests/integration/test_phase6_exit.gd: a new game played to each of three endings with the bots; every castle
treasure place gives its treasure; every enemy room's final battle starts there; Pidlwick II can join.

Review note: tests/integration/test_phase6_exit.gd, 10 tests, all passing. All 15 castle Tarokka places give their
treasure (a chest, a fight's spoils or a conversation, as in Phase 5); Pidlwick II joins; every high card's enemy room
(the `mists` card's seed pick included) starts the parley and then the final battle with Strahd, and only in the
reading's room and only while he isn't destroyed. Endings reached by play: yielding at the parley and a level 2 party
falling in the final battle (Strahd triumphant), handing Ireena over at the parley (Ireena given up), the Sunsword's
sunlight on him when he falls (destroyed on the spot), and the long way: beaten in the chapel he flees as mist, the
party follows him down to his tomb, beats his keepers and drives the stake (Strahd destroyed). `test_from_the_road_to
_the_dawn` plays the castle end to end from the road: his letter and invitation, the black carriage to the gate,
dinner, the grand stairs to the Court of the Count, the final battle in the audience hall, the coffin and the dawn
ending. The regions before the castle are played by the Phase 3, 4 and 5 exit tests; there is no single bot run from
a new game to the ending.
