Plan §10 Phase 6; ADR 0014.

ADR 0014 (endings): data/endings/ and its schema, the `end_game` statement, the ending screen, the save marked
finished, the final battle's wipe leading to an ending. At least three endings reachable by play (Strahd destroyed,
Strahd triumphant, Ireena given up) and epilogue slides for every region's end-state flags.

## Review note

Five endings in data/endings/ (schema ending.schema.json; contract: docs/contracts/campaign.md "Endings"):
`new_darklord` (40, destroyed + gift:gift_of_the_hollow), `ireena_at_peace` (30, destroyed + ireena_pool_choice
promised), `strahd_destroyed` (20), `ireena_given_up` (15, strahd_parley ireena), `strahd_triumphant` (10, Strahd
stands: a final-battle wipe or yield). Narration in narrative/endings/*.dialogue (lines only, varied by guest:ireena,
guest:ismark, the yield); 80 or so epilogue slides by topic (own slides, then `epilogue_from`, one per topic) read every
region's end-state flags. story/endings.gd picks and records the ending in `campaign_ending`; `end_game` (dialogue_file,
dialogue_runner, dialogue_lint) requests it; game_root shows ui/screens/ending_screen.gd after `end_game`, a
yield/ireena parley, a wipe in a `final_battle` encounter (LocationView.last_encounter) or a won fight that leaves
`strahd_destroyed`; SaveSystem.save_finished marks the slot finished (listed last, "The End: <title>"). Tests:
tests/unit/test_endings.gd, tests/integration/test_ending_screen.gd. Captures: `make capture SCENE=res://scenes/game.tscn
NAME=ending ARGS="--ending=<id>"`.
