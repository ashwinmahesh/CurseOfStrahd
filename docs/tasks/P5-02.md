## Levels 7-9 (2026-10-06)
All 2024 PHB spells of levels 7, 8 and 9 are in data/spells/ (53 new, plus Etherealness and Plane Shift from before),
entered from knowledge without `checked_against`. Recipes in the data: Finger of Death, Fire Storm, Delayed Blast
Fireball, Mordenkainen's Sword, Regenerate (first heal), Conjure Celestial (searing light only), Befuddlement,
Dominate Monster, Earthquake, Holy Aura, Incendiary Cloud, Mind Blank, Power Word Stun (stun branch), Sunburst,
Tsunami (first wave), Antimagic Field (no casting inside), Foresight, Meteor Swarm (one Sphere), Power Word Kill
(damage branch), Storm of Vengeance (first turn), Weird. The rest are text only. For the spell audit, code is still
needed for: HP thresholds (Power Word Kill/Stun, Divine Word), Delayed Blast Fireball's growing bead, Conjure
Celestial's healing light, Power Word Fortify and Mass Heal pools, Power Word Heal, Prismatic Spray/Wall rays, Maze
and Forcecage, Time Stop, True Polymorph/Shapechange/Animal Shapes, Antimagic Field suppression, Regenerate's 1 HP
per turn, Earthquake's fissures and Storm of Vengeance's later turns. The sweep test stops at level 4, so none of
these run in `make test` yet.
