## Levels 7-9 (2026-10-06)
All 2024 PHB spells of levels 7, 8 and 9 are in data/spells/ (53 new, plus Etherealness and Plane Shift from before),
entered from knowledge without `checked_against`. Recipes in the data: Finger of Death, Fire Storm, Delayed Blast
Fireball, Mordenkainen's Sword, Regenerate (first heal), Conjure Celestial (searing light only), Befuddlement,
Dominate Monster, Earthquake, Holy Aura, Incendiary Cloud, Mind Blank, Power Word Stun (stun branch), Sunburst,
Tsunami (first wave), Antimagic Field (no casting inside), Foresight, Meteor Swarm (one Sphere), Power Word Kill
(damage branch), Storm of Vengeance (first turn), Weird. The rest are text only. For the spell audit, code is still
needed for: HP thresholds (Power Word Kill/Stun, Divine Word), Delayed Blast Fireball's growing bead, Conjure
Celestial's healing light, Power Word Fortify and Mass Heal pools, Power Word Heal, Prismatic Spray/Wall rays, Maze
and Forcecage, Time Stop, True Polymorph/Shapechange/Animal Shapes, Antimagic Field suppression, Regenerate's 1 HP
per turn, Earthquake's fissures and Storm of Vengeance's later turns. The sweep test stops at level 4, so none of
these run in `make test` yet.

## Levels 5-6 (2026-10-06)
All 81 2024 PHB spells of levels 5 (47) and 6 (34) are in data/spells/, entered from knowledge without
`checked_against` (no Conjure Volley: it isn't in the 2024 PHB). Recipes in the data: Banishing Smite (damage only),
Bigby's Hand (fist only), Circle of Power (save Advantage), Cloudkill, Cone of Cold, Conjure Elemental, Contagion,
Destructive Wave, Dispel Evil and Good (Disadvantage only), Dominate Person, Flame Strike, Greater Restoration
(Charmed/Petrified), Hold Monster, Insect Plague, Jallarzi's Storm of Radiance, Mass Cure Wounds, Mislead
(invisibility), Steel Wind Strike, Synaptic Static, Telekinesis (first move), Yolande's Regal Presence; Blade Barrier,
Chain Lightning, Circle of Death, Conjure Fey, Disintegrate, Flesh to Stone, Harm, Heal, Mass Suggestion, Otiluke's
Freezing Sphere, Otto's Irresistible Dance, Sunbeam, True Seeing, Wall of Ice (first blast), Wall of Thorns. The rest
are text only. Code still needed for the spell audit: Summon Celestial/Dragon/Fiend and Animate Objects (SummonBlocks),
Swift Quiver (weapon attacks as a sustained action), Eyebite (Self range with a 60 ft target, per-turn Magic action),
Banishing Smite's banishment at 50 HP, Bigby's push/grapple/cover, Telekinesis' later turns, Conjure Elemental's "one
creature at a time" and 4d8 follow-up (it deals 8d8 each failed start-of-turn save), Cloudkill's drift, Contagion's and
Flesh to Stone's three-saves rule, Otto's action to repeat the save, Harm's lower maximum, Heal's +10 per slot,
Sunbeam's sunlight mote, Circle of Power's no damage on a half-damage save, Globe of Invulnerability, Wall of
Force/Stone as blocking walls, Antilife Shell. Zone damage with two types (Jallarzi's) is dealt as the first type.
