== florin_ask
Old Florin [weary]: Tudorel? He went up the castle road two winters back to ask the steward for work.
Old Florin: The roof was leaking through onto the straw, and a boy of sixteen eats like a horse.
Old Florin: People think I come here every morning to pray that he walks back through that door.
Old Florin [sad]: I know what walks down from that hill, so I pray he stays wherever he fell.
Narrator: He clasps his hands together on the pew in front of him, but you can still see them shaking.

== florin_secret
Old Florin [weary]: We had boiled turnip peelings for three days running, and my knees had gone too stiff for the ditching.
Old Florin: I told him the castle pays in silver and never lacks for firewood.
Old Florin [sad]: I have not said that aloud to a living soul, not even the priest.
Old Florin: Do not waste your breath forgiving me for it, because I will not forgive myself.

== tudorel_meet
Narrator: A young footman in good grey-and-black wool pours pale tea into thin porcelain cups without spilling a drop.
Tudorel: Dry your boots by the fender if you like; her ladyship does not mind the steam.
Narrator: He catches you looking at his face, and his hand pauses on the silver pot for just a breath.
Tudorel [weary]: I know what you are thinking, and you may think it, only keep your voices down in the hall.
Tudorel: I have been warm for two winters here, and there is mutton in the kitchen every Tuesday.
Narrator: Fixed neatly to the high collar of his coat is a small silver crest, the raven and crown of the castle.

== tudorel_coin
Narrator: When you mention the old man in the village church, the boy goes quite still.
Tudorel [sad]: I give Old Kolya a silver piece every month when he comes up with the wicker, wrapped in a bit of tea-paper with no name on it.
Tudorel: If my grandfather knew whose coat I was wearing to earn it, he would throw it in the well.
Tudorel [afraid]: Please do not tell him where you saw me, or whose bread I am eating.
Tudorel: Tell him I died clean in the ditch if you have to, or whatever you think will let him sleep.

Old Florin [angry]: The young fool always did care more for a warm belly than a clean name.

== florin_lie
Narrator: Old Florin takes the news like a blow he has been bracing his shoulders against for two years.
Old Florin [sad]: Thank you for coming all this way to tell me plainly.
Old Florin: In my heart I knew it before the first snow melted, though a man still looks at the road.
Old Florin [weary]: It is a great courtesy to know he is at peace and out of the cold.
Old Florin: Would you mind lighting one of the tallow candles by the door on your way out?

== florin_half
Narrator: Old Florin looks at your faces for a long, quiet time without blinking.
Old Florin [weary]: Warm, fed, and in honest service, and you did not think to ask the master's name.
Old Florin: That is either a very good lie or a very careful truth.
Old Florin [sad]: At my age, I do not think I need to pry into which one you brought me.
Old Florin: Thank you for the kindness, travellers, and mind the ruts on the road back.

== florin_after
Narrator: Old Florin sits near the brazier, turning a single silver coin over and over between his crooked fingers.
Old Florin [weary]: Old Kolya left a twist of paper on my windowsill this morning before the mist lifted.
Old Florin [smile]: Strange thing about castle silver: the paper it comes in always smells of good tea.
