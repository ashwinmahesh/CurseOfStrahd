== hearing_intro
Narrator: A small, nervous crowd gathers by the stocks as you lay your business before the Baron in his coat of forty yellow ribbons.
Baron [smile]: A plot! A municipal plot, right here in the square! How marvellously instructive for the townsfolk. Proceed at once.
Narrator: Watchman Dobre stands beside the platform with his thumbs in his belt, wearing the broad, fixed smile required by town ordinance.
Dobre [smile]: Happy to assist the inquiry, my lord. Nobody loves an honest accounting more than I do.
Baron: Quite right, Dobre! Now then, tell me all about this Horatiu Cornel and why he isn't wearing his ribbon.
