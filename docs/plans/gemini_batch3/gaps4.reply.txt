== marta_pay
Goodwife Marta [smile]: Twelve speckled ones, all whole, wrapped in my clean linen so they don't knock together on the cobbles.
Goodwife Marta [afraid]: Carry them like you're carrying the Baron's temper, dear: one crack on the way up the hill and we all hear about it.

== paraschiva_pay
Old Paraschiva: Two gold is fair. Keep the cord knotted around the stopper, or the damp gets in and turns the heather sour.
Old Paraschiva [weary]: And keep to the middle of the road going back. What waits in the ditch has no teeth for honey, only for the hand holding it.

== vadoma_pay
Vadoma [sly]: Into the little painted box it goes, silk and all, so the damp doesn't steal the colour before your cook does.
Vadoma: A dead gentleman leaves behind boots that pinch, a ring that won't come off, and just enough spice to flavour someone else's soup.
