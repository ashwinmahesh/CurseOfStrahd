1B: Narrator: Through the cloudy brine, {name} makes out a grey, finger-long creature curled upon itself like a peeled grub. Its blind end lifts, feelers parting, and swivels through the spirit until it points straight at {name}.
2A: Narrator: The spirit in the jar is clouded thick as tallow, though a grey shape inside shifts—or settles—as your shadow crosses the glass.
2B: Narrator: Nothing shows through the murky brine save a pale smudge the length of a finger, which may only have drifted when the floorboards creaked.
3A: Extracted with silver forceps from the left eye of a shipwrecked sailor in a western port. The man swore a vessel shaped like a squid had fallen burning from the sky with him aboard. Though kept five winters in strong spirit, the specimen is not dead; it turns in the jar to face whoever looks upon it. DO NOT OPEN.
3B: Taken in a far country from behind the eye of a wandering stranger who walked east in a fever. He claimed a great ship with the arms of a squid had dropped out of the heavens and broken upon the hills. Even pickled in spirit, the grey thing pivots by its feelers to watch whichever face approaches the glass. Keep the wax sealed. DO NOT OPEN.
