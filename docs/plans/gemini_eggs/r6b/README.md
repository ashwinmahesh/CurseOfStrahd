## Where the plan and the game differ

- The party's quiet word "under the stars" for a companion at Close or better is left out: it would have stood beside
  the three romances without being one. The night ends instead with someone asleep against Godrick.
- The party's Narrator line names no one, since any companions may be along; each hero or companion present gives a
  toast (nine, plus Godrick's own).
- Ilse has no approval (she is a hero, not one of the six companions `approve` knows), so her talk is not "at Warm or
  better": it comes from level 4, and the plan's approval +1 for saying it back is a laugh instead. The cask's approval
  statement lists the six companions only, for the same reason.
- The night line says "one of your company" (Gemini's own words), since a party may have heroes and no companions.
  It is a `[once and level >= 4]` variant of `camp:night`, the wild's night; a camp indoors or in a town has its own.
