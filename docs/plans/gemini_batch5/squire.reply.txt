== hearing_intro
Narrator: Five dead knights take their places among the small graves, seating themselves on the frost as if a long oak table still stood between them.
Vladimir Horngaard: A petition at the stones has not been called since the first winter of our order. State your business, boy, before the wind takes my patience.
Sir Godfrey Gwilym: You have the right of it, lad. Speak plainly. We have no chairs out here, and the commander's knees were bad even when he had blood in them.
Cosmin [afraid]: I ask the table to hear the matter of Ioan, your last squire, whom your book names a coward. I have his blood, and I say the book lies.
Vladimir Horngaard [weary]: It is my hand in that book, cooper's son. Sit in the snow, if you must, and we will hear what you have brought to prove me a liar.
== hearing_case
Narrator: A heavy ledger rests on Vladimir's armoured knee, its vellum stiff with frost. He does not need to look down to read the line.
Vladimir Horngaard: Ioan, son of the mason. Left his post on the night the hall fell. One word stands beside his name, and I wrote it when the roof was still smoking.
Vladimir Horngaard: Who speaks for a boy four hundred years dead, and what have you brought into the cold besides his great-nephew's stubbornness?
== ev_stone
Narrator: You brush the rime from the back of the last marker and read the cramped, hurried letters chipped into the rough slate.
Vladimir Horngaard: He cut five stones while the vanguard was breaking at the gate. His chisel work was always crooked when he was in a hurry.
Cosmin: He stayed until the others had their names over them. A boy running for his life does not stop to spell five dead friends properly.
== ev_words
Narrator: You repeat the words that lingered in the ruin's cold air. Around the graves, five grey heads lift at once.
Vladimir Horngaard [sad]: Let them go home. He spoke that to the hall before the end, when the smoke was in our throats, and I would not hear it.
Vladimir Horngaard [weary]: A squire would not know our lord's last command unless a knight gave it to him to carry. And I set his name down in the ash for obeying.
Sir Godfrey Gwilym [sad]: He wanted the valley to know we had released them from their tithe. We were very proud, then, and very loud, and only the boy listened.
== ev_godfrey
Sir Godfrey Gwilym [sad]: That was my mare. The grey one with the white pastern. I put the reins in his hand myself at the postern gate, because his own pony was lame.
Sir Godfrey Gwilym: I meant to tell the clerk in the morning, Vladimir. Only by morning there was no clerk, and my head had been split on the stair, and it went from me.
Vladimir Horngaard [weary]: Four centuries at the same table, Godfrey, and you might have mentioned the horse before the boy's kin came freezing to our door.
== history_ok
Narrator: You cite the Order's own rolls: no brother or squire is marked fled unless two witnesses saw his back on the road.
Vladimir Horngaard: You have read our law better than our clerk did. Nobody saw him go, because everyone who looked toward the road that night was already dead.
== history_miss
Narrator: You reach for a precedent in the old rolls, but the names and the winters tangle in the telling, and the knights only stare at you.
Vladimir Horngaard: That was the rule of the third chapter at Berez, stranger, and they were all drowned before my grandfather was born. Keep to what you know.
== persuade_ok
Narrator: You speak quietly of the stone-cutter's boy who rode into the dark alone, and the cooper's boy who has slept three nights on the frost to clear him.
Cosmin: I do not care if I freeze, my lord. Only don't let my father die thinking we came from a man who left his brothers in the fire.
Vladimir Horngaard [sad]: We have kept our anger warm a long time, and it has done nothing for the dead. Perhaps it is time it did some small thing for the living.
== persuade_miss
Narrator: Your plea runs on into the wind, and the dead knights look away toward the dark trees, where speeches have never mended anything.
Vladimir Horngaard [weary]: Spare us the sermon. We know what honour costs, stranger. We are sitting in it.
== ottokar_late
Narrator: Hooves drum without sound across the crusted snow, and a sixth phantom reins in hard beside the graves, his cloak still half-unclasped.
Sir Ottokar the Late [weary]: Did I miss it?
== hearing_won
Narrator: One by one, four grey gauntlets rise from the snow and lay themselves flat upon the empty air where the table would be.
Vladimir Horngaard: Five voices for the squire. Let the word be struck from the roll. Ioan of the stones kept his post, and rode by order of this house.
Narrator: Vladimir rises, the frost cracking from his greaves, and looks down at the shivering boy.
Cosmin [afraid]: Does that mean I may go home and tell my father, my lord?
Sir Godfrey Gwilym [smile]: You may go home and fetch a thicker coat, Squire Cosmin. We have no beds left, and you are no good to the Order with your toes black.
Cosmin [smile]: Yes, Sir Godfrey. I have a coat at the inn. It has three buttons left, if the rats haven't had them.
== hearing_lost
Vladimir Horngaard: A grave and a story are not enough to unwrite the book of this house. The roll stands as it was written.
Sir Godfrey Gwilym [sad]: Wait by the brazier tonight, lad. My memory is a sieve, and it may be that more comes back to me before the dawn.
Cosmin: I can stand the vigil instead, my lord. If you will not hear his name cleared, you can see how his blood holds a sword.
== hearing_book
Narrator: With a dry quill that leaves a faint grey crease in the vellum, Sir Godfrey draws a single straight line through the old word.
Sir Godfrey Gwilym [smile]: Ioan: rode with my lord's last word, on my horse, on my order. And under it, Cosmin: squire. Go on, lad, before I forget how to spell Vallaki.
