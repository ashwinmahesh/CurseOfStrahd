Sir Ottokar the Late: I could not leave an old woman in the rain with night coming on. When I reached the hill, the banners were already down.
Sir Ottokar the Late: I have never told the others, lest they think I offer an excuse. Yet if the wheel broke tomorrow, I would stop again.
