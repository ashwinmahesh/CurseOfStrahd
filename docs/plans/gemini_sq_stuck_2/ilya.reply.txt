A2
Anna Krezkova [weary]: Whatever sits up in that abbey now has its eyes turned toward the castle, not the village. I have stopped leaving the latch off for a blessing from the mountain.

B
Anna Krezkova [neutral]: There will be no sudden grace for him, only the slow way back. He took three spoonfuls of Kasha's barley broth at noon and complained it needed salt, which is prayer enough for this house.
Anna Krezkova [weary]: Nobody is coming down that hill to lay hands on his brow, and we are done waiting on miracles. The White Sun broke the fire in him; I can do the rest with wool blankets and boiled water.
Anna Krezkova [neutral]: (She presses a small linen pouch of forty gold coins into your palm.) Take it and don't argue—Dmitri set it aside for the mason to carve his stone, and I'll not have stone-money sitting in a cupboard while the boy breathes.
Anna Krezkova [smile]: Come back in a month and he'll be pestering you for stories by the well, thin as a switch and twice as stubborn.

C
The water from the Pool of the White Sun broke Ilya's fever, though no priest or Abbot came to lift his weakness at a touch. We left him in Krezk taking broth by the spoonful and gaining his strength back day by day under Anna's hands. Whatever price the valley asks, the boy will live to climb the wall again.
