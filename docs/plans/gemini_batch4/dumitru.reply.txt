== asks
Dumitru: A visitor. Someone who comes up the hill on purpose, just to see Krezk.
Dumitru: I write to my Uncle Bogdan in Vallaki every Sunday. About the snow, mostly, and what the goats broke.
Dumitru: I keep the letters in an old biscuit tin by the gate, so they're ready when a carrier comes.
Dumitru [sad]: The tin is getting rather full. We're on the second layer now.
== no_visitors
Dumitru: Nobody comes up the road unless the wolves chase them, and that doesn't really count as a visit.
Dumitru [weary]: Even the watchman's cousin stopped coming two winters back, and he owed the watchman a shovel.

== cousins_fail

Dumitru: Thank you for trying, though. You did the voices very nicely.
Dumitru: I think I'd rather have a real visitor, even if they don't know my uncle at all.
== fennimore_hint
Dumitru: There's a man in a muddy coat who stands at the crossroads with a little leather book.
Dumitru: The watchman says he gave our wall five stars from the ditch, just for being tall and not falling down.
Dumitru [smile]: Do you think, if someone asked him politely, he might come all the way up to the gate?
== fennimore_ask
Ambrose Fennimore [weary]: Walk up a goat track in the sleet to call upon a child. Yes, that sounds entirely in keeping with my week.
Ambrose Fennimore: Still, my chapter on Krezk currently reads, 'High wall, smells of turnip, declined to enter.'
Ambrose Fennimore: A local correspondent might improve the section on hospitality.
Ambrose Fennimore: Lead on, then. Mind the ruts. Two stars for the road, and that is generous.
== fennimore_visit
Ambrose Fennimore: Master Dumitru, I presume. Ambrose Fennimore, on an official inspection of the upper road.
Dumitru [smile]: You brought your book! Did you see the gate? The iron is very thick.
Ambrose Fennimore: Formidable. Now, I am told you maintain an archive of local dispatches in a tin.
Dumitru: Every Sunday for three years. The ones at the bottom smell a bit like ginger biscuits.
Ambrose Fennimore [smile]: An archive with notes of spice. Four stars for preservation, young man.
== fennimore_rating
Ambrose Fennimore: Allow me to leave a slip of paper for the tin. 'Krezk: Four stars. Cold, steep, excellent reception at the gate.'
Dumitru [smile]: Four stars! Wait until the Burgomaster hears we beat the Blue Water Inn.
Dumitru: I'll put it right under the lid so Uncle Bogdan sees it first.
== done
Dumitru [smile]: Thank you. Having someone walk up the road makes the whole mountain feel less tall.
Narrator: He pats the lid onto the biscuit tin with both mittens, tucking it carefully beneath his arm.
Dumitru: If you ever come back this way, you don't even have to knock. I'll know you.
== after
Dumitru [smile]: Four stars! And he said he might give us a fifth if the sun ever comes out.
Dumitru: I wrote Uncle Bogdan all about it. That was a two-page Sunday.
