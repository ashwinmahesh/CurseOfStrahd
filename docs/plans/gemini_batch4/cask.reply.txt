== silviu_ask
Old Silviu: Going east to Vallaki? You look like you've got the boots for it, and a cart that hasn't lost an axle yet.
Old Silviu: My daughter Dana is getting married the day after next. Noon, in the square, whether the mist lifts or not.
Old Silviu: I made the cask myself. Good oak, tight ash hoops, not a weep in the seams, and I can't leave the yard to bring it.
Old Silviu: Get it to her before the bells, keep it upright, and there's silver waiting for you at the other end.
== silviu_year
Narrator: Old Silviu rests his broad, scarred thumb against the branded mark on the cask head and goes quiet for a moment.
Old Silviu: It's the Red Dragon Crush from the autumn little Elvir was born. Last full barrel of that pressing left in the cellar.
Old Silviu [sad]: Good sun on the south slope that year. The boy never grew old enough to taste it, so it ought to go to a wedding.
== silviu_road
Old Silviu: First, the frost has chewed the Svalich road to pieces. Drop a wheel in a deep rut and you'll bruise the wine.
Old Silviu: Second, there's a grey wolf by the crossroads that knows a slow cart means suet, salt pork, or a limping horse.
Old Silviu: Third is thirsty hands along the way, including your own. Don't pull the bung, not even to smell it.
== ruts_ok
Narrator: You pack dry straw tight around the bilge and lash the hemp rope twice through the iron rings until the barrel cannot budge.
Old Silviu: That'll hold. The cart can rattle your teeth out of your gums now and the lees will stay where they belong.
== ruts_bad
Narrator: The straw is damp and thin, and no matter how hard you haul on the rope, the cask still shifts an inch when the axle groans.
Old Silviu [weary]: Well, my hoops won't spring, at least. Just try to steer round the worst of the stones if you want it clear.
== wolf_ok
Narrator: A bundle of rancid tallow and butcher's scraps goes under the tailboard, ready to toss the moment grey ears show in the brush.
Old Silviu: Smart enough. He's an old beggar, not a hero. Give him a mouthful of fat and he'll let the wheels roll past.
== wolf_bad
Narrator: You find only a dry heel of salt rind and a rusty tin pan to bang, which looks more likely to annoy a wolf than feed one.
Old Silviu [weary]: If he comes trotting out, don't stop to argue with him. Keep the nag moving and hope he's already eaten.
== kip_ok
Narrator: A strip of stamped winery lead goes over the bung, sealed flat with hot tallow where everyone can see a thumbprint.
Kip [sly]: You'd think I carried a gimlet in my boot just for family heirlooms. I only wanted to make sure it wasn't leaking.
== kip_bad
Narrator: While your back is turned, a tiny reed plug goes into the vent-hole, and a dark red drop vanishes onto a very quick thumb.
Kip [sly]: Sound as a church bell, I promise you. Just breathing the cellar dust off the top so the bride doesn't sneeze.
== silviu_go
Old Silviu: Noon the day after next, by the town pump. Look for the girl telling three grown men how to hang a ribbon.
Old Silviu: Tell her the old man's knees kept him home, not his heart. Now get on before the light goes.
== dana_meet
Narrator: In Vallaki's damp square, a bride in a sensible wool shawl is directing two cousins with a hammer while guests huddle near a brazier.
Dana [smile]: Hold the ladder, Toma, don't just look at it! Oh, thank the Morninglord, is that my father's oak on your wagon?
Dana: I knew those hoops the moment you turned the corner. Nobody else in the valley drives ash down that neat.
== cask_good
Narrator: You tap the spigot into the chime, and the wine runs into Dana's cup as dark and clear as garnet glass.
Dana [smile]: Not a speck of cloud in it. It smells like the south hill in September, before the cold comes down from the peaks.
Dana [sad]: He kept the very last of Elvir's year for us. I didn't think he'd ever bring himself to let it out of the dark.
== cask_shaken
Narrator: The wine pours out a little murky from the long jolting on the road, purple and thick in the tin cup, but fragrant still.
Dana: The road gave it a proper thrashing, didn't it? Never mind, let the sediment sit a minute and it'll drink just the same.
Dana [smile]: Better a bruised vintage at the table than an empty cup. My father's oak brought it home, and that's what counts.
== cask_vinegar
Narrator: What trickles from the tap is sharp enough to make your eyes water at arm's length, soured clean through by air and hard travel.
Dana: Oh, sweet saints above, that could strip the rust off a ploughshare! Give me the cup before the groom smells it.
Dana [smile]: Look at the work on these staves, though. He sent us his best barrel, and we're drinking a drop each if it curls our toes.
== speech_good
Master Costel: Friends, neighbours, and damp well-wishers! Observe how even our banners hang straighter in the presence of such a vintage!
Master Costel [smile]: Pressed in a gentler autumn, guarded in the dark, and poured out ruby-bright to warm a Vallaki noon!
Master Costel: Let the bride and groom keep their household as Old Silviu keeps his cellar: tight against the cold, and sweet within!
== speech_shaken
Master Costel: Citizens and wedding guests! Look into your cups and do not ask for crystal, for crystal has never travelled the Svalich road!
Master Costel: This wine has been jolted by ruts, threatened by wolves, and carried through the fog, yet here it stands upon the board!
Master Costel [smile]: So too with marriage in our valley: a little cloudy after a hard mile, perhaps, but sound at the bottom!
== speech_vinegar
Master Costel: Good people of Vallaki, do not wince! Any fool in silk may toast a wedding with sugar and summer plums!
Master Costel: What we hold today is sterner stuff: wine that has met the world, taken the air, and turned as honest and sharp as winter!
Master Costel [smile]: Drink it down without blinking, and remember that a sour draught shared by a warm fire keeps the teeth in your head!
== dana_thanks
Dana [smile]: Take this pouch before my husband spends it on more of Costel's painted cloth. You earned every coin on that road.
Dana: When you pass the winery, tell my father the hoops held tight, and that I saved him a piece of the salt-crust loaf.
== silviu_after
Old Silviu: Costel made a speech, did he? Well, the cask held together, and the girl had her day in the square.
Old Silviu [smile]: Bread's still got the wedding salt on the crust. Pull up that dry keg and sit down a minute, the rain's coming on.
