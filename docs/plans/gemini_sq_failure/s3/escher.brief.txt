THE CHARACTER
Escher is a vampire spawn, once Strahd's favourite companion, now set aside and locked in a suite of velvet and dust high in Castle Ravenloft's towers. He is vain, idle, quick, and furious underneath the drawl. Arch and lazy, every sentence a little bored with itself, then a flash of real anger quickly covered. He calls Strahd "our host" or "he", never by name. He calls the party "darlings" when he wants something. He prices everything. Pet phrases: "Do keep up." "Draw your own conclusions." "I did warn you." Sample lines: "One of Strahd's former. Do keep up." / "He collects people, you see. He just doesn't play with them for very long." / "Do shut the door on your way out. The draught is dreadful, and I can't even die of it."

THE QUEST: Escher's Petition
For nine years Escher has slid the same petition under Strahd's door, one line: "Let me see the sun." Last night an answer came back: Yes. Escher agreed to pay the party, once the sun is up, with the one present Strahd gave him that he could never use (a helm full of captured daylight) if they stand with him on the south tower roof before dawn. (Secretly the rest of the answer was "Bring your friends": a devil called Querreth guards the roofs. The party may or may not know that.) The party said "We'll come." Existing lines around the agreement:
  Escher: Oh, this? My petition. Every night for nine years I've written him the same line in my best hand, Let me see the sun, and slid it under his door. He never answers. He likes to be asked.
  Escher [sly]: And now I find I don't want to go up alone. I'd like it seen by somebody who isn't him. Come up to the south tower roof with me before dawn, and stay until the sun is over the mountains.
  Escher: Before dawn, then. Up the stair from the gallery to the roofs, and along to the south tower. I'll be at the parapet. I'll be early. I've been early for nine years.
  Escher: Before dawn. The south tower. I'll be there before you, in the good coat.
  Escher: I didn't show you that part. I wanted the sun more than I wanted you to come back down again. I'm not proud of it. I'm not proud of very much.
