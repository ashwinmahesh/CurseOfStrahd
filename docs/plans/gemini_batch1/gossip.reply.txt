== nothing_new_1
Vasile [angry]: Again? You asked us that an hour ago. Do we look like men to whom things happen on the hour?
Petre: A crow sat on the well-beam. Then it flew off toward the woods. That was the afternoon.
Vasile [weary]: It was the same crow from this morning. I know its bad foot.
Petre: There you are, then. Come back at dusk if you want to hear about the mud.

== nothing_new_2
Vasile: Well, Old Mihail's spade broke in the frost, if you missed that the first four times.
Petre: That was Tuesday, Vasile. You cannot keep serving Tuesday to guests.
Vasile [sly]: They're from out of the valley. Tuesday is brand new to them.
Petre [weary]: Pay him no mind. Nobody has died since breakfast, and we are all very put out about it.

== nothing_new_3
Vasile: Three whole days without a screaming in the lane. That has to be a record since the fog came.
Petre [afraid]: Don't say it out loud, you fool. The quiet only means it's taking a breath.
Vasile [weary]: Let it breathe, then. My knees ache less when nothing is chasing us.

== wrong_rumour
Vasile [sly]: If you must know, the late burgomaster's old wolfhound has begun to speak. Plain as you or me, just after midnight.
Petre: It has not.
Vasile: One single word, every night, right at the kennel fence. Enough to chill the marrow in your shins.
Petre [weary]: Go on, then. What word does it say, Vasile?
Vasile: It says "Woof." But with a terrible, knowing weight to it.

== petre_coat_told
Petre [sad]: It was the burgomaster's daughter. Mistress Ireena. The first hard frost after my roof gave in, I had only a sack over my shirt.
Petre: She came down the steps with it over her arm and said it was too broad in the shoulder for her brother.
Petre [sad]: A grown man ought not be cold enough to take good wool from a girl's hands. Still, the wind was off the mountain.
Petre: She never looks at it when she passes me in the street. That is the best part of the charity.
Petre [weary]: Keep it to yourself, mind. If Vasile hears, he'll be coughing under her window before dark.
