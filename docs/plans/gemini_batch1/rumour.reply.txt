== offer
Vasile [afraid]: Make a rumour? Out of nothing? A rumour isn't a loaf of bread, stranger. It has to happen to you in the dark.
Petre: The mist happened to us. The wolves happened to us. A fourth rumour happening to us wouldn't be the worst thing.
Vasile: You mean someone tells it to us, and then we have it? Like a coat from a dead man?
Petre [sly]: If it fits, Vasile, it fits. Nobody asks where a dry coat came from.
Narrator: They look at each other for a long time, weighing the terrible responsibility of a fourth thing to say.

== seed_baron_told
Narrator: You lean in and tell them that the Baron of Vallaki is completely bald beneath his wig.
Vasile [afraid]: Bald? Entirely? Like a turnip left in the cellar since the autumn before last?
Petre: Nobody has ever seen the Baron without his hair, Vasile. Not even the Baroness, and she sleeps in the room.
Vasile [sly]: Well, of course nobody has seen it, Petre. That is why he wears the wig. That is the whole point of the thing.

== seed_lupu_told
Vasile: The burgomaster's old wolfhound can count? Up to how many?
Narrator: You tell them that nobody has ever thought to ask the dog how high he goes.
Petre [weary]: A sensible animal. If you let on you can count in this valley, they put you in charge of the taxes.
Vasile: Still. A dog that knows how many legs he has. That is something to think about when the wind comes up.

== seed_tuesday_told
Narrator: You tell them that Bildrath gives a discount on Tuesdays. A long, damp silence settles over the street.
Petre: No living soul in Barovia has ever seen Bildrath take a copper off anything, not even for his own sister's burial.
Vasile [sly]: Well, if anyone had seen it, Petre, it would just be a fact. A fact is no good to us at all.
Petre: True enough. You can't pass a fact back and forth all winter. It wears right through.

== sold_ok
Vasile [smile]: Four rumours. Come tomorrow morning, I will start with the new one, and you can take the afternoon.
Petre [smile]: Fair is fair. We ought to tell old Marta by the pump before the damp gets into her ears.
Narrator: They stand a little straighter in their thin coats, happier than either of them has been in ten years.

== sold_fail
Vasile [sad]: No, no, that won't do at all. It has no weight to it. You can tell when a story was made by hand.
Vasile: Take one of mine instead, if you're short: they say Goodwife Petra's well actually has a bottom to it.
Petre [weary]: Every well has a bottom to it, Vasile. That is what stops the water from falling out of the ground.

== sold_plain
Narrator: You state the rumour plainly, without lowering your voice or looking over your shoulder.
Vasile [weary]: You don't tell it very well, do you? Still, I suppose the shape of it is there, if I squint.
Petre: We half believe you, stranger. Which in Barovia is as much as any rumour gets before it dies of the cold.

== petra_echo_baron
Goodwife Petra: I heard the news from the square, though I keep to my well. They say the Baron of Vallaki has no hair left at all.
Goodwife Petra [sly]: Worse than that, they say he wears two wigs now, and the second one is only there to keep the first one warm.

== petra_echo_lupu
Goodwife Petra: Vasile was by the bucket this morning. He says the burgomaster's old grey hound knows his numbers like a clerk.
Goodwife Petra [afraid]: Every night he sits by the iron gate and counts the dead in the churchyard, and he has never once been wrong.

== petra_echo_tuesday
Goodwife Petra: It has gone all round the lanes that Bildrath is giving a discount on Tuesdays now, though I never thought I'd live to hear it.
Goodwife Petra [weary]: Mind you, Marta says the discount is only on a Tuesday, and only for folk who were christened Tuesday at the font.

== bildrath_tuesday
Bildrath [angry]: I have been informed by three separate paupers since dawn that today is Tuesday.
Bildrath: I have never given a discount on a Tuesday, nor on a Wednesday, nor on the day my own mother went into the ground.
Bildrath: However, it appears the village has decided this is a custom, and I am a man who respects local custom.
Bildrath [weary]: You may have one copper off the tallow candles. Once. As a purely cultural matter.
Bildrath [angry]: If you repeat this to anyone outside that door, I will charge you rent for looking at my walls.

== vasile_thanks
Vasile [smile]: Back already? It went all the way to the church and round by the well before noon.
Petre: Came back to us completely wrong, too. Marta says it happened to my cousin in Vallaki.
Vasile: That is how you know it's a proper rumour, stranger. It comes home wearing somebody else's boots.
Vasile [weary]: Thank you. We didn't have anything to give you back, except the listening, and we've done that.
