== flock_in_one_lost
Erno: Seven, eight, Snowdrop makes nine. There were ten when we came down the hill, before the white got thick.
Narrator: He rests his chin on the top rail of the fold and looks out at the wall of fog, where the road stops.
Erno [sad]: Gran says the mist gives back what it likes. That was the small brown one with the soft ears, so I hope it likes her.
