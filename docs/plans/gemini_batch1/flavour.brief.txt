No modern slang, no pop culture, no real-world references. Funny comes from specifics and deadpan, not from jokes about the horror. Where a scene is sad, let it be sad.

CHARACTERS: Bildrath is the village shopkeeper: neat, narrow, spotless apron, no credit, no returns, charges ten times the going price because there is nowhere else. Iulian is a hollow-eyed man who waits at the door of the windmill for the pastries whose dreams make him happy; in the dreams he is a baker and good at it.
