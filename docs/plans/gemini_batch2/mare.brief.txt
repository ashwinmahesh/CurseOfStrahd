RULES FOR EVERY LINE: under 190 characters, usually 60 to 130; no braces or placeholders; no em dashes (use commas, full stops or colons); no real-world references; straight apostrophes and quotes; each line should make sense on its own. CHARACTERS: Radu is the Tser Pool camp's swaggering horse-trader in a red coat; he sold his grey mare Steaua to a black carriage with no driver because a kind voice in it promised his friend's son Nicu would ride home. She is tied at the crossroads milestone being turned into one of the castle's black horses; the carriage comes at midnight to fetch her. Lavinia is the camp's dancer. The camp's troupe has a pantomime horse for winter plays called the Winter Horse: a cloth-and-straw horse with a long sad face and two people's worth of legs.

THE SCENE: the party's new plan: don't fight the carriage; leave a decoy in Steaua's place, and bring Steaua home. Radu is appalled and delighted by the idea. They borrow the Winter Horse (with Lavinia's blessing, offered through Radu), blacken it with lamp soot, and at the milestone before midnight switch it for Steaua, who is calmed and runs home. The carriage arrives, takes the thing on the cord, and leaves; if the switch is noticed, the carriage's escort comes for the party instead. The mood: tense, then absurd, then relief.
