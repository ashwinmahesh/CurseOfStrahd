== start_again
Costica [sad]: Forgive me, I did not recognise your boots through the mist until I had already stepped from the bushes.
Costica [neutral]: In my trade, however, once one has emerged from the brambles, etiquette demands that one see the robbery through.

== pay

Narrator: He sets his rusty sword carefully in the mud, fishes a stub of charcoal and a damp scrap of parchment from his coat, and writes with great concentration.
Costica [neutral]: Here is your receipt, duly signed, so that no other footpad along the Old Svalich Road may tax you twice on the same afternoon.

Costica [smile]: It is quite fresh, I assure you, and I shall step back into the wet bushes immediately.

== who
Costica [neutral]: I am Costica, highwayman of the Old Svalich Road, at your service.
Costica [sad]: Times are lean in my profession, as very few people travel for pleasure and fewer still carry spare coin.
Costica [neutral]: My cousin Bildrath keeps the mercantile in the village, and he tells me a man must find a trade and stick to it.
Costica [smile]: Everything I manage to plunder, I spend at his counter, though as family he charges me only ten times the usual rate.

== secret
Costica [smile]: You will be pleased to hear that your two gold pieces were put to immediate and practical use.
Costica [neutral]: My cousin Bildrath happened to have a very fine felt hat in his shop window, which he had taken from me last month against my tab.
Costica [sad]: Your generous contribution covered nearly a tenth of what he asked to sell it back to me, so he let me hold it for an hour.
Costica [smile]: It is a comfort to deal with family, where one knows the money stays in good hands.

== walk_past

Costica [sad]: Tell me honestly, as travellers of the world, should I have pitched my voice lower when stepping from the gorse?

== goodbye
Costica [neutral]: Safe travels to you then, and do mind the deep puddle just past the bend.
