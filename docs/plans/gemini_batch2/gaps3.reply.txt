== spell_ok_day
Narrator: Magic pulls at the soles of your boots with a brief, weightless tug, as if the gorge has forgotten how to drop you.
Narrator: A heartbeat later, you stand on the wet stone ledge beside the boy.

== dinte_lead
Narrator: You slip the rope over Dinte's neck. He does not stop you, which with Dinte is the closest thing to permission.
Narrator: You walk him out through the gate with his big yellow teeth kept thoughtfully near your elbow.
