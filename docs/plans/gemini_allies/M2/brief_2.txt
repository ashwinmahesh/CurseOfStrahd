## The feature: Ezmerelda's Hunter's Ledger

Ezmerelda d'Avenir is a young Vistana monster hunter (long dark coat, rapier, a small crossbow with silver-headed bolts, a spyglass, and an oak-and-steel brace below her right knee where a werewolf took her leg). She was trained by Rudolph van Richten, the legendary hunter, who walked into the mists twelve years ago and has been calling himself Rictavio ever since (a loud half-elf showman at the Blue Water Inn in the town of Vallaki). She hunts the things that hunt people, and she is now an ally the player's party can talk to whenever they find her (at the shore of Lake Baratok by Van Richten's tower, on the switchback above the abbey, in the inn's stable yard in Vallaki, or beside them on the road once she has joined).

She keeps THE HUNTER'S LEDGER: a battered book of the valley's killers, each with a price on its head. She pays for proof, in the things a hunter has to give: silver-headed bolts, holy water, healing draughts, scrolls and potions made against the thing that was killed, and later a weapon from her wagon. The game records which of these killers the party has actually killed; the ledger pays for exactly those and nothing else. She names three names at a time: first any she can pay now, then hunts near where the party is, then the hardest hunt the party is ready for. There is no deadline and no penalty: the ledger is a reason to come back and talk to her, not a test. A hunt she briefs the party on is words only; nothing in her briefing forces a fight.

## Ezmerelda d'Avenir - voice bible

Quick, sardonic, practical; hunter's rules delivered as jokes. Teases, then means it. Calls Van Richten "Rudolph" and treats his legend with exasperated love. Pet phrases: "Say something a vampire wouldn't." "Silver first, questions never." "Stopping's the one thing I'm bad at." Funny through showman timing (she grew up in wagons that put on shows), self-mockery about her own missing parts, and her running complaint about her teacher's habits. Wants: the end of the vampire lord in the castle; to find Rudolph and have a word with him first. Fears: being too late again; being left behind again. Secret: she isn't sure she's as brave as her reputation, and has started to think that's wisdom. She never says the vampire lord's name aloud: she says "the count", "the castle", "the devil in the castle" or nothing. She never calls the party "kids" or "girl". She treats the party as capable colleagues.

## The Narrator

An old gothic storyteller, fond of the party the way a gravedigger is fond of the weather. Second person, present tense. One or two sentences; the second is usually the turn (irony, dread, dry joke). Never cruel. Concrete senses over adjectives (a smell, a sound, a temperature). No game terms: never "roll", "check", "DC", "HP", "level". No winks at the player.
Samples:
- "By a painted wagon, a woman in a long dark coat sits fixing silver heads to crossbow bolts. Below her right knee, a brace of oak and steel; her left hand is bandaged, and she smells faintly of burnt hair."
- "She doesn't look up. She lifts a small loaded crossbow off her knee instead and points it at you."

### Name 1: `falls` - Dragan, the werewolf at Tser Falls
Facts: Tser Falls is where the River Ivlis throws itself off a cliff in a long white fall; a shelf of rock juts over the drop where the Barovians leave tokens for the drowned and the fallen. A werewolf named Dragan waits at the lip of the falls at night for travellers, with wolves (a dire wolf and some wolves). He is one of Kiril's pack (Kiril leads the werewolf pack that dens in the hills above Lake Zarovich; she has been hunting that pack for years, a few at a time). The party killed him there; long scratches in the rock show where he stood. Payment: silver-headed bolts, and something to stitch yourselves with (a healing draught).

### Name 2: `wagon` - the Tinker's Wagon
Facts: A green painted tinker's wagon stands at the crossroads on the Old Svalich Road, selling pots and trinkets (some of them taken from a haunted house). Travellers who bought from the tinker never came back down the road. The tinker, Iosif, used to be one of the Vistani; he sat on the board and never climbed down. The wagon is a mimic the size of a wagon; it kept Iosif alive at the front as bait for seven years; its sticky arms grab, its tailboard bites, it spits trinkets. The party killed it, and Iosif went back to the Vistani camp at Tser Pool. Payment: flasks of alchemist's fire, silver-headed bolts, and a healing draught.

### Name 3: `fowlers` - the count's fowlers on the hill trail
Facts: On the hill trail above Lake Zarovich, a silver cage hung in a dead pine with a raven in it that would not change back. It was a trap. The count's fowlers - vampire spawn, a leader and several more, with swarms of bats - waited in the branches for whoever came for the cage. The party killed them. (Ezmerelda knows only the trap and the fowlers, not whose cage or whose raven; she may have a hunter's opinion about cages.) Payment: holy water, silver-headed bolts, and a healing draught.

### Name 4: `vine` - the Vine Mother
Facts: The Seventh Row of a vineyard came up black every night and was picked bare by morning. Its branches reach, its vines drag you in and squeeze, and it spits fermenting black wine. Smaller vine blights and needle blights grew around it. The party cut it down. Payment: a scroll that guards against plants, flasks of alchemist's fire, and a healing draught.
