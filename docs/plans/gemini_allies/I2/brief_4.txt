## The scene: his letters to her

While Ireena is sheltering somewhere safe (the Blue Water Inn or St. Andral's church in Vallaki, or the burgomaster's house in Krezk; the lines must work at any of them, so NEVER name the place), the vampire lord writes to her. Three letters, spaced by the story. She shows the party each one in a check-in conversation and asks what they would do with it. She never says his name ("him", "the devil", "our visitor"). In each letter HE speaks, as Strahd, and the game shows his lines under his own name; the Narrator describes the paper.

Strahd's voice (a "velvet knife"): elegant, courteous, unhurried, complete sentences, old-fashioned grammar, never raises his voice. Every courtesy names its threat: not a vague menace but WHO, WHERE or WHEN (Kolyan in his grave, the window she sleeps behind, the one the others would die for, the road each night). He speaks of Ireena as already his: tender in tone, never in meaning; he never asks after her comfort; he calls her "Ireena", softly. No jokes at his own expense, never lonely, wistful or put out. He signs "Strahd". He knows exactly whose face she wears and does not say it. Pet phrases: "Welcome." "How charming." "We will speak again." "Do not let me keep you." Existing lines of his (match this register; do not repeat them): "Keep the scarf on. I know what is under it. I put it there." / "Every road in this valley ends at my door. I had them laid that way." / "My dear guests. Forgive a host for writing when he might call. My nights are spoken for, and my days are not my own." / "You have put Ireena behind a wall and a door. I have always admired walls. They tell me exactly where to look."

THE THREE LETTERS (each arrives by an unseen hand; the courier is never named or described):
LETTER 1 comes after he has been to her window (or to the churchyard gate where she sheltered) once already. He writes as a man who called and was not let in, and who is not discouraged. Strahd x3 lines.
LETTER 2 comes after the festival in Vallaki has ended, however it went (do not say who won, and do not name the Baron, Lady Wachter or the priest). He comments on the town's lights and banners and on the people who think they keep him out. Strahd x3 lines.
LETTER 3 comes with the dinner: a black carriage has been sent for the party (the party has had its own invitation), and her letter invites her to the same table. Ireena tells the party she BURNED this one. She did not (she kept it, and the game knows it): the party can see through her with an Insight check (the game rolls it; you write both the success and the plain version). It is her secret from the voice bible: some nights she does not want to keep the window shut. She hates that about herself and has told no one. If the party sees through her, she admits it, reads out one line of the letter she kept, and asks them to stop HER, not him, if she ever reaches for the latch. It is a secret, not a betrayal: she is frightened of herself, not leaning toward him.
