## The situation

Ireena Kolyana is a tall red-haired woman in her early twenties, the adopted daughter of the late burgomaster of the village of Barovia. The vampire lord Strahd hunts her. The player's party has left her to shelter in the town of Vallaki, either at the Blue Water Inn or in St. Andral's church, and has gone about its business.

Izek Strazni is the Baron's enforcer in Vallaki: a big scarred man in a watch captain's coat whose right arm is not his own (scaled red-black to the shoulder, clawed, hot, and able to burst into flame). When he was nine, wolves took his little sister in the woods by the lake, and took his arm too. He has dreamed of a red-haired woman every night since and pays the toymaker to carve dolls of her face. He has seen Ireena. Neither of them knows she is his lost sister, and he does not know it either; he only knows he wants her, safe, near him, never in the woods again.

A visit now comes to the party, at night, while the party is somewhere in Vallaki (the inn, the church, or the town square): somebody local brings them news about Izek and Ireena. There are three stages, each its own scene, played at different nights:

1. OMEN. Izek has taken to standing, motionless, outside the place where Ireena sleeps, staring at her window. Nothing has happened yet. A warning.
2. TAKEN. Some nights later, during the small hours, Izek walked in (the inn) or through the churchyard (the church) and carried her off to his room in the Baron's mansion. He did not hurt her; she is unhurt as far as anyone knows. Nobody stopped him. She is in his room, off the great hall. The door is not locked. He sits in front of it.
3. ESCAPE. Two days later, nobody having come for her, she got out by herself: she hit Izek in the face with a brass candlestick in front of six of the Baron's watch, broke his nose, and walked out of the mansion's front door past men who did not know whether to stop her. The watch saw it; he is humiliated. She is back at her refuge, bruised and colder toward the party, who did not come.

Who brings the news depends on where the party is when the visit finds them: at the Blue Water Inn it is URWIN (the innkeeper); at St. Andral's it is FATHER LUCIAN; anywhere else in Vallaki it is a TOWN GUARD (a watchman of the Baron). Whatever the speaker says must read correctly whichever refuge Ireena was in (the inn or the church), so speaker lines are written NEUTRAL about which refuge ("where she sleeps", "her room", "the door") - the Narrator lines, which come first, say which refuge it is.

What the player sees, in order: a Narrator line for the refuge (two versions: inn / church), then the speaker for where the party stands (three versions), then a small menu of what the party can say. Every scene ends the conversation.
