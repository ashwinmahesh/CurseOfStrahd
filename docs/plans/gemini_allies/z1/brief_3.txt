## The scene

After Ireena has been taken to Izek's room and has got away from him (she got out alone and broke his nose with a candlestick in front of the watch; or the party walked her out by day; or the party made him let her go with a threat), the party can find Izek again at night in his room off the great hall at the Baron's mansion: the same bare room, the cot, the washstand, the sword in the corner, the open chest of red-haired wooden dolls. He is alone now. Ireena is not there. He does not know she is his sister, and nobody in this scene says so. He is not repentant: he still thinks he was keeping her safe, and he still wants her. But the thing that made him sure of himself has gone.

Two variants, chosen by how it ended:
- AFTER_ESCAPE: she got out alone and broke his nose in front of six of the Baron's watch. He is humiliated. His nose is swollen and bruised dark; he has splinted it with a strip of tin or a folded cloth. He sits on the cot with a doll in his ordinary (left) hand and does not look up when the party comes in. He is quiet, flat and, for him, almost honest. He admits (once, in his own flat way) that she walked past him and he did not stop her, and that he does not know why.
- AFTER_PARTY: the party walked her out by day, or forced him to let her go. He is angry but cold, not loud: he counts what was taken out of his room (he does not say "mine" about a person: he says "she"), and he repeats that he is good at waiting.

The first line the player hears is a Narrator line; Izek speaks next; then the conversation goes on into the menu that already exists (a menu where the party may threaten him, tell him the truth if they know it, draw steel, or go). So these lines only OPEN the scene. Do not write the menu.

## Voice reminders

IZEK: flat, short, imperatives; never more than two sentences unless about her; about her he goes quiet and almost gentle, which is worse. Moods: [angry], [sad] or none.
NARRATOR: old gothic storyteller, second person or plain scene description, present tense, one or two sentences, the second a turn; concrete senses; never cruel; no game terms.

## Canon limits

No new characters, places or history. Nobody says Ireena's name in Izek's mouth unless it is "she"; Izek may say "her". Nobody says Strahd's name. Do not name Izek's lost sister. He does not hurt anyone in this scene.

Format reminder: one line per row, `Speaker [mood]: Text`; speakers are Narrator and Izek. Group under `~ node_name` in the order given. No commentary.
