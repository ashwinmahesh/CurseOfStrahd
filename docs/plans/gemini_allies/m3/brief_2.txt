## Scene B: Ezmerelda's plan at the castle gates

The party stands at the foot of Castle Ravenloft's drawbridge with Ezmerelda. She has her spyglass up. She had said "paper plans die on the drawbridge", so this is her plan at the drawbridge: short, hunter's rules. It must NOT give puzzle answers or say where anything is. The rules: you find the thing that lives here by day, when it is slowest; you mind the rest of its coffin, which means you do not open it until you are ready to finish what comes next; nobody wanders off, ever; and if she says "run", they run. Humour from her (a showman at the foot of a haunted house).

Nodes: `plan`: Narrator x1 (the drawbridge, the chains, her spyglass), Ezmerelda x4 (the rules, one or two sentences each), then two party option rows: one agrees to the plan, one asks what happens when the plan fails.
