## The situation

Ezmerelda d'Avenir (a Vistana monster hunter, trained by the legendary Rudolph van Richten; long dark coat, rapier, silvered crossbow bolts, a spyglass, and an oak-and-steel brace where a werewolf took her leg below the right knee) travels with the player's party. When the party makes camp and rests for the night, she sometimes asks for a word by the fire: a short scene, played once. She is quick, sardonic and practical; she turns hunters' rules into jokes and then means them; she grew up in wagons that put on shows, so she has showman's timing and mocks her own missing parts. Under that she is afraid of being too late again, and of being left behind again, and she is not sure she is as brave as her reputation.

Each scene has this shape:
- a node named for the scene: Narrator x1 (the fire and what she is doing, one or two sentences), then Ezmerelda x2 (her opening), then two party option rows (the player's character speaks, first person, short, under 14 words, no skill checks);
- two reply nodes, named `<scene>_a` and `<scene>_b`, each Ezmerelda x2 (or x1), one for each option. The scene ends after the reply (do not write `-> END`, I will).
About 7 lines and 2 option rows a scene.

## The five scenes

1. leg: offered once the werewolf den in the hills has been settled one way or the other. The werewolf who took her leg, years ago in the hills above Lake Zarovich, was big, black and laughing: Kiril, the pack's old leader. TWO opening nodes with the same two option rows pointing to the same replies `leg_a` and `leg_b` (the replies must read right after either opening): `leg_alive` (Kiril is still alive and still hunts for the castle: she is quiet, working the brace's straps, and her anger is not loud) and `leg_done` (Kiril is dead, by the party's hand or by his own pack's: it should feel like winning and does not; the brace is lighter, or she imagines it is). Options: one asks whether she would have wanted to do it herself, one asks what she remembers of that night.
2. rudolph: offered once the party has found out where her teacher Rudolph van Richten (he hides as a showman called Rictavio) is, or has brought them together. Why does he keep leaving? She thinks he decided everyone he teaches ends up dead, and he is half right. He keyed the ward on his tower to her name alone to keep her out. She loves him with exasperation. Options: one says he is lucky to have a student who will follow him through a ward, one asks whether she has ever tried leaving first.
3. wagons: offered once the party has been to the Vistani camp at Tser Pool, where her people are. She left the wagons young; they pretend not to know her; she was raised in a wagon that put on shows and still has the timing. Not bitter, wistful and funny. No names of relatives. Options: one asks her for a trick (a coin, a card; she does one and gets it slightly wrong on purpose or by accident), one asks if she would go back.
4. fear: offered once the party has come through some hard fights together. The night she decided she was not as brave as her reputation says, and that this may be wisdom. "Brave" is just what people call it when she doesn't stop. Options: one says that is all bravery ever is, one says the player is afraid too.
5. castle_eve: offered the night before the party goes to Castle Ravenloft, where the vampire lord lives. She goes first through doors (she says she has the leg that does not bleed). If she does not come back, someone should write her entry in Rudolph's next book, and spell it right (d'Avenir, the apostrophe in it) and get the ending right. Options: one says they will write it together, one says she will not need an entry.
