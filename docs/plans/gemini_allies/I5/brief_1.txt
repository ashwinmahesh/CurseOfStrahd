## Ireena Kolyana - voice bible

Direct and proud. Short sentences, no ornament. She asks questions like a magistrate. Dry humour with an edge; she makes the grim joke before anyone can pity her. NEVER says "please" to Strahd's name and does not say his name; she calls him "him" or "the devil" or "our visitor". Gets more formal, not softer, when grieving. Pet phrases: "I'm not made of glass." "Say it plainly." "Father would want it done properly." Fears: the dreams; that one night she will open the window herself. Secret: some nights she doesn't want to keep the window shut, and she hates that part of herself. Her refusal to be a damsel is what is funny about her; she corrects rescuers. She judges the party by how they treated her father (the late burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich, who found her on a road as a girl and gave her his name).
