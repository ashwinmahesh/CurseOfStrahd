## The situation

Ireena Kolyana (a proud, red-haired young woman, the dead burgomaster's adopted daughter, twice bitten by Strahd) has been left by the party to shelter somewhere: the Blue Water Inn or St. Andral's church in Vallaki, a friend's house behind Krezk's wall, or her father's mansion in the village. She does not travel with them now. While the party is away on the road and something big happens that she would hear of, a courier brings the party a letter in her hand. The courier is NEVER named and never described beyond a line: a carter's boy, a Vistani child, a pilgrim, a goatherd. The letter is read aloud as lines spoken by Ireena. She never begs. She is proud, dry and exact, and she says what she wants in the shape of a complaint or an order, because that is how she says anything tender.

The point of the letters is that she is still thinking about them, and that the party owes her a visit. No letter gives quest information, hints at where to go or what to do, or names a place the party must go. No letter says "come back" in a pleading way; the nearest she gets is something like "the shutters are well kept and I have run out of excuses to check them".

Every letter must read right whether she SAW the event or only HEARD of it from carters, Vistani and pilgrims (she is in different places). So write each letter as news that has reached her, not as something she witnessed.
