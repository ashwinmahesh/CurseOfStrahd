IZEK 1: Izek sits at the edge of the firelight where he can see Ireena's bedroll without turning his head. His eyes lift from the coals only to make sure his sister is still there.
IZEK 2: Izek braces a block of pine against his knee with his scaled forearm and pares away curls of wood with a small knife. Another rough little face is taking shape under his thumb.
IZEK 3: Izek rests his clawed arm in the dark beside his boot, turning a bit of cord over in his other hand. He ties a slow, clumsy knot and picks it apart again.
IZEK 4: Izek takes his bowl last from the pot and eats alone, gripping the tin in his good hand. He scrapes it clean without looking up, then sets it quietly by the fire.
IZEK 5: Izek watches the dark beyond Ireena's shoulder, his chin sunk in his collar. Whenever she shifts under her blanket, he goes very still until she settles again.
IZEK 6: Izek sits awake long after the talk dies down, his broad back between the bedrolls and the dark. He says nothing at all, and does not seem to miss the words.
