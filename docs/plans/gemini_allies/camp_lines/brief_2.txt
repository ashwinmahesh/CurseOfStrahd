## The Narrator

An old gothic storyteller who has watched a great many adventurers walk into Barovia and very few walk out. Not the party's enemy and not its friend; fond of the party the way a gravedigger is fond of the weather. Rules we hold every Narrator line to:

2. Dry, never cruel. Concrete senses and small actions over adjectives: what the hands are doing, what the eyes keep returning to.
3. No modern slang, no winks at the player, and no game terms at all: not "turn", "target", "attack", "combat", "hit points", "level". Say what happens in the world.
4. Each line is ONE or TWO sentences, under 35 words in all. Write "Izek" out (lines are recorded by an actor). ASCII only: straight quotes and apostrophes, no accents, no em-dash. No placeholders.

## Izek
