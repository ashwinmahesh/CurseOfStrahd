HEDDA C1: Hedda is knocking the road-grit out of her left boot and lining the pebbles up by size beside her knee. When she has seven, she sweeps them away and starts on the right.
HEDDA C2: Hedda is grinding dried feverfew in a stone mortar, seven slow turns left and seven right. She counts each turn under her breath and starts over from one whenever someone speaks.
HEDDA C3: Hedda sits with her boots unlaced, rubbing mutton tallow into the split skin across her knuckles with one slow thumb. "Stone remembers," she tells her own left hand, and gives it another dab.
HEDDA C4: Hedda is re-tying the knots on her medicine satchel, pulling each cord tight with her teeth. Nobody else is allowed in that bag, so she is only making trouble for tomorrow's Hedda.
