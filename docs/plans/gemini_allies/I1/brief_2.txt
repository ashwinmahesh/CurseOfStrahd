## What these nodes are

During a check-in Ireena also reacts to NEWS: big things the party has done since she last saw them. Each news item is a short node of her own reaction. The game plays at most three of them in a check-in and each only once, so each must stand alone, with no reference to the others and no "as I said". She is in one of the five situations from the first request (the inn, the church, Krezk, her father's mansion, or travelling with the party), and the SAME lines are played in all of them, so every line has to read right whether she SAW the event (she was with the party, or nearby) or only HEARD of it (from the party, from rumour, from a courier). Therefore: do not say "when you told me", "I saw you", "you came back and said". Talk about the event and what it means to her, in the present tense, as someone who knows it happened. She never says Strahd's name ("him", "the devil", "our visitor", "the lord of the castle"). She is dry, proud and exact, makes the grim joke before anyone can pity her, and judges people by how they treated her father's memory. She is not fragile and the lines must not make her fragile. 1-2 sentences a line, ideally under 25 words; use the line counts below as targets, not more.

Allowed extra names (and no others): Kolyan or "Father", Ismark, Father Lucian, Urwin Martikov, Dmitri and Anna Krezkov, Ilya, the Baron, Lady Wachter, Izek, Madam Eva, the Abbot, Vasilka (the Abbot's made bride), Baba Lysaga, Marina, Mordenkainen, Rictavio, Ezmerelda. Do not invent anyone else.

## Format

One line per row, exactly: `Ireena [mood]: Text` (mood optional; allowed moods: smile, angry, afraid, sad, weary). Plain ASCII punctuation (straight quotes, hyphens, no em dashes). No stage directions, no placeholders. Group the lines under the node names above, each node on its own line as `~ node_name`, in the order given. Do not add nodes, code or commentary.
