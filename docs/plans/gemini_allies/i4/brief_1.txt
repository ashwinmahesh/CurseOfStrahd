## The situation

Ireena Kolyana (a proud, red-haired young woman in her early twenties, the dead burgomaster Kolyan's adopted daughter, found on a road as a girl with no memory of where she came from, twice bitten by the vampire lord Strahd) travels with the player's party. When the party makes camp and rests for the night, she sometimes asks for a word by the fire: a short scene, played once. She sits at the fire with the company. In this game she does not fight: something in the valley goes round her like water round a stone, so enemies do not touch her and she does not strike, and she hates standing back from fights while the others bleed.

Each scene has this shape:
- a node named for the scene: Narrator x1 (what the fire and she look like, one or two sentences), then Ireena x2 (her opening), then two party option rows (the party is a group of adventurers; the option text is what the player's character says, first person, short, under 14 words, no skill checks);
- two reply nodes, named `<scene>_a` and `<scene>_b`, each Ireena x1 or x2, one for each option. The scene ends after the reply (do not write `-> END`, I will).
That is about 7 lines and 2 option rows per scene: 7 scenes = about 49 lines.

## The seven scenes (the name is the node name; the note says when it is offered and what it is about)

1. kept_back: offered once the party has been through many fights with her at the side. Ireena is restless: she has watched every fight from behind the line and cannot answer a single blow. She does not want to be a thing the valley is keeping. She asks for sword practice by the fire, her hands steady, and means it. Options: one takes her seriously (practice at dawn), one tells her it keeps her alive.
2. burial: offered after her father's burial. Ireena is formal and exact about the funeral, as she gets when she grieves: how the grave beside her mother was dug, the lamp she carried, who spoke. She asks whether the party remembers the words as they were said, because she wants them kept exactly. Options: one repeats that Kolyan was well sent off, one admits they remember little but remember that she did not look away.
3. window: offered after Strahd came to her window and she was saved from going. She says she does not want to be watched asleep, and asks that someone near her stays awake. She is ashamed that she wanted to go. Options: one promises to take the watch before hers, one tells her honestly they cannot promise to stop it every time.
4. izek: offered after the party told Izek, the Baron's enforcer in Vallaki, that Ireena is the sister he lost in the woods as a boy. Ireena turns it over: she has a brother she does not remember, a big, scarred man who carved her face into dolls. She does not know what a sister owes him. Options: one says she owes him nothing yet, one says he is a man who has been looking for her all his life.
5. pool: offered after the Pool of the White Sun in Krezk, where she saw a dead man in the water who knew her by another name. TWO opening variants: pool_promised (she promised him she will come back to the pool when the devil is dead) and pool_stayed (she chose her own life and said goodbye to him). Use the node names `pool_promised` and `pool_stayed` for the two openings (each Narrator x1, Ireena x2), both with the same two option rows pointing to the same `pool_a` and `pool_b` replies (so the replies must read right after either opening). Options: one asks what he looked like, one says it is her choice either way.
6. amber: offered after a member of the party has taken a gift from the Amber Temple in the northern mountains, a temple of old bargains sealed in amber. She says she can smell old resin on them, sweet and wrong, and it frightens her more than anything the devil has done, because he takes and these were given. She does not forgive easily. Options: one tells her what it cost, one tells her it was worth it.
7. castle_eve: offered the night before the party goes to Castle Ravenloft, the devil's home, where she has never been and knows where the stairs go. She is calm and formal. She says what she wants done if she is taken: no rescue from the party at the price of the party, and a message for her brother Ismark. Options: one refuses to leave her, one promises the message will be delivered.
