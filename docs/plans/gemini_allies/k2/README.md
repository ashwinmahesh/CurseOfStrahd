## What was edited after the drafts

Canon (checked against our data):

- **Hands.** Gemini's message for Patrina, "the stones in my hands were heavy, even when I dropped them", had him throw stones; he stood
  at the back with empty hands. It became "my hands were empty, and I know now which was heavier".
- **Where he was.** Strahd's "Three hundred years at my gates" assumed Kasimir lived at the castle; he lodged with the Vistani, so
  "three hundred years in my valley". "Stayed to tend your graves" is not what the dusk elves did (tenants): "stayed as your tenants".
- **Timing.** "Your sister arrived some hours ahead of your carriage, though she had the longer walk" invented hours and a walk:
  "arrived ahead of you". "Cold iron" (Kasimir is a wizard) became "a steady hand".
- **Tense of the bargain.** "Traded away the rest of my life" became "all but a year", because he kept one. "Walk with you into
  Castle Ravenloft" in `counting_b` contradicted his waiting at the gate: "find my sister, and learn what she has made of her second life".
- **Small.** "Fishing net" (the net he mends is never cast) became "net"; "the soup" kept; a missing space; the farewell's "walks back to a
  flat stone" kept (the dinner party is in the courtyard, the stone is down the road).

Register and length (cut about 15%): a clause out of the gates hub's first greeting and the castle-eve talk's last line.

Structure (mine, nothing to draft): the conditions (the prop's call, the NPC's place, the dinner's two openings), the shared tail's flags
and `leave`, the hub's menu with the scrolls opening his shop, the camp talks' conditions and order.
