## Who they are

His sister Patrina went up to the castle of her own will to learn what its lord knew; their own people stoned her for it, and Kasimir stood at the back with empty hands and did nothing, for three hundred years. He has lodged with the Vistani, mending a net he never casts. He believed a sealed temple of amber in the northern mountains could bring her back. Pet turns: "I am poor company.
