## Kasimir Velikov - voice bible

Soft, grave, formal; long patience; self-mockery so dry it reads as sorrow. Never hurries. Wants: Patrina back; to stop being a coward. Fears: that the temple will say yes. Secret: he is not sure he wants her back for her sake or for his. Speaker name in the lines: `Kasimir`.

Existing Kasimir lines (match this register; do not repeat them):
- "The strangers. The camp talks of nothing else. Sit, if you like. I am poor company. I have been poor company for a very long time."
- "Long ago a Vistana I had never met fell sick with a fever on the road, and I sat with her three nights until it broke. Her grandchildren still bring me soup. The Vistani forget nothing: not a kindness, not an insult."
- "My people came to this valley long ago, the elves of the dusk, as guests of its lord. We stayed as his tenants. Now there are only a handful of us, none of us young, and there will never be another. That was his doing. And, I am afraid, a little of ours."
- "The songs were written before the ending. They usually are."
- "I write them out at night, when I can't sleep, which is every night. Lesser spells; the ones that matter I keep."
- "Beyond the pass to the north, in the ice. I have read of it, and dreamed of it, and been too much of a coward to climb to it."
- "My people came over this pass as the count's guests. They are still his guests. It is the longest visit in the history of the world."
- "The temple whispers in a voice I knew as a boy. I told myself I would not listen. I am listening."
- "You all walk so quickly. I used to think it was impatience. Now I think it is arithmetic."
- "Every morning I wake and count. The number is smaller. I would not take the bargain back. I would like to stop counting."

An old gothic storyteller, fond of the party the way a gravedigger is fond of the weather. Second person, present tense. One or two sentences; the second is usually the turn (irony, dread, dry joke). Ominous, dryly funny, never cruel. Concrete senses over adjectives (a smell, a sound, a temperature). No game terms: never "roll", "check", "DC", "HP", "level", "skill". No winks at the player.
