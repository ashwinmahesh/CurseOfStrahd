## The character and the situation

Kasimir Velikov is a dusk elf (ash-dark skin, white hair) who lodges with the Vistani in their camp outside the town of Vallaki, on the shore of Lake Zarovich. He sits on a stool by a wagon with its shutters closed, mending a fishing net he will never cast. He is soft-spoken, grave, formal, with a very dry self-mockery that reads as sorrow. He never hurries.

His sister Patrina was cleverer and prouder than he is. She went up to the castle of the valley's lord (the vampire Strahd) of her own will, to learn what he knew. Their own people stoned her for it, and Kasimir stood at the back of the crowd and did not stop them. Her bones lie under the castle. Her ghost, a banshee, haunts the Amber Temple: a temple sealed in amber, high in the ice above the Tsolenka Pass in the mountains north of Vallaki, where the sealed remains of dead gods and worse can talk and bargain. He has dreamed of going there and has been too much of a coward to climb to it. He told the player's party all this, and told them: "If ever you go that way, I would like very much to go with you."

What the game now adds (these are the scenes to write):

1. The party can ask him to come with them to the temple. He agrees and walks with the party as a companion.
2. If the party laid Patrina to rest without him (by prayer or by words), they can come back to the camp and tell him. Her last words to the party were: "Tell him I'm done waiting. Tell him the stones didn't hurt as much as the quiet afterward. He'll understand that. He was always very good at quiet." The party relays them to him in the camp, one piece at a time, as many pieces as they choose. At the end he sets the net down and leaves it down.
3. If the party destroyed Patrina's spirit in a fight, they can come back to the camp and tell him. He does not rage and does not ask how. He asks only one thing: was she afraid? Whatever they answer, his warmth toward the party is gone from then on (he stays courteous, and his scrolls cost more; the game handles the price).
4. If he travelled with the party and the temple business is over (Patrina is at rest; she told him: "Go home, little brother. Don't bring me back. Don't make bargains. Be brave somewhere I can't see you; it will annoy me less."), he offers to go back to the camp unless the party would like him to stay. The player chooses.
