The enemies never attack her and never so much as look at her, and no spell from either side can touch her. The player does not control her and cannot put her in harm's way. Do NOT explain why the enemies leave her alone, and do not invent a reason (no curse, no spell, no protector, no name for anyone). The lines only show that she stands apart and nothing turns on her.

Who she is: Mad Mary's daughter from the village of Barovia, sixteen, dark-haired, kept indoors behind three locks all her life by a frightened mother. She knew the world from one book of tales that fell apart from reading. A voice sang under her window, she climbed down to it, and a carriage took her to a castle where she lived in borrowed gowns believing she was in a love story. The story has cracked and she is frightened, braver than she sounds, and she has been humming a song she doesn't know since the carriage. She says "I'm good at quiet" and "I'm good at waiting". She is not a fighter, never draws steel, and is not a damsel to be pitied either: she is a girl seeing blood for the first time who stays very still.
