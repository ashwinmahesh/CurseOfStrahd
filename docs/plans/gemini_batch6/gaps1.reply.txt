== halberdier_toll
Narrator: You hold out the forty coppers, and the halberdier counts them over in your palm, though his iron fingers pass clean through half the pile.
Narrator: Satisfied with the tally, he inclines his helm once and steps clear of the gate for the first time in three hundred years.
Narrator: When he turns to begin his next round, he raps three times upon the great doors, a little more softly than before.
