== goat_hall
Narrator: The village goat stands in the middle of the great entry, chewing the hem of a three-hundred-year-old hunting tapestry.
Narrator: Beside it, the Hall Broom sweeps in neat, respectful circles around all four hooves, as if dusting the legs of a sideboard.
Narrator: Neither of them pays you the slightest mind. A thread of gold bullion disappears into the goat's mouth with a dry rustle.
== goat_hall_speaks
Narrator: The goat chews four times before answering. "I walked in. The drawbridge was down, and nobody was using the stairs."
Narrator: "This is a hunting scene," it says around a mouthful of dyed wool. "The hounds are a bit dry, but the stag is extraordinary."
Narrator: "Best thing I have eaten in the valley. Easily beats the burgomaster's shutters. The broom is very polite, too."
== fennimore_arrive
Narrator: Fennimore stands on the cliff road outside the gates with his collar turned up against the mist, squinting at the towers over his notebook.
Fennimore: Ah, there you are. Do not mind me, I am only doing the exterior before the light goes completely.
Fennimore: Imposing approach, very fine masonry, terrible draughts from the gorge. The gargoyles are a little much, if I am honest.
Fennimore [weary]: Castle Ravenloft: two stars. Commanding view of the valley, no porter at the gate, and the host has not been seen since the soup.
Fennimore: Still, the hall is swept. That is more than I could say for Vallaki.
== fennimore_book
Fennimore [weary]: Eleven years next month. Nine hundred and fourteen pages, and I am nearly out of the good paper I brought from home.
Fennimore: I used to think I was writing it for the coach office back beyond the mists, so the next poor soul would know where not to dine.
Fennimore [sad]: There is no coach office, of course. The road only goes the one way, and I am not a man for climbing gates.
Fennimore [smile]: So the book is for whoever finds it on me, or for nobody at all. Either way, a thing ought to be written down properly.
== fennimore_end
Fennimore: Before you go inside, I have put you in the appendix. Travelling companions: five stars. No notes.
Fennimore [smile]: Thank you for the rabbit stew, and the dry tin for my ink, and the company on the road from the mill.
Fennimore: Do come back out if you can. I should hate to have to revise the castle down to one.
