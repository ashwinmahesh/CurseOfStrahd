# NPC roster: trait, want and secret

Appendix to [npc_depth_and_quests.md](npc_depth_and_quests.md) (Part A). One row per NPC who speaks or could, grouped
by region, in the order the batches take them. The line count is how many lines they speak in the game today.

**Approach:** `has` already has all three in the game (book or earlier quests); the row says what they are, and the
NPC gets at most a line. `flavour` 2–5 new lines: the trait in a greeting, the want when asked, the secret behind a
check, a second visit or another NPC's gossip. `hook` a small errand (6–15 lines, no fight, no new map, a small
reward). `quest` a new or reshaped side quest (Part B names it). `comic` marks a comic-relief part (Part C).

Secrets are gated: an Insight or other check (failing it gives nothing), a second visit, a companion's interject, or
another NPC's gossip. A secret never sets a main flag and never opens or closes a quest by itself.
