You are drafting short lines of text for a single-player computer RPG based on the D&D adventure "Curse of Strahd". All wording is our own: do not copy the adventure book. You write; I will trim and check canon. Reply with the lines only, in the format given at the end.

THE PLACE
Argynvostholt is a ruined mansion on a wooded ridge in the haunted land of Barovia. A silver dragon, Argynvost, who liked people enough to wear a man's shape among them, built it and founded a knightly order there, the Order of the Silver Dragon. He lit a beacon at the top of the tower every night so the valley knew someone kept watch. Four hundred years ago the young lord of Castle Ravenloft (Strahd) came over the mountains with an army. The Order lost, the house burned in one night, the dragon died in his own courtyard and Strahd carried his skull home as a trophy. The knights got up again as revenants (dead knights who remember their posts, not their names). Their commander, Vladimir Horngaard, keeps them at their posts. The frost and silver light of the dragon still cling to the place.

TONE (this is the house style, follow it)
A ghost story about loyalty. The dead are courteous, tired and occasionally funny. The Narrator is an old gothic storyteller, fond of the party the way a gravedigger is fond of the weather: interested, without illusions, never gloating, never cruel. Rules for every Narrator line:
- Second person, present tense ("You step inside"). Never "the party". Never use a character's name.
- One or two sentences. The second sentence is usually the turn: the irony, the dread, the dry joke.
- Concrete senses (a smell, a sound, a temperature) over adjectives.
- No game terms (not "HP", "roll", "level", "check", "trap"), no modern slang, no winking at the player.
- Describe only what a character standing there could perceive. Do not reveal hidden things.
- Respect the dead: wry about the furniture, never about the corpse.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes. Use commas, colons, full stops. Plain ASCII apostrophes and quotes.
- Each line under 35 words.
