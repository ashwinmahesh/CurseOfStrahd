You are drafting lines for a single-player gothic horror RPG based on Curse of Strahd (Barovia, Strahd von Zarovich). Write in our own words; never quote the published book. Reply with plain text only: one line per slot, in the form `SLOT_ID: text`, in the order given, nothing else. Finish without sending messages to anyone else.

## Voices

**The Narrator** speaks for the world: an old gothic storyteller, second person, present tense ("You step inside."), one or two sentences, the second often the turn (the irony, the dread, the dry joke). Ominous, dryly funny, never cruel. Concrete senses (a smell, a sound, a temperature) over adjectives. No game terms (no "HP", "roll", "level", "check"). Never states things the characters could not perceive. In the scariest places (this temple, these tombs) the humour is thin and dry, and some lines carry no joke at all.

**Master Vosk** is a fiend (an arcanaloth, a jackal-headed fiend of contracts) posing as a mild, bespectacled human scholar in the temple's library. He brokers the bargains of the evil things sealed in amber and takes "a small commission". He talks like a silky, generous notary: terms, fees, considerations, "concluded". Cheerful professionalism about damnation; treats the end of a soul like a filing error. Pet phrases: "Do come and find me." "People never read the terms." He never quite lies about a bargain; he lies freely about everything else.

## Package A: the ambush under the faceless god (the Amber Temple)

The Amber Temple is a frozen temple cut into a mountain, where wizards long ago sealed the remnants of dead, evil gods in sarcophagi of glowing amber. The great hall holds a colossal robed statue whose face was never carved: smooth stone under the hood. The party arrives from the entrance stair into a pillared gallery, then walks into the great hall.

What happens (this is new to the game): as the party first steps into the great hall, the darkness at the statue's head is wrong: deeper than the rest, and something stands in it, unseen, a shape with two tall pointed ears. At the same moment three flameskulls (flying, burning skulls, the old wardens' guards) fire from narrow arrow slits in a hidden gallery in the west wall. The party is caught by surprise. After a couple of rounds the shape in the dark speaks once, politely, and is simply gone. Later the party meets Master Vosk, apparently a harmless scholar, in the library. He is the shape in the dark, and he does not admit it outright, but a careful listener can tell.

The hidden gallery: a cramped stone firing gallery behind the hall's west wall, reached from a frozen reading hall, with two arrow slits looking down the length of the temple. A rack of the wardens' staves stands at its back; one staff is rimed with frost that never melts (it is a Staff of Frost).

Slots:
- T1_FIGHT_TEXT: shown as the ambush begins (Narrator, 1 or 2 sentences): the dark at the god's head moves, and green fire spits from the west wall.
- T1_WITHDRAW_SAY: Vosk, unseen, speaking from the dark as he leaves the fight (one or two short sentences, under 25 words). He sounds pleased, as if the party passed an assessment. He must not say his name. A hint of the notary.
- T1_VOSK_KNOWS: Vosk, later, in the library, the first time the party meets him after that ambush (one or two sentences). Mild and friendly; he mentions that the hall "welcomes" visitors rudely, or asks if they enjoyed the hall, in a way that a careful listener realises he was there. He never admits it.
- T1_VOSK_KNOWS_ALT: a second, different version of T1_VOSK_KNOWS.
- T1_ENTER_GALLERY: Narrator, entering the hidden gallery for the first time (1 or 2 sentences): cramped, cold, two arrow slits with the great hall's dark beyond, scorch marks round the slits.
- T1_ENTER_GALLERY_AFTER: Narrator, the same gallery after the flameskulls are destroyed (1 sentence).
- T1_EXAMINE_SLIT: Narrator, looking out through an arrow slit (1 or 2 sentences): from here the whole hall lies open below, and the faceless statue, and the place where the party stood a moment ago.
- T1_OPEN_RACK: Narrator, opening the rack of staves (1 sentence): the one staff rimed with frost that doesn't melt in the hand.
- T1_BANTER: one party member (unnamed, a line any adventurer could say) after the ambush, about the voice in the dark (one short sentence).

## Package B: the guardians' curtain (the tombs under Castle Ravenloft)

Deep under Castle Ravenloft, past the catacombs, a short stair hall leads down to the tomb of King Barov and Queen Ravenovia, Strahd's parents. Halfway down the hall stand two bronze knights, each holding a spear, and between them hangs a curtain of pale blue light. The old enchantment lets the worthy pass. In our game it judges whether anyone in the party carries one of the dark gifts from the Amber Temple (a bargain with one of those sealed evil things). A party that carries none walks through and the light asks nothing of them. A party that carries one finds itself turned back: the light closes round them and they are standing at the top of the stair again, with no memory of the walk back. Coming UP from the royal tomb (the tomb can also be reached by climbing in through a window from outside), the curtain lets anyone through.

Slots:
- C11_ENTER_HALL: Narrator, entering the stair hall (1 or 2 sentences): the two bronze knights, spears grounded, the curtain of blue light hanging between them, humming faintly.
- C11_EXAMINE_KNIGHT: Narrator, looking at one of the bronze knights (1 or 2 sentences): their faces, the spear, the old craft. Something about them suggests they are watching what passes, not who.
- C11_EXAMINE_CURTAIN: Narrator, looking at the curtain of light (1 or 2 sentences): cold blue light, like moonlight through water; it does not move with the air.
- C11_WORTHY: Narrator, the first time a party with no dark gift walks into the curtain (1 or 2 sentences): it passes over them and asks nothing, and the knights' spears do not stir.
- C11_TURNED_BACK: Narrator, the first time a party carrying a dark gift walks into the curtain (2 sentences): the light closes round them, cold, and then they are standing at the top of the stair, facing down it, with no memory of turning round.
- C11_FROM_BELOW: Narrator, the first time the party walks up the stair from the royal tomb into the curtain (1 sentence): the light lets them pass without a flicker; it only cares who goes down.
- C11_LOCKED: Narrator, when a party with a dark gift tries the curtain again after being turned back (1 short sentence, under 15 words).
