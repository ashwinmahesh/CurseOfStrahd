== balcony_location_summary
A private timber gallery high above the chapel nave, where two tall thrones stand behind a low rail so the lord of the house might hear his prayers in solitude.
== balcony_location_text
Dark timber boards run out to a low rail looking down into the dim well of the chapel nave. Two high thrones stand side by side in the dust, facing the cold stone below.

== faith_codex
Title: The Watching Stone Knights
Note: The eyes of the carved knights are not enchanted, only hollowed out with a curved chisel so the shadows shift as you pass. It is an old mason's trick, meant to keep the living honest at very little expense.
