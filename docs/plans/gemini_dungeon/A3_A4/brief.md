You are drafting lines for a single-player gothic horror RPG based on Curse of Strahd (Barovia, Strahd von Zarovich). Write in our own words; never quote the published book. Reply with plain text only: one line per slot, in the form `SLOT_ID: text`, in the order given, nothing else. Finish without sending messages to anyone else.

## The voices

**The Narrator**: an old gothic storyteller who speaks for the world. Second person, present tense ("You step inside."). One or two sentences; the second is often the turn (irony, dread, a dry joke, or here, grief). Ominous, dryly funny, never cruel. Concrete senses (a smell, a sound, a temperature) over adjectives. No game terms (no "HP", "roll", "check", "level", "trap", "encounter"). Never states things the characters could not perceive. No dashes of any kind; use full stops, commas and semicolons. When something else speaks through the Narrator (the wind in a gargoyle), the Narrator describes it and quotes the words in double quotes.

**Dead Knight** (speaker id "Dead Knight"): a revenant knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon, dead four hundred years and still at his post. Very few words, flat, military, a voice like a door on a cold morning. Repeats his orders. Never threatens. Examples already in the game: "Speak to the commander." "All present. None dismissed." "He lives. We watch."

## Background you can use

Argynvostholt is a burned mansion on a ridge, once the house of Argynvost, a silver dragon who wore a man's shape and founded the Order of the Silver Dragon, knights who protected the valley. Strahd's armies came over the mountains, besieged the house and killed the dragon in the courtyard; his skull was carried to Castle Ravenloft as a trophy. The knights all died too, and came back as revenants under their commander, Vladimir Horngaard, whose hatred keeps them from rest; their ghosts (phantom warriors) still fight the last battle over and over. At the top of the beacon tower stands a cold iron cradle where the dragon's beacon once burned to tell the valley it was watched. The Order's oath, carved on a stone downstairs: "I will keep the watch while others sleep. I will shield the sleeper and the stranger alike. I will lift up the fallen and ask no price. I will take nothing that is not given. And when the light goes out, I will be the light."

Players reach the third floor up a stair from the upper floor's landing (where the knights' dormitory, the dragon's study and Godfrey's room are).

## The third floor (new rooms)

- **The stair head and the trapped hallway**: the stair arrives in a cold hallway between two doors, the knights' quarters on either side, locked. Unless the party knows the Order's oath, as they walk it stone grinds across both ends of the hall and grey phantom knights step out through the quarters' doors and up through the floor behind them, and attack. If the party knows the oath, the phantoms lean out of the doors, look at them, and draw back.
- **The knights' quarters** (two rooms): rows of narrow cots, footlockers, armour on stands, nothing slept in for centuries.
- **The upper passage**: a long corridor; at its east end the ceiling has fallen in and the sky shows through a hole in the roof, rubble and rain underfoot.
- **The dragon's audience hall**: a vast hall with tall windows, built so a dragon could hold court. A huge throne stands at the far end, turned to face the windows and away from the door, as if whoever sits there would rather look at the castle on its crag across the valley than at anyone who comes in. Nobody is on it.
- **The round table**: a room with a great round table, the Order's oath carved round its rim. Four dead knights sit at it in their armour, motionless, waiting for orders that never come.
- **A closet** off the round table room: old surcoats with the silver dragon on them, folded and moth-eaten.
- **The dragon's vault**, behind a hidden door in the audience hall's wall: stone walls scored by great claws, and empty; looted long ago. One small thing the looters missed.
- **The upstairs gallery**: three tall stained-glass windows, cracked but whole: the silver dragon over the valley; knights kneeling to take the oath; the beacon burning on its tower.
- **Argynvost's bedroom**: plain, almost bare: empty stone shelves where he kept the curiosities he loved, all taken; a bed far too big for a man.

## The roof (new)

The roof of the house, open to the sky: a walk round the slates between low parapets, with turrets at the corners, the beacon tower rising at one end with a second door into it, a hole in the roof where it fell in, and a sheer drop at the edges. A great stone dragon gargoyle crouches on the parapet; the wind through its open jaws sounds almost like words. An ancient ballista still stands, its wood grey and its cords rotted. Another ballista lies smashed; before it broke it was turned round to aim at the beacon tower, and the stone of the tower where it pointed is gouged by enormous claws (the knights once turned their own weapon on something that landed there).
