== tudorel_square
Narrator: A young footman in grey-and-black wool stands by the wet cobbles, clutching a small linen bundle to his chest.
Narrator: The cold mist smells of wet ash, and a small silver raven and crown on his collar catches the grey light.
Tudorel [weary]: If you have come for Lady Wachter's household, the doors are barred and her ladyship is gone from the house.
Tudorel: The cook took the copper pans, and the rest of us were told to stand in the square until someone decides what to do with us.
Tudorel [sad]: I would take the livery off, only it is the warmest coat I own and the wind is coming down from the mountain.
Tudorel: You are looking at me as if you know my face, though I am certain I have never poured for you.
