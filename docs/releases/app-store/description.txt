The mists have closed behind you. Somewhere above the valley, Castle Ravenloft waits.

Lead a party of four into Barovia in Curse of Strahd, a single-player tactical role-playing game of dangerous journeys, uneasy alliances and turn-based battles. Explore haunted villages, candlelit interiors and forgotten ruins as you uncover the secrets of a land ruled by a vampire.

BUILD YOUR PARTY
Choose from six voiced companions or create a hero of your own. Shape your characters through classes, backgrounds, spells, equipment and level-up choices. You control every party member, in exploration and in combat.

MAKE EVERY TURN COUNT
Position your party, weigh the odds and combine attacks, spells and reactions on a tactical grid. Preview your choices before committing, watch for allies caught in area effects, and inspect the rolls and bonuses behind the outcome.

FOLLOW THE CARDS
Travel from the mist-shrouded roads to Castle Ravenloft through a campaign of conversations, discoveries and consequential decisions. A Tarokka reading changes the path of each adventure, with multiple endings to discover.

EXPLORE AT YOUR OWN PACE
Speak with the people of Barovia, investigate strange objects, manage your equipment and follow your journal across the valley. Storybook artwork, expressive character portraits and voiced dialogue bring its inhabitants to life.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Use touch controls designed for iPhone and iPad, or play with a compatible controller. Keep the world in view with a quiet exploration interface and combat actions that open when you need them. Save locally and import or export adventures through Files. Try party builds and battles in Skirmish mode.
