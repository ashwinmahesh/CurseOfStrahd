# The sixteen calls (and the opener), as written and as edited

Drafted by Gemini through the ai-bridge (the packaged service, `agy` on Ashwin's Google subscription; the bridge did not report the model), 2026-10-18, from `calls_brief.md`; the bridge's record with the raw reply in `message.result` is `calls_reply_raw.json`. Edited for the voice, canon and the game's dialogue format. The lines are in narrative/amber_temple/vestiges.dialogue (`~ <name>_call`) and are unvoiced.

## opener

Draft: The hall remains dead silent, yet a voice unfolds directly behind your eyes like a memory that is not yours.

Final: The air stays dead silent, yet a voice unfolds directly behind your eyes, like a memory that is not yours.

Why: "The hall remains dead silent" fitted only the great hall; the voices reach the party in the ring and the vaults too, so "The air stays dead silent"; a comma after "eyes".

## taar_haak

Draft: BLOOD ON YOUR BOOTS, WE SMELL IT, WE CALL DIBS ON THE TALL ONE! No, the scarred one hits harder, step up and show us your teeth!

Final: BLOOD ON YOUR BOOTS, WE SMELL IT! WE TAKE THE TALL ONE. No, the broad one hits harder. Step up, step up, show us your teeth!

Why: "WE CALL DIBS" is modern slang. Five heads that claim the tall one and argue about the broad one are the same joke in the voice of the book; "the scarred one" assumed a party member who may not exist.

## yog

Draft: Stone cracks and flesh tears, yet you still walk. Stand here with me... and learn how mountains bear the weight.

Final: Stone cracks. Flesh tears. Still you... walk. Come. Stand. Learn how a mountain... holds.

Why: Yog speaks in monosyllables with long pauses, and its other lines hold that; the draft was fluent. Cut to short words and pauses, same idea (a mountain bearing weight).

## norganas

Draft: You carry a memory that aches to hold, right behind your eyes... Step closer, and we can rub it clean before you even recall... what was it?

Final: You carry a memory that aches, right behind your eyes... Come closer, and I will rub it clean before you even recall... what was it?

Why: The draft slipped into "we" ("we can rub it clean"); Norganas is one voice that forgets. "I will rub it clean", "aches to hold" cut to "aches".

## seriach

Draft: Easy, boys, easy, let the two-legged meat catch its breath first. You walk like hunters, yet you smell like the stag at the end of the mile.

Final: Easy, boys, easy, let the two-legged meat catch its breath first. You walk like hunters, yet you smell like the stag at the end of the run.

Why: "the stag at the end of the mile" read oddly; a hunt ends at the end of the run.

## tenebrous

Draft: Shh, put out that dreadful torch before it blinds us both. Your eyes are aching from the glare, so come rest them in the dark.

Why: "torch": the party may carry a lantern or a spell; "light" covers all of them.
