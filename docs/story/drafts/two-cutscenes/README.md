# Two cutscenes: Gemini drafts (owner rule, 2026-10-18)

The Tome of Strahd's twelve captions (narrative/narrator/treasures.dialogue, keys `read:tome_of_strahd` and
`scene:tome_*`) were drafted by Gemini through the ai-bridge (the packaged service, `agy` on the owner's Google
subscription; the bridge did not report the model it used), then edited for canon and length.

- `tome_brief.md`: the brief that was sent.
- `tome_reply_raw.json`: the bridge's record of the send, with Gemini's raw reply in `message.result`.
- `tome_captions_final.md`: the captions as edited, and every edit made to the draft and why.

The mirror in Escher's suite needed no new lines: the Narrator line that was already there reads under the picture.
All twelve Tome lines are unvoiced (no `make voice` from a lane).
