# Tome of Strahd: captions after review (Gemini draft edited by Claude)

Raw Gemini reply: tome_reply_raw.json (brief: tome_brief.md). Edits made and why:
- 1.1/1.2: "raised a castle" became "made the castle that bears his mother's name his seat" (the game never says he built it); added that he does not turn round, to match the still.
- 2.1: "arrived at the castle" removed (Sergei is his brother, not a guest); Tatyana's flowers moved to the wedding caption.
- 2.3: dropped "he bargained to stop growing older than her": that line is the Amber Temple vault's reveal when the Tome and the copper ledger are read together (narrative/amber_temple/vault.dialogue ~ pact_tome), so the Tome must not say it first. Kept the "rival I could not meet on a field" quote, and the garbled "garden colour" is gone.
- 3.2/3.3: the price is left unsaid until the wedding, and "by his own hand" is no longer used twice.
- 4.2: Gemini had the arrows both strike and break on him; the codex says only that the guards shot him and he did not die.
- 4.3: quote rewritten so the last page reads as him telling himself.
