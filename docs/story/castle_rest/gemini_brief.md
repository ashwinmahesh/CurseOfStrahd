You are drafting Narrator lines for "Curse of Strahd", a single-player CRPG built on the D&D 5e Curse of Strahd campaign. Reply with ONLY the lines in the formats below (no preamble, no commentary, no code fence).

## Who the Narrator is
An old gothic storyteller who has watched a great many adventurers walk into Barovia and very few walk out. Not the party's enemy and not its friend. Fond of the party the way a gravedigger is fond of the weather: with interest, without illusions. Never gloats over a failure, never cheers a success. Notices.

## What is new
Until now the party could never sleep inside Castle Ravenloft, because every floor still held enemies somewhere. From now on they may camp for the night on a floor once its enemies are beaten. Strahd's castle is never quiet, though: in the small hours bats, the dead or his vampire spawn may come for the camp. So the castle needs night lines for the places where the party can now sleep, a few more dreams, and three rewritten lines for what comes for the camp.

## Rules for every line
1. Second person, present tense. When a line is about one character use {name}.
2. One or two sentences, at most 40 words (a night line or dream) or 25 words (a visitor line); most are 20 to 30.
3. Ominous, dryly funny, never cruel. Laugh at the dark and the situation, never at the player.
4. Concrete senses over adjectives (a sound, a weight, a temperature).
5. No modern slang, no winks at the player, no game terms (no "HP", "roll", "level", "action", "spell slot", "Long Rest", "camp" as a game thing, "item", "encounter").
6. Plain punctuation: commas, colons, full stops. No em-dashes, no semicolons.
7. Do not invent named characters, and do not contradict the places' descriptions.

## 2. Dreams (more of the castle's, added to the same entry)
After a night's sleep the Narrator may add one dream. Each begins "You dream of" and ends on an unsettling or wry turn. The castle's existing two, for tone, which you must not repeat:

- You dream of a ballroom where you are the only guest who has to breathe. The host bows. The music never stops.
- You dream of a long table laid for one, and a chair pulled out for you, and a voice saying how glad he is that you finally came.

Dreams of other places, for variety:

- You dream of a bridge over nothing, and halfway across a shadow passing over you with a sound like a sail filling.
- You dream of a cave full of sleeping wolves, and one of them is wearing your face.

## 3. What comes for the camp in the night (rewrites)
One line each, said by the Narrator as the watch sees them come: the same meaning as the current line, but it must read right wherever the party sleeps in the castle, indoors under a vault or out on a roof. So: no sky, no fire, no open ground. Current lines:

- castle_dead (bats first, then ghouls, skeletons and a rat): "Bats first, a cloud of them, and then the castle's dead and the things that eat them, crawling toward the warmth."
- castle_spawn (three vampire spawn behind two swarms of bats): "Pale figures walk out of the dark behind the bats, smiling, in no hurry at all."
