# Gemini drafts for the catacombs' crypts (Dungeon Expansion Plan C1)

Every new line in castle_ravenloft_catacombs (Narrator), castle_ravenloft/catacombs_crypts, catacombs_skulls and
catacombs_sasha was drafted by Gemini through Ashwin's ai-bridge (his Google subscription, `agy`, no API key; the packaged
service at 127.0.0.1:8765, project `ai-bridge`), on 2026-10-18. Claude trimmed and canon-checked the drafts.

- `briefN.txt` is what was sent (the shared preamble plus that batch's slots); `replyN.json` is the bridge's message record
  (conversation, state, result) and `replyN.txt` the raw reply text, unedited.
- Brief 1: the seven in-place crypts. Brief 2: the Lesser Vaults. Brief 3: the two skulls, Sasha and Klutz (menus and
  speech). Brief 4: Saint Markovia's bone, the Baron's mold, Klutz's sword, Sasha's bier. Brief 5: one option line.
- The lines in the game are the drafts with these edits (slot id -> what changed); every slot not listed is exactly
  Gemini's text:

  - `TROISKY_HELM`: dropped the chin-strap wire (the trap is a sunken step, not a wire); added the step
  - `BASCAL_BELL`: added the cord (the trap is a cord strung to the bell)
  - `KLUTZ_SWORD_LOOK`: the draft had the sword hilt-down through the spine; now in at the front, out at the back
  - `MARKOVIA_DARTS`: 'needles'/'built by the lord of the castle' -> darts / laid by the lord's masons
  - `MARKOVIA_BONE`: semicolon -> 'so take it with clean hands'
  - `STAHBAL_SHAFT`: no count of lids (the fight decides how many rise)
  - `STAHBAL_WIGHTS`: 'fifteen lids' and 'old Campaign iron' -> the lids / old iron; 'form ranks' kept
  - `INTREE_EPITAPH`: 'took his body for the vault' -> put his body in the wall
  - `FINDERWAY_ALCOVES`: semicolon split into two sentences
  - `JARNWALD_GHOULS`: rewritten around the room, not a 'shaft' with a 'last guest'
  - `INTREE_GREET`: 'two doors down' -> in the next crypt (the two skull crypts are neighbours)
  - `MARKOVIA_WORDS`: dropped 'carry what remains of me into the upper rooms, and strike the master down' (unchecked canon); kept the blessing
  - `MARKOVIA_OPT_LIFT`: shortened
  - `MARKOVIA_OPT_LEAVE`: shortened
  - `STEFAN_OPT_ALCOVES`: as drafted

All of it stays unvoiced (no `make voice` from a lane): the hand-off names the voiced lines (everything but the
option lines, which are never voiced) for the voice thread.
