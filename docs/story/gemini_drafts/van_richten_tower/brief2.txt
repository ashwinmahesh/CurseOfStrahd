SETTING OF THIS TASK: Van Richten's Tower, a round tower of black stone on a small island in Lake Baratok, a cold tarn under Mount Baratok. A long-dead archmage named Khazan built it. TONE: gothic horror with dry, understated, character-driven humour. Funny comes from specifics and deadpan, not from jokes about the horror. Where a scene is sad, let it be sad. THE VISTANI'S HEAD. Inside the chest, bedded in salt and dried herbs, is the severed head of a Vistani man. Van Richten kept it so he could use the spell Speak with Dead on it to learn about the Vistani. It is not gruesome; it is old, dry and patient. When the spell takes, the eyes open and the dry mouth moves; the head answers in the first person, tired and dry, with a Vistani's pride but no caricature. It knows only what it knew alive.
