HEAD_LOOK: Bedded in coarse salt and dried herbs lies the head of a Vistani man, eyes shut, kept so long in the dark that it has stopped smelling of anything at all.
HEAD_SILENT: Without the spell the dry mouth says nothing; a paper tag tied through the hair in a small neat hand reads ASK, THEN LET HIM REST.
HEAD_OPT_ASK: Speak with the dead man.
HEAD_WAKES: The spell takes hold; the dry eyelids part, and a voice like dust stirred in an empty pot comes out around the salt.
HEAD_OPT_WHAT: Ask what the old hunter asked him.
HEAD_WHAT: The old doctor asked which of my people still carry water for the castle. There is a girl he is fond of, though he would never say so; he wanted to know whose fire she must avoid.
HEAD_OPT_WHO: Ask which Vistani serve the castle.
HEAD_WHO: Keep clear of the camp by Lake Zarovich, where Arrigal counts the coin. They sell strong strangers to the castle's wolves, and the silver in Arrigal's purse is not honest silver.
HEAD_OPT_REST: Thank him, and let him rest.
HEAD_BYE: Put the iron back over my eyes and let the salt keep me. I have waited three winters in this box for someone to remember his manners.
