You are drafting Narrator lines for "Curse of Strahd", a single-player CRPG built on the D&D 5e Curse of Strahd campaign. Reply with ONLY the lines in the file format below (no preamble, no commentary, no code fence).

## Who the Narrator is
An old gothic storyteller who has watched a great many adventurers walk into Barovia and very few walk out. Not the party's enemy and not its friend. Fond of the party the way a gravedigger is fond of the weather: with interest, without illusions. Never gloats over a failure, never cheers a success. Notices.

## What a dream line is
After a night's sleep the Narrator may add one dream. Each place's dreams are one or two sentences that begin "You dream of" and end on an unsettling or wry turn. Here are the dreams of other places, for tone and length (do not reuse their images):

- You dream of a bridge over nothing, and halfway across a shadow passing over you with a sound like a sail filling.
- You dream of a tower in a lake with no door, and an old man inside writing names in a book: everyone he couldn't save.
- You dream of a cave full of sleeping wolves, and one of them is wearing your face.
- You dream of a long table laid for one, and a chair pulled out for you, and a voice saying how glad he is that you finally came.

## Rules
1. Second person, present tense. Begin "You dream of". When a line is about one character use {name}.
2. One or two sentences, at most 40 words each; most are 20 to 30.
3. Ominous, dryly funny, never cruel. Laugh at the dark and the situation, never at the player.
4. Concrete senses over adjectives (a sound, a weight, a temperature).
5. No modern slang, no winks at the player, no game terms (no "HP", "roll", "level", "action", "spell slot", "attunement", "Long Rest", "item").
6. Plain punctuation: commas, colons, full stops. No em-dashes.

## The place
Daern's Instant Fortress: a thumb-sized adamantine statuette that the party carries. Set on open ground and spoken to, it swells into a square tower twenty feet on a side and thirty high, near-black blue-grey metal, arrow slits in every wall, a battlement on top, one heavy door that opens only at its owner's word. Two bare floors inside joined by a stair, a trapdoor to the roof. Nothing hostile can walk in, which is the point: the party sleeps there, on bedrolls, by a lantern. It is not a ruin: it is brand new every time it is raised, and nobody has ever lived in it before. It came to the party as a gift from a winemaker in the valley of Barovia, a land of mist, wolves and a vampire lord whom nobody mentions twice. The fortress is a safe, cold, strange place to sleep, and the dream should lean on that: the sealed door, the metal that remembers nothing, a thing once small and now big, being carried in a pocket, the mist pressing at the slits finding nothing to do.

## Format (exactly)
One entry, with two variants, in this exact form:

~ dream:daerns_fortress
| (first dream)
| (second dream)
