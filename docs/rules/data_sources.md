## Magic items (DMG2024) entered from memory

The 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide isn't in the free Basic Rules, so every magic item (data/magic_items, `book:
DMG2024`) was entered from knowledge of the book (owner decision, personal use only): names, rarities,
attunement, charges, DCs and effects all need a check against the owner's copy. The catalogue was rebuilt from
memory as: every item of the 2014 DMG the 2024 book kept, the common items it folded in (from Xanathar's), the
class implements (Amulet of the Devout, Arcane Grimoire, Bloodwell Vial, Moon Sickle, Rhythm-Maker's Drum,
Dragonhide Belt, Wraps of Unarmed Power), Adamantine Weapon, and its artifacts. Left out as doubtful for the
2024 book: the Sword of Answering, the All-Purpose Tool (Artificer only) and the Tasha's tattoos and shards.
Artifacts' random properties use a condensed table (rules/equipment/magic_items.gd). The stat blocks for
creatures the items summon (data/monsters) were since checked against SRD 5.2.1 (see Monster editions).
Every item's `text`, `summary` and power text is written in our own words; only the game terms are the book's.

## Death House (Curse of Strahd appendix B) from memory

Death House's rooms, decor, encounters and treasure (data/locations/death_house_*.json,
docs/regions/death_house_book_check.md) follow the book from memory, cross-checked against DM guides and play
reports. Unsure, to check against the owner's copy:
- Ground floor: the den's locked cabinet (heavy and light crossbow and 20 bolts; one memory adds a hand
  crossbow and 20 bolts for each); whether the main hall's coat of arms and the cloakroom's black cloaks are the
  book's; the kitchen oven's shape (domed, from a guide).
- Second floor: the iron key in the library desk opening the attic padlock; the skeleton in leather armor with
  three darts in the secret room; the three scrolls (bless, protection from poison, spiritual weapon).
- Third floor and attic: that the attic stair has two secret doors (off the balcony, and behind the
  nursemaid's mirror) and no DCs we know of (we use 12 and 10); that the secret stair to the dungeon starts in
  the attic storage room's north-east corner; the nursemaid's specter in her suite and her remains in the
  attic trunk; the master suite's portrait of Gustav and Elisabeth over the hearth.
- Dungeon: the mimic as the door into the cult leaders' den (one guide puts it at the shrine's south door);
  four ghouls in the passage; five shadows in the shrine set off by the orb or statue; the swarm of insects
  in Elisabeth's crypt (23D); the spiked pit's DCs and damage (we keep 2d6 at DC 15); where the hidden
  trapdoor (32) comes out (we use the village, open only after the sacrifice); the reliquary at the foot of the
  lower stair; the alcove chests' contents (gems and trinkets become their gp value); the footlocker's
  cloak of protection and spellbook (two sources agree).
- Not modelled: a silvered shortsword (ours is plain), the mess kit, the yellow spellbook's spell list.
